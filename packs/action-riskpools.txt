CONTENT

Shared risk pools
Public–private insurance and regional risk pools keep cover available and spread losses beyond any one budget.
Who could act: Banks, insurers and financial regulators, National governments, Development banks, donors and climate funds.
Over what time: years.
How progress could be measured: Share of heat losses covered by pooled risk.

EXAMPLE
Regional risk pools such as the African Risk Capacity and the Caribbean’s CCRIF share disaster risk across countries. [https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/psd/sovereign-catastrophe-risk-pools-15-years-and-still-more-come]

VICIOUS CYCLES IT COULD HELP INTERRUPT
- The protection-gap loop: As insurers retreat, public budgets absorb the losses, protection is cut, and the risk drives insurers further away.
