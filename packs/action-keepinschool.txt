HOW TO USE THIS PACK

CONTENT

Keep children in school
Cash transfers, school meals and heat-ready classrooms and timetables keep children learning through hot seasons.
Who could act: Schools and education authorities, National governments.
Over what time: season.
How progress could be measured: Attendance and drop-outs during the heat season.

EXAMPLE
When heat closes schools, children lose more than lessons: consultations for Tamil Nadu’s heat strategy found that long breaks cost children in schools serving marginalised groups their midday meals. India’s school meal scheme can keep serving meals through the summer holidays in areas declared drought-hit, as Odisha did in 27 districts in a year when a forecast heatwave also extended school closures. In the Philippines, heat helped drive the return to a June school start, keeping classes out of the hottest months, April and May. [https://spc.tn.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/Heat_Mitigation_Strategy.pdf] [https://www.thequint.com/news/hot-news/met-dept-predicts-severe-heatwave-odisha-extends-school-holidays] [https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1940232/deped-all-set-for-june-school-opening-in-25]

VICIOUS CYCLES IT COULD HELP INTERRUPT
- The lost-schooling loop: Children pulled out of school earn less as adults, and poorer families pull more children out of school.
