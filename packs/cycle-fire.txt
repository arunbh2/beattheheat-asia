CONTENT

The wildfire loop
Heat dries forests into fuel. Fires release carbon and strip away the trees that cool the land.
Evidence for the cycle as a whole: established.

THE LINKS IN THE CYCLE
- heat
- nature
- Wildfires: Hot, dry vegetation ignites more easily and burns longer, sending smoke far beyond the fire itself. [https://www.unep.org/resources/report/spreading-wildfire-rising-threat-extraordinary-landscape-fires]

STUDIES BEHIND IT
- World Bank, 2016: The Cost of Fire: An Economic Analysis of Indonesia’s 2015 Fire Crisis. Indonesia’s forest and peatland fires of June to October 2015 cost the country an estimated US$16.1 billion. Where: Indonesia. Limit it states: One exceptional year, in an El Niño; costs in other years differ. [https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/776101467990969768/pdf/103668-BRI-Cost-of-Fires-Knowledge-Note-PUBLIC-ADD-NEW-SERIES-Indonesia-Sustainable-Landscapes-Knowledge-Note.pdf]
- UNEP and GRID-Arendal, 2022: Spreading like Wildfire: The Rising Threat of Extraordinary Landscape Fires. Climate change and land-use change are making wildfires worse. Extreme fires are projected to rise by up to 14% by 2030 and 50% by the end of the century. Where: Global. Limit it states: Global projections; not broken down for Asian countries. [https://www.unep.org/resources/report/spreading-wildfire-rising-threat-extraordinary-landscape-fires]

ACTIONS THAT COULD INTERRUPT IT
- Deep emission cuts: Every fraction of a degree avoided weakens every loop that runs through land, ice and forests at once. It also keeps more places within the limits to adaptation, where protecting people still works.
- Fire-smart landscapes: Early detection, community fire crews and land management that reduces fuel keep fires small and forests standing.
