CONTENT

Power that doesn’t need water
Solar and wind use very little water, and dry cooling protects thermal plants when rivers run low or warm.
Who could act: Utilities and energy regulators, National governments.
Over what time: decade.
How progress could be measured: Power capacity added that needs little or no water.

None recorded yet.

VICIOUS CYCLES IT COULD HELP INTERRUPT
- The energy–water loop: Water systems need power and power plants need water. In a heatwave, each failure deepens the other.
