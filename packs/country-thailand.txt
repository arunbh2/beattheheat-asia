## Rules for heat at work

**Legal basis:** Official guidance or data Ministerial Regulation on occupational heat/light/noise B.E.2559(2016), under the Occupational Safety, Health and Environment Act B.E.2554(2011), published17October 2016. Current labour authority provides text and measurement notifications; full later-amendment audit not completed. [Thai heat/light/noise occupational regulation ](https://osh.labour.go.th/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/17102559.pdf) · [Department of Labour Protection and Welfare legal resource page ](https://osh.labour.go.th/ร้อน-แสง-เสียง-2559/) **Limits:**
- **Workload:** light **Value:** 34 **Unit:** °C WBGT
- **Workload:** moderate **Value:** 32 **Unit:** °C WBGT
- **Workload:** heavy **Value:** 30 **Unit:** °C WBGT

**Measurement:** Law defines average WBGT during the hottest two hours of normal work, with workload classes; obtain qualified assessment for the specific task. **Legal controls:** Warning signs, engineering correction and documentation; workload controls where engineering cannot meet standard. **Limit sources:** [Thai heat/light/noise occupational regulation ](https://osh.labour.go.th/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/17102559.pdf) **Caveat:** These are statutory WBGT limits, not a universal ambient-temperature stop-work rule, proof of universal physiological safety or automatic entitlement for all self-employed workers. Paid rest and enforcement would be worth verifying locally. **Research:** Measured in a study Thai observational construction/foundry study found heat strain among some workers even under compliant conditions; do not interpret compliance as a guarantee. [Occupational heat standard and worker strain study ](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33732537/)

## Money for protection

- **BMA public budgets and grant/concessional pathways** Official guidance or data UNDP/BMA strategy analyses budget and legally possible capital routes. Most heat projects are public goods suited to public budgets; money to build is generally available, but running costs are not always covered, and there is no pipeline of investment-ready adaptation projects yet. The city may issue bonds with prior Cabinet approval but has not yet done so, and its AA+ credit rating was discontinued in 2022. District offices have no independent fiscal authority; the strategy proposes dedicated district allocations and criteria that steer money towards more vulnerable districts, and suggests offering cooling facilities to sponsors as one citywide programme, with agreements that set out operation and maintenance. It does not establish that every route has been used for heat or that Common Shade is eligible. [Bangkok heat-financing strategy - full report ](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-10/en-undp-bma_financial_strategy_on_urban_heat.pdf) **Access:** Agency budget/project preparation and relevant approvals; no open small-collaborative application verified.
- **Environmental Fund adaptation route** Official guidance or data Strategy describes a local-government adaptation proposal route under Environmental Fund guidance: up to THB 5 million per project for up to three years, with at least 70% of the budget spent on activities. About 74.8% of the fund's disbursements have gone to local governments. Original current call and project eligibility need confirmation. [Bangkok heat-financing strategy - full report ](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-10/en-undp-bma_financial_strategy_on_urban_heat.pdf) **Access:** Explore jointly with eligible public counterpart; not an automatic NGO entitlement.
- **Historical RAC NAMA fund** Reported, not evaluated GIZ factsheet documents EGAT-managed mechanisms for producers, users and training/testing. Historical phase closed; not presented as a current call. [RAC NAMA Thailand project history ](https://www.green-cooling-initiative.org/about-us/our-projects/rac-nama-thailand) · [RAC NAMA fund mechanism factsheet ](https://www.giz.de/de/downloads/RAC%20NAMA%20fund_FINAL.pdf) **Access:** Historical learning case; current access not verified.
- **EGAT Cooling Innovation Fund** Reported, not evaluated 2022 primary successor account describes fund and technical support focus; current eligibility and allocations not verified. [Cooling Innovation Fund successor account ](https://mitigation-action.org/news/cooling-innovation-fund-delegation-shares-funds-progress-with-the-nama-facility-as-part-of-study-trip/) **Access:** Confirm with EGAT/GIZ; no current open route assumed.
- **Bangkok health/worker research collaborations** Reported, not evaluated BMA/AIT/Vital Strategies and HomeNet/WIEGO work offer research/delivery relationships, not verified open grant mechanisms. [AIT/Vital Strategies/BMA heat-health-plan development ](https://ait.ac.th/2026/09/ait-vital-strategies-and-bma-advance-bangkok-heat-health-action-plan/) · [HomeNet Thailand/WIEGO Bangkok heat study ](https://www.wiego.org/research-library-publications/climate-change-and-heat-stress-impacts-bangkok-thailand/) **Access:** Proposed contribution to existing work, subject to invitation and fit.
