CONTENT

Social protection floors
Basic income security that keeps households above the line between one hot season and the next.
Who could act: National governments, Development banks, donors and climate funds.
Over what time: years.
How progress could be measured: Households reached by heat-season support.

EXAMPLE
In Rajshahi, Bangladesh, a heatwave study found the government's Old Age Allowance suited to early cash before heat peaks, given its digitised list of recipients and nationwide reach; a simulation then tested the idea. [https://www.anticipation-hub.org/Documents/Reports/0_RCCC_SRSP_simulation_Rajshahi_Advocacy_brief_V3.pdf]

VICIOUS CYCLES IT COULD HELP INTERRUPT
- The poverty–heat trap: People who can’t afford to stop working fall ill. Illness costs income, and lost income makes stopping even less affordable.
