## Heat context

**Climate:** Official guidance or data Terai lowland heat includes seasonally drier pre-monsoon exposure and humid monsoon conditions. Climate suitability would be worth assessing at the actual place, season and task. [DHM post-monsoon precipitation and temperature summary, 2024 ](https://www.dhm.gov.np/uploads/dhm/climateService/ON_2024-Preliminary_precipitation_and_temperature_summary.pdf) · [Dhangadhi Heat Action Plan ](https://www.climatecentre.org/wp-content/uploads/Dhangadhi-Heat-Action-Plan-2025.pdf) · [RAIN heat early-action implementation account ](https://nepal.peopleinneed.net/en/from-forecast-to-early-action-how-the-rain-programme-acted-on-rising-heat-13033gp) **Trends:** Reported, not evaluated National synthesis describes intensifying heat concern in the Terai and increasing exposure elsewhere. DHM2024 post-monsoon report documents above-normal southern minimum temperatures; this seasonal observation is not a long-term trend estimate. [Living with Heat in Nepal - original publisher overview ](https://www.ceew.in/publications/nepal-heat-health-action) · [DHM post-monsoon precipitation and temperature summary, 2024 ](https://www.dhm.gov.np/uploads/dhm/climateService/ON_2024-Preliminary_precipitation_and_temperature_summary.pdf) **Night heat:** Official guidance or data Dhangadhi plan describes reduced night-time recovery in monsoon months. Comparable occupied-bedroom night records across all eight cities not verified. [Dhangadhi Heat Action Plan ](https://www.climatecentre.org/wp-content/uploads/Dhangadhi-Heat-Action-Plan-2025.pdf) **Measurement caution:** Keep dry-bulb air temperature, Heat Index, WBGT, land-surface and occupied-room temperature separate. No national exposure rank assigned.

## Legal basis

Official guidance or data DRRM Act 2017 expressly includes heat wave under natural disaster and establishes multilevel disaster arrangements and funds. A published HAP does not by itself establish a new offence, entitlement or budget release. [Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act 2017 ](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/NEP228597.pdf)

## Warnings

**Documented:** Official guidance or data DHM provides heat monitoring/bulletins; municipalities and humanitarian partners connect forecasts with local action. RIMES/DHM held a national heat impact-based forecasting demonstration workshop inJuly 2026. [DHM heat and cold wave monitoring archive ](https://dhm.gov.np/climate-services/heat-and-cold-wave-monitoring) · [DHM/RIMES national heat impact-based forecasting demonstration workshop ](https://www.rimes.int/rimes-supports-nepals-national-demonstration-of-ibf-for-heat-extremes/) **Channels:** Reported, not evaluated RAIN reports FCHV visits, radio and public announcements in Bajika/Maithili; municipal/Red Cross arrangements include non-smartphone communication. [RAIN heat early-action implementation account ](https://nepal.peopleinneed.net/en/from-forecast-to-early-action-how-the-rain-programme-acted-on-rising-heat-13033gp) · [NRCS/municipal anticipatory action in Nepalgunj ](https://redcross.eu/projects/how-the-nepal-red-cross-society-and-communities-in-nepalgunj-are-getting-ahead-of-extreme-heat) **Thresholds:** Official guidance or data Dhangadhi HAP proposes daily maximum Heat Index40.1°C for two consecutive days or41.6°C for one or more days, derived from 1979–2023 ERA5 weather analysis. Mortality gaps/low variance prevented local epidemiological modelling. Confirm version/metric/forecast product and authorisation before operation. [Dhangadhi Heat Action Plan ](https://www.climatecentre.org/wp-content/uploads/Dhangadhi-Heat-Action-Plan-2025.pdf) **Cash trigger:** No universal nationwide heat-triggered social-protection or insurance payout entitlement verified. **Limits:** National percentile bulletin, local Heat Index proposal and a funding protocol are distinct triggers. No live alert or universally safe temperature supplied.

## Rules for heat at work

**Law:** Official guidance or data Labour Act 2017 sections68–69 require workplace safety/health policy and employer protections; sections75–77 address reporting and immediate danger procedures. Primary English translation reviewed, not a current legal opinion. [Labour Act 2017 English translation ](https://image.mfa.go.th/mfa/0/5bdN9SBnCZ/File_PDF/The-Labor-Act-2017-2074.pdf) **Heat directions:** Official guidance or data NDRRMA2024 action plan directs factories/workshops to provide protective conditions including ventilation, water and rest. Health-insurance wording is a planning direction, not evidence of parametric payout coverage. [NDRRMA national heatwave action plan 2024 ](https://dpnet.org.np/uploads/files/heatwave_eng_webpublish%202026-01-20%2005-39-23.pdf) **Heat specific standard:** Official guidance or data Department of Labour and Occupational Safety officially lists the workplace maximum heat-level standard BS2082. Existence verified; full technical schedule, measurement conditions and current legal implementation details not independently appraised. [Workplace maximum heat-level standard BS2082 - official publication record ](https://dolos.gov.np/content/25/maximum-heat-levels--standards--for-workplaces--2082/) **Numeric WBGT limit verification:** Numerical schedule not verified from primary text; standard existence is verified. Do not infer absence of heat rules or import foreign-country thresholds. **Paid rest and coverage:** Heat-specific paid-rest entitlement, self-employed coverage, enforcement and current technical schedules require verification. **Worker evidence:** Measured in a study Original cross-sectional study documents outdoor-worker heat awareness, practices and perceived risks. It is not a measured productivity-loss or clinical intervention study. [Outdoor workers heat knowledge, attitudes and practices study ](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0329557)

## Money for protection

- **Public disaster-management funds and municipal sector budgets** [Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act 2017 ](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/NEP228597.pdf) **Access:** Via authorised public counterpart and applicable fund/budget rules; no open NGO entitlement. **Limits:** Act sections22–23 establish funds. ## Gaps in what we found

## Known, inferred, uncertain
