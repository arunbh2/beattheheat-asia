CONTENT

Crop insurance and fair credit
Crop insurance that pays out after a heat-hit season, affordable credit and heat-tolerant seed stop one bad harvest becoming a debt trap.
Who could act: Banks, insurers and financial regulators, Farmers’ organisations and agriculture agencies, Development banks, donors and climate funds.
Over what time: years.
How progress could be measured: Farmers covered by crop insurance or fair credit.

EXAMPLE
None recorded yet.

VICIOUS CYCLES IT COULD HELP INTERRUPT
- The farm-debt loop: A failed harvest pushes farmers into debt. Debt leaves less for seed, water and fertiliser, so the next harvest fails more easily.
