CONTENT

Deep emission cuts
Every fraction of a degree avoided weakens every loop that runs through land, ice and forests at once. It also keeps more places within the limits to adaptation, where protecting people still works.
Who could act: National governments, International bodies, Development banks, donors and climate funds.
Over what time: decade.
How progress could be measured: Emissions cut against the national target.

EXAMPLE
Across 2.7 million firms in 23 advanced economies, places used to heat coped better with days of 30–35 °C, but none had adapted to days above 40 °C. Each degree avoided keeps more days below that line. [https://doi.org/10.1787/19d94638-en]

VICIOUS CYCLES IT COULD HELP INTERRUPT
- The dry-soil loop: Heat dries the soil. Dry soil can’t cool itself, so the land heats up faster.
- The melting-ice loop: Heat melts ice. Darker ground and water absorb more sunlight, and the melting speeds up.
- The wildfire loop: Heat dries forests into fuel. Fires release carbon and strip away the trees that cool the land.
