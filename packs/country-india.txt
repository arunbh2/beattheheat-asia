# India

Heat, the plans and rules that shape protection, the organisations at work, and where a shared effort could help. Researched in October 2026.

**Watch: India and heat** · 0:47 · narrated, captions in the picture

**How to read this.** Facts link to their sources; anything marked proposed or inferred is the researchers’ suggestion, not a commitment by anyone named. Gaps are gaps in what we found, not faults.

## Heat context

**Summary:** India spans hot-dry, composite and hot-humid contexts. District composite risk, daytime weather alerts, humid heat, night heat and indoor exposure are different measures.

## Official plans and authority

**Every plan on file:** the [India heat action plan archive](https://beattheheat.asia/resources/heat-action-plans/india/) lists 146 verified documents (85 standalone heat action plans, 37 heat-health plans inside wider climate-and-health plans, 1 national plan and 23 guidance documents), with a link to each PDF.

**Description:** National guidance, state/district/local HAPs and health-sector plans operate through different institutional/legal routes. Publication alone is not a separately enforceable heat entitlement. **Current rules check:** Central OSH Rules 2026 are notified, not a draft. Check appropriate-government, sector and state implementation.

## Warning system

**Description:** IMD meteorological guidance informs health and local response. Separate forecast, action and financial payout triggers; household receipt not verified.

## Cooling and buildings

**Description:** ICAP workstreams, BEE building/retrofit resources and Telangana cool-roof policy provide entry points; local notification, compliance and affordability require checks.

## Money for protection

- **SDRF/NDRF response** **Access:** Government route; applicable assistance norms and authoritative copy to confirm **Opportunity status:** Guidelines verified; not an open collaborative grant
- **SDMF/NDMF mitigation** **Access:** State-led eligible project preparation/appraisal; no automatic NGO eligibility **Opportunity status:** Framework verified; city heat allocations/releases not verified
- **NPCCHH/NHM district PIP** **Access:** District/state health planning and sanction **Opportunity status:** Guidance verified; specific approvals/releases not verified
- **Municipal climate budgeting** **Access:** Municipal planning and budget cycle **Opportunity status:** Mumbai process described; heat-specific allocation/release not established
- **Heat Index Cover for SEWA-associated workers** **Access:** Historical programme-specific enrolment; current eligibility not verified **Opportunity status:** FY2025 insurer report; no open 2026 opportunity established

## Known, inferred, uncertain

**Known:** Primary plans, guidance and selected studies exist as documented. **Inferred:** Place-based coordination and project preparation could connect currently separate protection functions. **Uncertain:** A comparable national city exposure ranking, current ward delivery, lifecycle costs, complete heat mortality, enforcement and disbursement.

## Paying when heat strikes

The layers of money we found for heat in India, from the systems that reach people to risk shared with insurers: Budgets and reserves, Insurance, pools and bonds, Social protection. Each is shown with how it is released, how it reaches people, and what we could not find.

## Who is working on it

Listing does not imply partnership, endorsement or ranking.

- **[Ministry of Home Affairs - Disaster Management Division ](https://www.mha.gov.in/index.php/policies-guidelines)** Issues national disaster response/mitigation funding guidelines.
- **[National Disaster Management Authority ](https://beta-tnsmart.rimes.int/assets/ActionPlan_District/TN_State_Heat_Action_Plan_2026_09june.pdf)** National heat guidance is referenced in current state and city planning.
- **[India Meteorological Department ](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/pdfs/heatcolduser/FAQ_heat_wave.pdf)** Defines and communicates heat-wave meteorological guidance.
- **[NCDC - Centre for Climate Change and Health / NPCCHH ](https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in/uploads/pdf/1.%20Heat%20wave%20advisory%20for%20State%20Health%20department_2026.pdf)** Provides heat-health preparedness, surveillance and district planning guidance.
- **[Ministry of Labour and Employment ](https://www.labour.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/05/ee246f790cad0b8e99c3828f34fa09a6.pdf)** Notified OSH Central Rules 2026 with sector-specific temperature and heat controls.
- **[MoEFCC Ozone Cell ](https://ozonecell.nic.in/home-page/india-cooling-action-plan/)** Hosts ICAP and its sector implementation recommendations.
- **[Bureau of Energy Efficiency ](https://www.beeindia.gov.in/show_content.php?lang=1&level=1&lid=436&ls_id=359)** Publishes cooling, retrofit and building-code resources.
