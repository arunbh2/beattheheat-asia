CONTENT

Mental health in heat plans
Heat plans that include mental health: support that reaches farm households after crop losses, services that stay open through heatwaves, and warnings that reach people in mental health care.
Who could act: Health authorities, National governments, Community and civil society groups.
Over what time: years.
How progress could be measured: Heat plans that name mental health, with services and support lines that stay open in heatwaves.
