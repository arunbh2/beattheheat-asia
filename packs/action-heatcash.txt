CONTENT

Income protection in heatwaves
Cash that pays out automatically when temperatures cross a danger threshold, so people can afford to stop. Triggers need careful design to limit basis risk: heat that hurts people without triggering a payout.
Who could act: Banks, insurers and financial regulators, National governments, Worker organisations and unions.
Over what time: years.
How progress could be measured: Workers covered, and days from trigger to payout.

EXAMPLE
Parametric heat insurance has been piloted with women informal workers in India. [https://www.icmif.org/news_story/ibisa-launches-a-new-parametric-heat-index-product-in-india-to-protect-women-workers-from-heat-induced-wage-losses/]

VICIOUS CYCLES IT COULD HELP INTERRUPT
- The poverty–heat trap: People who can’t afford to stop working fall ill. Illness costs income, and lost income makes stopping even less affordable.
- The displacement loop: People forced to move often land in heat-trapping housing, fall ill, lose income, fall further behind, and are forced to move again.
