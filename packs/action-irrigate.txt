CONTENT

Smart irrigation and groundwater rules
Drip irrigation, metering, aquifer recharge and fair pumping rules keep wells from running dry.
Who could act: Farmers’ organisations and agriculture agencies, Water authorities.
Over what time: years.
How progress could be measured: Groundwater levels over time.

EXAMPLE
None recorded yet.

WATCH FOR
- Watering fields cools the air but makes it more humid, which can raise the humid heat that farmworkers feel. Saving water and protecting workers are worth checking together. [https://impact.ornl.gov/en/publications/moist-heat-stress-extremes-in-india-enhanced-by-irrigation/]

VICIOUS CYCLES IT COULD HELP INTERRUPT
- The groundwater loop: Dry fields need more water, so farmers pump harder, aquifers fall and the shortage deepens.
