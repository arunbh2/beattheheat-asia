CONTENT

Free care for heat illness
Covering emergency treatment for heat illness stops one bad day from becoming years of debt.
Who could act: Health authorities, National governments.
Over what time: season.
How progress could be measured: Heat illness cases treated free of charge.

EXAMPLE
Singapore's MediFund is a safety net for people who face financial difficulties with their remaining bills after government subsidies, insurance and MediSave; a committee at each approved institution decides the help. [https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/medifund/]

VICIOUS CYCLES IT COULD HELP INTERRUPT
- The medical-debt trap: Treatment costs push families into debt. Debt removes any option to rest, and working through the heat brings more illness.
- The energy-poverty trap: People who can’t afford to cool fall ill. Illness brings debt, and debt puts cooling even further out of reach.
