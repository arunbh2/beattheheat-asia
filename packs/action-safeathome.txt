CONTENT

Violence prevention in heat planning
Helplines, shelters and women’s groups included in heat plans and kept open in heatwaves, income support in hot seasons, and health workers trained to recognise violence on their visits.
Who could act: Community and civil society groups, National governments, Health authorities.
Over what time: years.
How progress could be measured: Heat plans that include helplines, shelters and women’s groups.
