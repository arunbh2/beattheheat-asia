# Use Common Shade with your own AI

Bring the evidence into the assistant or notebook tool you already use. Each pack is one plain-text file of the site's own words and sources, for one country, one vicious cycle or one action, with questions to start from.

- **Load a pack into a notebook tool**Notebook tools that answer only from the files you give them are a good fit. Add one pack, or a few, and ask your questions there.
- **Ask an assistant grounded in it**Paste the pack, or attach it, and ask the assistant to answer only from the pack and to name the source behind each point.
- **Check the answer against the sources**Every pack ends with its sources. Open the ones an answer rests on. Each pack also carries the evidence labels: known, inferred or uncertain.

**An assistant can be wrong, even with a good pack in front of it. The sources in each pack are the authority: check any answer you rely on against them.**

Packs are plain files on this site. Loading one into an AI service sends its text to that service under that service's terms; Common Shade sends nothing about you anywhere. For the whole site at once, there is [one plain-text file](https://beattheheat.asia/llms-full.txt). How AI is used to build Common Shade is set out on [How Common Shade sees heat](https://beattheheat.asia/approach/#ai).
