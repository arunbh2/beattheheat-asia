# A city under strain

You don’t have to work outdoors to be caught by heat. When transformers trip on a hot night, water pumps stop, clinics lose their cooling and no one sleeps. Choose what your city has in place, then run five days of a heatwave.

A teaching model. Its strain levels are illustrative, not measurements, and it is not a forecast for any real city.

## What does your city have in place?

- **Cool roofs and shade on homes**Rooms stay cooler, so fewer air conditioners run flat out.
- **Efficient fans and air conditioners**The same cooling for less power at the peak.
- **Street trees, watered through the heat**Shade by day, and nights that cool a little more.
- **Back-up power at clinics and pumps**Solar and batteries keep clinics cool and water flowing in a cut.
- **Water stored before the heat**Tanks filled ahead, so taps keep running for a while without pumps.
- **Hospitals warned two days ahead**Extra staff, fluids and cool beds before the peak.

## The evidence behind the city

- US$1.4 billion was the World Bank’s early estimate of what northern Viet Nam’s power cuts cost in May and June 2023, after heatwaves pushed up demand and dried hydropower reservoirs.Source: [World Bank estimate, reported by AFP via The Manila Times, 2023: Vietnam’s heat-driven power crisis](https://manilatimes.net/2023/08/12/business/foreign-business/power-crisis-cost-vietnam-14b-wb/1904920/amp)
- In 2016, India lost 14 TWh of electricity because thermal power plants ran short of water.Source: [World Resources Institute, 2018](https://www.wri.org/insights/40-indias-thermal-power-plants-are-water-scarce-areas-threatening-shutdowns)
- Overheating equipment and power cuts take down phone masts, networks and data centres.Source: [Asia News Network, 2026: Bangladesh’s mobile signals weaken as blackouts spread](https://asianews.network/?p=269961)
- When the grid fails, homes, shops and hospitals that can afford it switch to diesel.Source: [IFC, 2019: The Dirty Footprint of the Broken Grid (20–30 million back-up generator sites)](https://www.ifc.org/en/pressroom/2019/new-ifc-study-details-environmental-economic-health-costs-of-fossil-fuel-generators)
- Heat, drought and missing upkeep kill street trees and parks just when their shade is needed. Across 164 cities, 56% of urban tree and shrub species already face temperatures beyond those in their natural range, and in hot cities such as New Delhi and Singapore every species studied is at risk.Source: [Esperon-Rodriguez et al., Nature Climate Change, 2022: climate change increases global risk to urban forests](https://www.vibrantcitieslab.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/climate-change-increases-global-risk-to-urban-forests.pdf)
- After Ahmedabad’s heat plan prepared hospitals and warned the public, an evaluation estimated about 1,190 deaths a year were avoided.Source: [Hess et al., Journal of Environmental and Public Health, 2018](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6236972/)
