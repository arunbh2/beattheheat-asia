# Extreme heat in Bengaluru

A plateau city that has lost most of its tree cover and lakes as it has grown and warmed.

**Heat type.** Mostly dry heat.

**Heat action plan** No published city heat plan foundNo published city heat action plan found. Researchers have called for ward-level heat action plans; the state plans for Karnataka cover the city.

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Bengaluru

Cycle | Why here | One action that breaks it |

**The dying-green loop** | Bengaluru’s green cover fell from 68% in 1973 to about 6%, and more than 79% of its waterbodies have vanished or been encroached upon. | Keep city trees alive |
**The overwhelmed-clinic loop** | 160 urban primary health centres serve 198 municipal wards: fewer than one per ward. | Heat action plans |

## Who heat hits hardest here

- Workers in heat-exposed jobs
- People in informal settlements

## Four questions for Bengaluru’s heat plan

- Is it funded, with a named owner?
- Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
- Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
- Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
