# Extreme heat in Chiang Mai

A northern Thai city where the hottest weeks of the year arrive with the haze season.

**Heat type.** Mostly dry heat.

**Heat action plan** No published city heat plan foundNo published city heat action plan found. In May 2026 temperatures in Chiang Mai and other northern provinces were forecast to reach 40 °C, and health officials urged people to avoid long periods outdoors.

## Who heat hits hardest here

- Older people
- Workers in heat-exposed jobs

## Four questions for Chiang Mai’s heat plan

- Is it funded, with a named owner?
- Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
- Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
- Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
