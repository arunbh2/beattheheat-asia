# Extreme heat in Surabaya

Indonesia’s second city, long known for its heat, which put in public parks where others put in buildings.

**Heat type.** Mostly humid heat.

**Heat action plan** No published city heat plan foundNo published city heat action plan found. Indonesia’s meteorology agency counts Surabaya among the 20% of the country’s cities with the highest land surface temperatures.

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Surabaya

Cycle | Why here | One action that breaks it |

**The dying-green loop** | The city added public parks where others added buildings, and has seen cooling, economic and creative benefits. | Keep city trees alive |
**The green-displacement loop** | A policy report on Surabaya’s kampungs notes that greening and other cooling measures tend to benefit wealthier groups first, and looks at small-scale cooling for dense kampungs. | Green without pushing people out |

## Who heat hits hardest here

- People in informal settlements
- Workers in heat-exposed jobs

## Four questions for Surabaya’s heat plan

- Is it funded, with a named owner?
- Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
- Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
- Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
