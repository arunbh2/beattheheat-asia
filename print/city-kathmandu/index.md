# Extreme heat in Kathmandu

A valley city warming fast, below the Himalayan ice that feeds South Asia’s rivers.

**Heat type.** Milder heat at altitude, hottest before the monsoon.

**Heat action plan** No published city heat plan foundNo city heat action plan found. Nepal adopted a national plan of action on heat waves in 2024, and Nepalgunj, in the plains, wrote the country’s first local heat action plan in 2023.

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Kathmandu

Cycle | Why here | One action that breaks it |

**The melting-ice loop** | The Hindu Kush Himalaya’s glaciers lost ice 65% faster in the 2010s, and could lose 30–50% of their volume by 2100. | Deep emission cuts |
**The medical-debt trap** | In five cities in Nepal and Bangladesh, heat cut incomes and pushed some households towards high-interest loans. | Heat-safe work |

## Who heat hits hardest here

- Women whose livelihoods are held back by unequal access
- Older people

## Four questions for Kathmandu’s heat plan

- Is it funded, with a named owner?
- Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
- Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
- Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
