# Using Common Shade in a 90-minute workshop

A shared picture first, then the felt experience of one worker’s week, then a plan with owners, money and triggers, and decisions recorded before people leave.

## Running order

Minutes | What happens | Why |

**0–10** | **Open with the film, then today’s heat in your city** | A shared picture before any discussion. |
**10–35** | **A week in the heat, in small groups, one worker each** | People feel where protection fails, before they debate it. |
**35–45** | **Groups report: what reached their worker, and what broke** | Turns individual stories into a shared list of gaps. |
**45–75** | **Plan a response together, on one screen** | Owners, money and triggers for the gaps the groups found. Run the stress test if time allows. |
**75–90** | **Agree the next three decisions, and who takes each** | Leave with commitments, recorded on the plan’s status ladder. |

## The five tools, in the order they build on each other

Tool | What you leave with | Time |

**1. Feel it**
A week in the heat | Where a worker’s money runs out, and which protection actually reaches them. | 15 min |
**2. Find what works**
Practical actions and cooling options | Actions by setting, with who does them, what they cost and what keeps them working. | 10–20 min |
**3. Plan it**
Plan a response | A plan with owners, money, triggers and a stress test of essential services. | 30–60 min |
**4. Make it work together**
The coordinator’s simulation | Who has to say yes, what breaks if they don’t, and a place protection plan. | 30 min |
**5. Learn from others**
How five cities organise for heat | Mechanisms to adapt, and what to check before you do. | 5 min |

## Tips for the facilitator

- One person drives the screen; everyone else talks.
- Use a real place the room knows, but keep the worker composite.
- Record what is agreed, and what is only discussed. The plan’s status ladder does this.
- Close by printing the plan, or copying it into the meeting notes.

## Before the session

- Choose the place the room knows best.
- Open the map, the worker exercise and the plan in three tabs.
- Check the projector shows small text clearly.
- Decide who records what is agreed.
