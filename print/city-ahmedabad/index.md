# Extreme heat in Ahmedabad

Where South Asia’s first heat action plan began, and where its results were measured.

**Heat type.** Dry heat before the monsoon, then humid heat during it.

**Heat action plan** Heat plan in forceAhmedabad launched South Asia’s first heat action plan in 2013, after a 2010 heatwave caused an estimated 1,344 excess deaths. An estimated 1,190 deaths a year were later avoided.

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Ahmedabad

Cycle | Why here | One action that breaks it |

**The overwhelmed-clinic loop** | The plan prepared hospitals and warned the public before heat peaked. | Heat action plans |
**The poverty–heat trap** | Up to three-quarters of India’s workforce depends on heat-exposed work. | Heat-safe work |
**The invisible-deaths loop** | As in most countries, heat deaths are hard to count, which makes the case for prevention harder to see. | Count every heat death |

## Who heat hits hardest here

- Workers in heat-exposed jobs
- People in informal settlements

## A worked plan to learn from

**Ahmedabad’s heat action plan** (Ahmedabad, India, 2013 onwards)

## Four questions for Ahmedabad’s heat plan

- Is it funded, with a named owner?
- Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
- Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
- Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
