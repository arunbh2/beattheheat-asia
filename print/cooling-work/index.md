# Cooling options: Outdoor work

What to build or change, what keeps it working, the traps to avoid and rough costs.

**Protects.** People whose income depends on being outdoors in the hottest hours: vendors, loaders, builders, sanitation workers, traffic police.

**Key caution.** A shelter without water, paid rest or a change of hours is not worker protection.

## Options, and what keeps each working

Option | What it does | Keeps working with | Avoid | Rough cost |

**Rest shelters on work routes** | Shade, seating and water where workers can recover during a shift. | Paid rest time, and someone who cleans and refills it. | Treating the shelter as a substitute for work rules. | About US$1,600–US$5,300 per site (₹1.5 lakh–₹5 lakh at Indian prices) |
**Drinking water and rehydration salts** | Refill points with safe water and oral rehydration salts at markets, sites and junctions. | A daily refill and cleaning roster, often ₹500–2,000 a day. | Counting it as cooling: it protects bodies, not temperatures. | About US$110–US$530 per site (₹10,000–₹50,000 at Indian prices) |
**Shade nets** | Quick shade over junctions, queues and work yards. | Poles set firmly into the ground, and nets up before the heat starts. | Bamboo frames that fall in the first storm. | About US$3.2–US$9.5 per m² (₹300–₹900 at Indian prices) |
**Shade trees on work routes** | Canopy along the streets where people work and wait. | Water, root space and three years of care. | Counting saplings planted instead of canopy that survives. | About US$21–US$84 per tree, with 2–3 years’ care (₹2,000–₹8,000 at Indian prices) |
**Misting at hotspots** | Fine water spray at a few crowded, dry spots. | Safe water, drainage, filters and a technician. | Humid places and water-short cities: in humid heat the benefit roughly halves. | About US$420–US$2,100 per site (₹40,000–₹2 lakh at Indian prices) |

## What the evidence says

- Nagpur strung green shade nets over 10–12 busy junctions in May 2026. Several tore or collapsed in strong winds, and residents pointed out the heat had begun at the end of March. A net had also fallen at Tukdoji Square in 2025 because its bamboo was not set firmly into the road.
- Comparing Phoenix with humid Houston, misters still cut heat stress in the humid city, but with about half the benefit, and only when there was some wind.

**How you will know it works.** Workers within a few minutes’ walk of shade and water, and rest breaks actually taken

**Five traps that waste cooling money:** Buying before diagnosing · Built, with no one to keep it · Green without water · Promising degrees you haven’t measured · A list of activities, not a model.

**Before you choose, ask:** Does it need electricity, and is the supply reliable in the hottest weeks? · What happens in a power cut: does it stop, or keep cooling without power? · Does it need water, and where will that water come from in a dry spell? · Who services or repairs it, and how fast? · Who owns the running cost each year, and from which budget? · Who can use it: renters, women, older people, people with disabilities? **Keep the outcomes apart:** a cooler room, comfort, less heat strain, better health and kept income are different outcomes; measure the one you mean.

Costs are rough planning ranges for early budgeting, not quotes. They are Indian prices, from a practitioner’s planning workbook, shown in US dollars at about ₹95 to US$1 (mid-2026) with the rupee range alongside.
