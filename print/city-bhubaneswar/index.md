# Extreme heat in Bhubaneswar

An eastern state capital that has paired its heat plan with a plan for cooling demand.

**Heat type.** Mostly humid heat.

**Heat action plan** Heat plan in forceIn September 2025 the Bhubaneswar Municipal Corporation and the think tank iFOREST launched the city’s first Integrated Heat and Cooling Action Plan, which addresses heat stress and cooling demand together. An earlier climate-adaptive heat action plan, prepared with IRADe, the Indian Institute of Public Health and the Odisha State Disaster Management Authority, mapped heat hotspots ward by ward.

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Bhubaneswar

Cycle | Why here | One action that breaks it |

**The cooling trap** | The city’s heat and cooling plan expects demand for air conditioning to rise sevenfold by 2050. | Cool by design |
**The poverty–heat trap** | The city’s heat plan found poor working women more vulnerable to heat stress, with dehydration among women who lack drinking water at work. | Heat-safe work |

## Who heat hits hardest here

- Women whose livelihoods are held back by unequal access
- Workers in heat-exposed jobs

## Four questions for Bhubaneswar’s heat plan

- Is it funded, with a named owner?
- Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
- Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
- Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
