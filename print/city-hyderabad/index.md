# Extreme heat in Hyderabad

A fast-growing Deccan city in a state that classes heatwaves as a disaster.

**Heat type.** Mostly dry heat.

**Heat action plan** Parts in place, no full plan foundTelangana classes heatwaves as a state-specific disaster. Its Heatwave Action Plan 2025 includes strategies for Hyderabad and a heat stress and vulnerability assessment for all 33 districts, and the state’s heatwave plan opens malls, public buildings and religious places as water points and cooling centres. No standalone city heat action plan found.

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Hyderabad

Cycle | Why here | One action that breaks it |

**The poverty–heat trap** | During heat alerts, Telangana’s plan moves outdoor work out of the hours from 12 noon to 3 pm. | Heat-safe work |
**The lost-schooling loop** | Telangana’s plan sets water bells in schools, avoids open-air classes and closes anganwadi centres by 11 am. | Keep children in school |

## Who heat hits hardest here

- Workers in heat-exposed jobs
- Migrants, prisoners and other excluded groups

## Four questions for Hyderabad’s heat plan

- Is it funded, with a named owner?
- Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
- Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
- Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
