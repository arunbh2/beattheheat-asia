Extreme heat in Singapore

A city-state with hot nights, dense towers and an early national heat stress advisory.

Heat type. Humid heat, all year round.
Heat action plan Heat plan in force

Singapore launched a national Heat Stress Advisory in July 2023. It rates heat stress as low, moderate or high from the wet-bulb globe temperature, and tells people what to do at each level.

Four questions for Singapore’s heat plan

  • Is it funded, with a named owner?
  • Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
  • Does it have legal backing, so departments must act?
  • Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
