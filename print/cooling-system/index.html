Cooling options: Making it last

What to build or change, what keeps it working, the traps to avoid and rough costs.

Protects. Every asset above: who targets it, who opens it on an alert, who pays for upkeep, and who hears complaints.
Key caution. Often the most useful layer, and the easiest to leave unfunded.

What the evidence says

  • In Melbourne, officials found money for new green infrastructure was usually available, but running budgets were under pressure and rarely increased, even as the area to maintain grew.
  • In Santiago, without long-term financing, only better-off municipalities could take part in green-space programmes, because only they could afford the extra upkeep.
  • Less than 10% of the climate finance that reaches cities goes to adaptation and resilience.
  • The IUCN Global Standard for Nature-based Solutions makes inclusive governance and equity one of its eight criteria.
How you will know it works. Cooling assets still working after three years, each with a named owner and an upkeep budget
