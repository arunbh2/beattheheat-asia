# Extreme heat in Mumbai

A coastal megacity where the nights are heating faster than the days.

**Heat type.** Mostly humid coastal heat.

**Heat action plan** Parts in place, no full plan foundMumbai’s 2022 Climate Action Plan names urban heat as a priority, and the city is working with C40 on heat-health protocols for its hospitals. No standalone city heat action plan found.

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Mumbai

Cycle | Why here | One action that breaks it |

**The sleepless-nights loop** | Mumbai gained 15 very warm nights a summer over the last decade. | Cool nights and support |
**The energy-poverty trap** | Tin-roofed informal settlements trap heat for people who can’t afford to cool. | Free care for heat illness |
**The invisible-deaths loop** | Death certificates usually record the immediate cause, so heat as the trigger goes unrecorded. | Count every heat death |

## Who heat hits hardest here

- People in informal settlements
- Workers in heat-exposed jobs

## A worked plan to learn from

**Thane’s ward-by-ward heat action plan** (Thane, India, 2024 onwards)

## Already at work here

## Four questions for Mumbai’s heat plan

- Is it funded, with a named owner?
- Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
- Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
- Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
