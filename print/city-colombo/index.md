# Extreme heat in Colombo

A coastal capital where humid heat meets crowded markets, harbours and fish landing sites.

**Heat type.** Humid heat, all year round.

**Heat action plan** Parts in place, no full plan foundColombo Municipal Council, with the Department of Meteorology and SLYCAN Trust, has worked on guidelines for a gender-sensitive heat action plan. The Department of Meteorology issues heat index advisories. No adopted city heat plan found yet.

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Colombo

Cycle | Why here | One action that breaks it |

**The cooling trap** | A University of Colombo study found a pronounced urban heat island, as green space gives way to concrete and asphalt. | Cool by design |
**The poverty–heat trap** | Heat advisories ask people to work in the shade; market and fish workers who sell in the open often cannot. | Heat-safe work |

## Who heat hits hardest here

- Workers in heat-exposed jobs
- Coastal and riverine communities

## Already at work here

- Asia-Pacific Network for Global Change Research (APN)

## Four questions for Colombo’s heat plan

- Is it funded, with a named owner?
- Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
- Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
- Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
