# One setting, protected before the heat

One sheet for each setting, filled in together at one meeting with what you already know. In part 3, tick each item on the day and fix anything left unticked.

A filled-in example (illustrative)

**Setting**: Schools

**Site**: A ward primary school: the midday meal queue

**Who is exposed, and when**: Pupils queuing in the open courtyard, 12:30 to 1:15, on school days

**Proposed change**: Serve from the shaded veranda; keep the ramp clear; meal staff agree the layout

**Authorises it**: Headteacher with the School Management Committee; the building owner for any fixture

**Runs it**: Meal staff; a teacher checks the drinking-water point each morning

**Ready by**: Before the first hot week of term

**One result to check**: Minutes pupils wait in direct sun at lunch, on comparable days, before and after

## 3Three pause points *Tick on the day.*

### Before opening

- Shade where people actually stand or wait, at the hottest hour
- Drinking water at peak hours; storage checked
- Power cut: who refills, and how fast, agreed
- Step-free way in, passable with a wheelchair or handcart
- Failure number posted on site

### When an official warning is issued

The warning comes from *e.g. IMD district warning, as issued*

Who receives it here

- Refill brought forward
- Water hours extended
- Queue or assembly moved into shade
