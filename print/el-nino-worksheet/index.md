# One service, protected before the heat

One sheet for each service, filled in together at one meeting with what you already know. In part 3, tick each item on the day and fix anything left unticked.

## 1Choose the service

Service and sites *e.g. the daily market, two lanes*

Who relies on it in the hottest hours, and what they lose if they leave *e.g. sellers lose sales*

Conditions that describe our city (tick all that apply)

- Busy outdoor places through hot afternoons
- Hot nights or humid heat
- Summer water that stops or depends on power or tankers
- Short, intense rain that floods routes or sites

What we already know, and from where *e.g. market register, last summer's tanker orders*

## 2Agree who keeps it working *These can be different bodies.*

Role | Organisation | Name and phone |
Authorises the site | | |

Working by (date), because *when heat usually arrives here, less the time to buy and install*

## 3Three pause points *Tick on the day.*

### Before opening

- Shade over occupied places at 2 p.m.
- Drinking water at peak hours; storage checked
- Power cut: who refills, and how fast, agreed
- Step-free way in, passable with a handcart
- Failure number posted on site

### When an official warning is issued

The warning comes from *e.g. IMD district warning, as issued*

Who receives it here

- Refill brought forward
- Water hours extended
- Queues moved under cover

### During and after

Who could not use it, and why

Sites working at 2 p.m.: ____ of ____

Failures reported ____ · fixed ____ · slowest fix ____ hours
