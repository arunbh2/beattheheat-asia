# Extreme heat in Phnom Penh

A fast-growing capital of garment factories and metal roofs, where the hot season peaks before the rains.

**Heat type.** Humid heat, hottest from March to May.

**Heat action plan** No published city heat plan foundA proposal with the Ministry of Water Resources and Meteorology and Phnom Penh Capital Hall would develop a heat action plan for the city, with thresholds and response protocols. No adopted city heat plan found yet.

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Phnom Penh

Cycle | Why here | One action that breaks it |

**The cooling trap** | UN ESCAP projects that the urban heat island could add 2 °C to 7 °C on top of global warming in Phnom Penh. | Cool by design |
**The poverty–heat trap** | At three garment factories, workers reporting heat symptoms rose from 68% in the cooler months to 88% in the hot months. | Heat-safe work |
**The lost-schooling loop** | In the April 2024 heatwave, Cambodia shortened public school days by two hours. | Keep children in school |

## Who heat hits hardest here

- Workers in heat-exposed jobs
- People in informal settlements

## Already at work here

- UN ESCAP

## Four questions for Phnom Penh’s heat plan

- Is it funded, with a named owner?
- Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
- Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
- Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
