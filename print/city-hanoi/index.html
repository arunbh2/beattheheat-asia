Extreme heat in Hanoi

A fast-growing northern capital whose power and water strain together in the heat.

Heat type. Mostly humid heat, in a hot, wet summer.
Heat action plan No published city heat plan found

No city heat action plan found. From 2018 to 2021 the Viet Nam Red Cross ran an early-action plan for heatwaves in 15 of Hanoi’s wards, triggered by a heat-index forecast.

Four questions for Hanoi’s heat plan

  • Is it funded, with a named owner?
  • Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
  • Does it have legal backing, so departments must act?
  • Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
