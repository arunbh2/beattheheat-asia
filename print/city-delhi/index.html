    City brief · For city officials, partners and funders

    Extreme heat in Delhi

    A capital of extreme pre-monsoon heat, street trade and falling groundwater.

    Heat type. Dry heat before the monsoon, then humid heat during it.
    Heat action plan Heat plan in force

    Delhi launched its 2025 Heat Action Plan in April 2025, with graded heat alerts, cooling shelters, 3,000 water points and heatstroke units in hospitals. Experts have recommended legal backing and dedicated funds to strengthen it.

    Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Delhi

    CycleWhy hereOne action that breaks it
    The heat credit squeezeOf 721 Delhi street vendors surveyed in 2024, 80% saw fewer customers in heatwaves and 49% lost income.Heat-smart lending
    The dying-green loopNew Delhi is among the cities where every urban tree species studied is at risk from rising heat.Keep city trees alive
    The groundwater loopNorth-west India (Rajasthan, Punjab and Haryana) lost 109 km³ of groundwater in six years, mainly to irrigation.Smart irrigation and groundwater rules
    The invisible-deaths loopIndia confirmed 185 heatstroke deaths against 48,385 suspected cases in 2024, up to 28 July.Count every heat death

    Who heat hits hardest here

    • Workers in heat-exposed jobs
    • People in informal settlements

    A worked plan to learn from

    Ahmedabad’s heat action plan (Ahmedabad, India, 2013 onwards)

    Already at work here

    • National Disaster Management Authority, India
    • India Meteorological Department and IITM Pune
    • National Centre for Disease Control, India
    • Council on Energy, Environment and Water (CEEW)
    • SEEDS
    • HeatWatch India

    Listing does not imply partnership, affiliation or endorsement.

    Four questions for Delhi’s heat plan

    • Is it funded, with a named owner?
    • Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
    • Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
    • Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
