Extreme heat in Delhi

A capital of extreme pre-monsoon heat, street trade and falling groundwater.

Heat type. Dry heat before the monsoon, then humid heat during it.
Heat action plan Heat plan in force

Delhi launched its 2025 Heat Action Plan in April 2025, with graded heat alerts, cooling shelters, 3,000 water points and heatstroke units in hospitals. Experts have recommended legal backing and dedicated funds to strengthen it.

Four questions for Delhi’s heat plan

  • Is it funded, with a named owner?
  • Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
  • Does it have legal backing, so departments must act?
  • Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
