# Extreme heat in Ho Chi Minh City

Viet Nam’s largest city, where heat radiates from crowded streets onto people who work outdoors.

**Heat type.** Mostly humid heat.

**Heat action plan** Parts in place, no full plan foundIn July 2026 the city approved an action plan for climate change response to 2030, with a vision to 2050, which aims to strengthen its capacity against heatwaves, droughts, heavy rain, flooding and rising seas. No standalone city heat action plan found.

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Ho Chi Minh City

Cycle | Why here | One action that breaks it |

**The poverty–heat trap** | Of 734 outdoor workers surveyed in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City, 94% reported at least one heat-related health problem while working during heatwaves. | Heat-safe work |
**The dying-green loop** | Modelling found that more green and permeable surfaces in the most built-up areas could cut heatwave days in the city’s hotspots by up to 24 days. | Keep city trees alive |

## Who heat hits hardest here

- Workers in heat-exposed jobs
- People in informal settlements
- Older people

## Four questions for Ho Chi Minh City’s heat plan

- Is it funded, with a named owner?
- Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
- Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
- Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
