# Extreme heat in Manila

A dense, humid metropolis where heat closes schools and pushes the heat index into danger.

**Heat type.** Mostly humid heat.

**Heat action plan** Parts in place, no full plan foundIn Metro Manila, Quezon City has set up a city Heat Action Team, named chief heat action officers and opened 32 libraries as cooling centres. Its full heat action plan is still being developed.

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Manila

Cycle | Why here | One action that breaks it |

**The lost-schooling loop** | In April 2024, 5,288 schools in the Philippines moved to remote learning, affecting more than 3.6 million students. | Keep children in school |
**The cooling trap** | One of the dense, high-rise city cores where air-conditioner waste heat adds to the night. | Cool by design |
**The farm-debt loop** | Rice yields in the Philippines fell about 10% for each 1 °C rise in growing-season night temperature. | Crop insurance and fair credit |

## Who heat hits hardest here

- Workers in heat-exposed jobs
- People in informal settlements

## Already at work here

- PAGASA, Philippines
- Department of Education, Philippines

Listing does not imply partnership, affiliation or endorsement.

## Four questions for Manila’s heat plan

- Is it funded, with a named owner?
- Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
- Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
- Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
