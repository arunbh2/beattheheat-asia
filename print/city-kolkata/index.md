# Extreme heat in Kolkata

A humid delta megacity where the heat index runs far above the thermometer.

**Heat type.** Mostly humid heat.

**Heat action plan** Parts in place, no full plan foundWest Bengal’s heat wave action plan gives municipal corporations and local bodies the task of preparing and carrying out heat wave action plans. The Kolkata Climate Action Plan, released by the Kolkata Municipal Corporation for consultation in June 2025, lists a city-wide heat action plan among its priority actions. No standalone city heat action plan found.

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Kolkata

Cycle | Why here | One action that breaks it |

**The invisible-deaths loop** | Kolkata’s daily average heat index usually runs 6 to 12 °C above the air temperature, but India’s heatwave criteria leave out humidity, so the danger of humid heat goes unreported. | Count every heat death |
**The poverty–heat trap** | Street vendors and other informal workers are caught between health risks and lost income as the city’s heat islands intensify. | Heat-safe work |

## Who heat hits hardest here

- Workers in heat-exposed jobs
- People in informal settlements

## Four questions for Kolkata’s heat plan

- Is it funded, with a named owner?
- Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
- Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
- Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
