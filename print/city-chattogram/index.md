# Extreme heat in Chattogram

Bangladesh’s port city, which had the country’s highest climate-driven heat exposure in a recent summer.

**Heat type.** Mostly humid coastal heat.

**Heat action plan** No published city heat plan foundNo published city heat action plan found. In a recent summer the city had 59 risky heat days, the country’s highest climate-driven heat exposure. During heatwaves, Bangladesh Red Crescent volunteers in the city have handed out cold drinks to outdoor workers, rickshaw drivers and traffic police.

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Chattogram

Cycle | Why here | One action that breaks it |

**The overwhelmed-clinic loop** | A mild heatwave in April 2024 brought a rise in fever, cough and diarrhoea, seen in private clinics and the medicine ward of Chittagong Medical College Hospital. | Heat action plans |
**The energy-poverty trap** | Across Bangladesh, households facing heat spend large sums on fans, refrigerators and air conditioners, and more on cooler travel, straining their budgets. | Free care for heat illness |

## Who heat hits hardest here

- Workers in heat-exposed jobs
- People in informal settlements

## Four questions for Chattogram’s heat plan

- Is it funded, with a named owner?
- Does it assess who is most vulnerable, and where they live?
- Does it have legal backing, so departments are required to act?
- Does it count indoor and night-time heat, not only daytime highs?
