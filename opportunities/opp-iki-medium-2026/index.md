# IKI Medium Grants 2026

International Climate Initiative (IKI) · One or two ODA-eligible countries in the Global South.

Grant or funding callFor organisationsHeat-network organisationClosed by providerConflicting information

Climate action and biodiversity projects in ODA-eligible countries, led by organisations based in Germany with local partners.

**Location**: One or two ODA-eligible countries in the Global South.

**Why this is relevant to heat**: Adaptation has been a theme in past rounds; a heat project's fit depends on the 2026 funding announcement's priorities.

**Deadline**: No deadline published (not the same as rolling)

**Funding**: EUR 300,000 to EUR 800,000 per award

**Source checked**: 22 November 2026

**Experience**: Not stated

**Contract or duration**: Not stated

**Who can apply and from where**: Organisations established in Germany, with one or two local implementing partners in the implementation country.

**How to apply**: As described on the official listing.

Conflicting informationThe IKI page lists Medium Grants 2026 as closed; secondary listings gave a deadline of 12 January 2027. The official page is followed here.

Not openThe source says applications are closed. This listing is kept in the archive for the record.
