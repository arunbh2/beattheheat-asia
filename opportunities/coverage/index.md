# Coverage of the heat network

Checked 204 of 367 network organisations in the latest review cycle (28 November 2026).

Listings are not exhaustive. Common Shade checks the public opportunity pages of every organisation in its heat network, plus a few official feeds; many organisations publish on portals that need a login or JavaScript, which a person reviews by hand.

An organisation's coverage status says whether its pages were read in this review cycle. It is separate from how fresh each listing is: every listing carries its own "Source checked" date. "None found" means none in the pages reviewed, not that the organisation has no opportunities anywhere.

- **4** Checked; relevant opportunities found
- **200** Checked; none found in the reviewed sources
- **90** Manual review required
- **40** Login or access blocked
- **33** Source unavailable
