# GCF Readiness support: proposals accepted at any time, recommended by 31 March 2027

Green Climate Fund · Developing countries that are Parties to the UNFCCC.

Partnership or support callFor organisationsRelated to heat resilienceRolling (as stated)Verified

Readiness support for national institutions, country programming and direct access entities; submissions may be made at any time during the Readiness Strategy 2024 to 2027.

**Location**: Developing countries that are Parties to the UNFCCC.

**Why this is relevant to heat**: Readiness can support adaptation planning that covers heat, where national priorities include it.

**Deadline**: 31 March 2027 (cut-off time not published)As published: “recommended proposal submission date of no later than 31 March 2027”

**Funding**: Not published

**Source checked**: 22 November 2026

**Experience**: Not stated

**Contract or duration**: Not stated

**Who can apply and from where**: National Designated Authorities, or entities they nominate; not open to other organisations directly.

**How to apply**: As described on the official listing.
