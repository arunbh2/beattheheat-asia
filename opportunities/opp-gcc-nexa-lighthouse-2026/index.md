# Nexa Lighthouse: Transition to Scale funding for climate and health innovation in Asia and the Pacific

Grand Challenges Canada · Projects implemented in eligible countries in Asia and the Pacific, listed in Appendix B of the funding opportunity.

Grant or funding callFor organisationsDirect heat relevanceOpenVerified

Funds proven innovations that help local health actors turn climate-risk signals into timely action, for hazards including extreme heat.

**Location**: Projects implemented in eligible countries in Asia and the Pacific, listed in Appendix B of the funding opportunity.

**Why this is relevant to heat**: Extreme heat is one of the hazards named in the call.

**Deadline**: 8 December 2026, 14:00 ET · 10 days leftAs published: “December 8, 2026, 2:00 p.m. ET (extended)”Extended: earlier deadline 17 November 2026

**Funding**: USD 250,000 to USD 2,000,000 per award

**Source checked**: 28 November 2026

**Experience**: Not stated

**Contract or duration**: Not stated

**Who can apply and from where**: Applicants incorporated and implementing in countries across Asia and the Pacific; the full list of eligible countries is in Appendix B of the funding opportunity.

**How to apply**: As described on the official listing.
