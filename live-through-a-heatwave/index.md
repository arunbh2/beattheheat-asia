# Live through a heatwave

Heat reaches people through their work, their homes and bodies, and the systems everyone depends on. Choose whose heatwave to follow: each takes about five minutes, and each ends with what could protect people at scale.

- [Work and income

### A week in the heat

Follow one worker’s money through a heatwave week, build protection, then try to break it.Begin](https://beattheheat.asia/a-week-in-the-heat/)
- [Care and health

### A night in the heat

Stay with an older woman living alone, a pregnant woman or a family with a baby through a hot night and a clinic visit.Begin](https://beattheheat.asia/a-night-in-the-heat/)
- [City systems

### A city under strain

Stress-test a city’s power, water, clinics and trees through a five-day heatwave, and see what holds.Begin](https://beattheheat.asia/a-city-under-strain/)

Each is a teaching exercise built from the evidence on this site, with composite, drawn people. None predicts what will happen to a real person or place.
