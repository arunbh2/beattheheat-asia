The ladder

## From promise to proof

Of the 8 commitments tracked so far, 1 has been delivered and none has been evaluated. The furthest any has climbed is "Delivered".

- 0
**1. Announced**Said in public. A weak signal on its own: announcements happen whether or not anything follows.
- 4
**2. Adopted**Made formal: a plan approved, a rule changed, an office set up.
- 3
**3. Funded**Money committed to it, whether or not the amount is public.
- 1
**4. Delivered**Something exists that people can use: a service open, a guide published, money paid out.
- 0
**5. Evaluated**Its results were measured and published.

A signal is a step, not a score. The ladder records how far each signal has got, never how well an institution performs, and it says nothing about places not shown. An announcement is a weak signal on its own; each rung is stronger evidence that change is real.

Commitment Something an institution has promised or started; it climbs the ladder.Gathering A conference or forum where the field meets; what it produces may become commitments.Finding New science that changes how heat is understood.Guidance A framework or guide published for others to use.

Finding9 November 2026

### 2026 will be one of the four warmest years on record

World Meteorological Organization

WMO's State of the Climate Update 2026, released as COP31 opened, finds that the past 12 years have each been the warmest on record and the past four the top four. The global average surface temperature for January to September 2026 was about 1.47 °C above the pre-industrial average. In early November the EU's Copernicus service said 2026 is most likely to be the second-hottest year on record.

What to watchA finding, not a commitment: watch whether heat plans and budgets are sized for the heat these years now bring, not the heat people remember.

### El Niño set to bring more heat into 2027

World Meteorological Organization

By mid-August 2026 El Niño had become firmly established. WMO reports it has been intensifying since August and is expected to be the strongest on record by December, set to unleash even more heat and extreme weather well into 2027.

What to watchEarly warnings and heat plans ready for the 2027 hot season, above all in Southeast Asia, where El Niño and warming broke records in April 2016.

### Warming will overshoot 1.5 °C: the Emissions Gap Report 2026

UNEP, with PBL Netherlands Environmental Assessment Agency

UNEP's Emissions Gap Report 2026, The Point of Return, finds that with global temperature rise set to exceed 1.5 °C, urgent and stringent cuts are needed to minimise the size and length of the overshoot and return to 1.5 °C by the end of the century. If current policies continue, warming could reach 2.8 °C this century; if all national pledges are delivered, 2.6 °C.

What to watchA finding, not a commitment: watch whether adaptation plans for heat assume the warming these projections describe.

### Bangkok's heat plan opens 304 cooling centres

Bangkok Metropolitan Administration

For the 2026 hot season Bangkok opened 304 cooling centres in schools, health centres and district offices. The city's own Urban Heat Management Action Plan for 2026 has 22 indicators, and a 2026 financing strategy with UNDP costs it at about THB 146 million to set up and THB 10.8 million a year to run, noting that running costs are not always covered.

What would move it upEvaluated: who used the cooling centres, and with what result.

How it has moved

- 2026 **Adopted** The city's Urban Heat Management Action Plan for 2026
- 2026 **Delivered** 304 cooling centres open for the hot season

CommitmentAugust 2026

### India adds heatwaves to its notified natural calamities

Government of India

In August 2026 India added heatwaves to its notified natural calamities, so national and state disaster response funds can pay for heatwave relief. Before, states could spend only up to 10% of their response fund on locally declared disasters such as heat. Heat mitigation projects are funded separately, from disaster mitigation funds.

What would move it upFunded and delivered: heatwave relief paid from a state or national response fund, and reported.

How it has moved

- August 2026 **Adopted** A rule change, reported from a reply in India's Lok Sabha

Global Heat Forum

FindingMay 2026

### April's pre-monsoon heatwave was made three times more likely

World Weather Attribution

Fifteen days of heat in late April 2026 pushed India's electricity demand to a record and brought farm drought to more than a million square kilometres. Scientists found climate change made it three times more likely, and that April heat like this now returns about every five years.

What to watchA finding, not a commitment: watch whether plans are sized to heat that now returns about every five years.
