Türkiye, as COP31 host, and the World Health Organization Global (UN climate process)

### [El Niño set to bring more heat into 2027](#sig-el-nino-2026-2027)

World Meteorological Organization Global

By mid-August 2026 El Niño had become firmly established. WMO reports it has been intensifying since August and is expected to be the strongest on record by December, set to unleash even more heat and extreme weather well into 2027. WMO calls it a risk multiplier for health, and reports that WHO has, for the first time, classified the anticipated health impacts as a health emergency.

What to watchEarly warnings and heat plans ready for the 2027 hot season, above all in Southeast Asia, where El Niño and warming broke records in April 2016.

### [2026 will be one of the four warmest years on record](#sig-wmo-state-of-climate-2026)

World Meteorological Organization Global

WMO's State of the Climate Update 2026, released as COP31 opened, finds that the past 12 years have each been the warmest on record and the past four the top four. The global average surface temperature for January to September 2026 was about 1.47 °C above the pre-industrial average. In early November the EU's Copernicus service said 2026 is most likely to be the second-hottest year on record. It estimates that 146.5 million people in India were exposed to extreme heat in an April 2026 heatwave, and that the number of countries reporting multi-hazard early warning systems rose from 56 in 2015 to 136 in 2025.

What to watchA finding, not a commitment: watch whether heat plans and budgets are sized for the heat these years now bring, not the heat people remember.

Sources and review

### [The Antalya Compact for Energy Efficient Buildings opens for endorsement](#sig-cop31-antalya-compact-2026)

COP31 Presidency Global (UN climate process)

Ahead of the conference, the COP31 Presidency launched the Antalya Compact for Energy Efficient Buildings, which aims for a 25% cut in the operational energy intensity of buildings worldwide by 2035, compared with 2020. Its actions include stronger energy codes, faster renovation and the electrification of heating and cooling. It covers buildings and energy as a whole, not heat alone; it bears on heat because cooler, more efficient buildings ease the indoor heat people live and work in, and the cost of cooling them.

El Niño watch

## Implications for Asia's next hot season

**Official status**: **El Niño Advisory** NOAA's alert status when El Niño conditions are observed and expected to continue.

**Bulletin**: NOAA Climate Prediction Center, ENSO Diagnostic Discussion, issued 12 November 2026

**Forecast period**: January to March 2027 for strength; ENSO phase odds to April to June 2027

### Observed What changed

El Niño strengthened compared with the previous month: sea surface temperature anomalies exceeded 4.0 °C and extended farther west across the eastern equatorial Pacific. The weekly Niño-3.4 index reached +2.5 °C, and rainfall stayed suppressed over Indonesia.

### Forecast What the bulletin expects

A strong-to-very strong El Niño is likely through January to March 2027 (a greater than 79% chance), before it begins to weaken in early 2027.

For March to May 2027, NOAA puts the chance of El Niño at 80%, most likely weak (53%) or moderate (25%); for April to June 2027, ENSO-neutral becomes more likely (61%) than El Niño (38%).

### Our reading What it means for preparation

The odds favour El Niño persisting into the start of the hot season in much of South and Southeast Asia, and ASMC expects above-normal temperature over the ASEAN region through January 2027. That is a reason to get ordinary heat protection ready early. It is not a forecast of a heatwave anywhere.

### Uncertain What remains uncertain locally

A Pacific probability does not give any town's chance of a heatwave, a water shortage or a flood. NOAA's strength odds describe the Pacific, not the strength of local impacts. Local outcomes depend on the season, geography, other ocean drivers and local warnings.

This panel reflects NOAA's discussion of 12 November 2026. NOAA's next discussion is scheduled for 10 December 2026.

[NOAA's discussion](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_advisory/ensodisc.shtml) · [NOAA's ENSO probabilities](https://cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso/roni/probabilities/) · [ASMC's regional outlook](https://asmc.asean.org/asmc-seasonal-outlook/) · [WMO's seasonal update](https://wmo.int/resources/publication-series/global-seasonal-climate-updates/gscu-ndj2026) · [El Niño and heat in Asia, explained](https://beattheheat.asia/el-nino/)

## How records are kept

### What this page is for

- What changed in heat policy, finance, research and practice, with dates and sources.
- Which developments concern a place, an action or a group of people: use the filters.
- For each promise, what the evidence confirms, what is unknown, and what would show the next step.
- Where to explore the implications: actions, country pages and funding pages.

Signals follows developments outside Common Shade. Watch Common Shade grow records changes to this site; the evidence map appraises what is known; the funding pages explain financial arrangements and the money traced. [Watch Common Shade grow](https://beattheheat.asia/growing/) · [Evidence map](https://beattheheat.asia/evidence-map/) · [Paying for protection](https://beattheheat.asia/paying-for-protection/) · [Funding traced](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/flows/)

### Method

- One canonical record per initiative, with stable identifiers for each record, deliverable and milestone; later developments are added to the same record, and nothing inconvenient is deleted.
- A commitment is tracked deliverable by deliverable. Funding announced, committed, paid out and spent are separate steps, as are a rule's adoption, the date it takes effect, and evidence that it is applied.
- Each milestone names its sources and what each one establishes. Whether it is confirmed comes from the site's source check (data/inbox/*/link-check.json): a page that merely opens is not evidence, and a secondary report is shown as reported until the original is checked.
- An evaluation is recorded with its findings, positive, mixed or negative, and is not treated as proof of cause.
- Interpretation is labelled as Common Shade's reading and kept apart from what sources say. Broad climate or health funding is never shown as money for heat unless the source says so.
- Maintenance: node tools/signals-review.js lists records due for review and what each source's check found, and turns a proposed milestone into a record only with its evidence. Nothing is published without review.

A review re-reads the sources cited here and the site's source-check records. It is not a full search for newer developments; a missing update means none was found in these sources, not that nothing happened.
