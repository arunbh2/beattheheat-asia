# Watch Common Shade grow

See what we are learning, what is changing, and how it connects to protection from extreme heat.

40 changes recorded since 23 October 2026: 5 corrections or revised interpretations, 3 connections documented and 3 questions opened. Beneath them, 1,084 works the commons draws on carry a publication date, and 807 are still being dated.

This is a record of how the knowledge on Common Shade changes. Growth here means more is known and connected; it never means people protected, partnerships formed or impact delivered.

## How this record works

- One record, [growth.json](https://beattheheat.asia/data/site/growth.json), holds every change; both views and the text for AI systems are built from it. Each change traces to the pull request that made it.

- "Why it matters" is our reading. "What the source says" is quoted from the source, and the build checks that the quotation appears in the site's own records.

- A changed file is not progress by itself. Changes are reviewed by a person before they are recorded; nothing is counted twice because it appears on several pages.

- Publication dates come from [growth-dates.json](https://beattheheat.asia/data/site/growth-dates.json), with how each date is known. Dates looked up online are from Crossref or the page's own metadata; an "online by" date is never shown as a publication date.
