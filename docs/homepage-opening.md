# The homepage opening and the shared header

The owner's request, November 2026. Moved here from CLAUDE.md word for word; CLAUDE.md keeps a short pointer. The styles are the last two blocks of `styles.css`, and the header fitting is in `tools/site-nav.js`.

the homepage opening uses five colours only (`--ink`, `--canvas`, `--orange`, `--grey-500`, `--line`; secondary text on ink is canvas at 78%), the spacing scale `--s-1` to `--s-8` (4 to 64px) and the house type roles, with no gradients, glows or grain (the last block of styles.css). Its order puts the title, what "wicked" means, the promise and the two ways in on the first phone screen. In the header Signals is green (the owner's choice), the map is an ink outline and Start here is filled orange; the header fits itself to the space it has (`csn--f1` to `csn--f9` in `tools/site-nav.js`): a small script steps controls to icons and then into the menu sheet, in a fixed order, until the brand and the controls share one row, and the bar wraps to a second row rather than overlapping. The brand, Start here and Menu are never hidden; the brand box never shrinks below its words. Em-based media queries set the first-paint guess before the script runs; without JavaScript the header shows the section links (wrapping onto their own row on narrow screens). Below the opening, the display face is kept for the head of each story (`h2.story__h`) and the epilogue; folded-row, panel and text-version heads are Hanken bold 22, headings inside a section Hanken bold 18 in sentence case, and figures in the display face stay. Every homepage band is canvas except the opening, the role door and "happening today" (ink); each act opens with a 1px `--line-strong` rule and 64px of space, other bands with a hairline; stories have 48px of padding on phones and 64px wider (the second block from the end of styles.css).
