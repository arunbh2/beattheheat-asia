Indonesia

How to read this. Facts link to their sources; anything marked proposed or inferred is the researchers’ suggestion, not a commitment by anyone named. Gaps are gaps in what we found, not faults.

Heat context

Climate: Official guidance or data Predominantly hot-humid lowland exposure, with important monsoonal and seasonally drier conditions; elevation and island differences prohibit a single national cooling prescription. Plans

  • Official guidance or data Health-sector RAN MAPIK2025–2030 adopted by Minister of Health Decree HK.01.07/Menkes/777/2025 on 25August2025; official register lists in force. It covers climate-health broadly and does not itself verify a heat-specific municipal activation protocol. Health-sector climate action plan decree (opens in a new tab) ↗ · Health decree current legal register (opens in a new tab) ↗
  • Official guidance or data I-NCAP2024 provides a cooling roadmap combining passive measures, efficiency and refrigerant transition; binding adoption instrument and funded municipal obligations not verified. Indonesia National Cooling Action Plan (opens in a new tab) ↗
  • Reported, not evaluated NAP2026–2030 submission is documented by UNDP; full attachment retrieval failed, so its detailed heat commitments and legal force were not appraised. UNDP NAP submission and planning systems account (opens in a new tab) ↗
  • Reported, not evaluated KATALIS-HEAT launched July2026 and plans evidence synthesis and municipal heat-health planning; launch is not completed adoption. KATALIS-HEAT launch (opens in a new tab) ↗

Warnings

  • Official guidance or data BMKG publishes forecasts, hot-weather explanations and official information through web, social channels and InfoBMKG. Nationwide validated heat-health impact thresholds, automatic cash triggers and offline reach were not verified. BMKG persistent hot weather explanation (opens in a new tab) ↗
  • Reported, not evaluated Pantau Panas Jakarta combines local sensor information and vulnerability mapping; programme page retains BMKG meteorological warning authority. Accessible rendering supplied no live data, so uptime and clinical validity are not established. Pantau Panas Jakarta (opens in a new tab) ↗
  • Reported, not evaluated CoCHAP describes developing city thresholds and a tiered system in Medan/Surabaya; historical design cannot be labelled a currently operating warning system. CoCHAP Indonesia programme account (opens in a new tab) ↗

Rules for heat at work

Official guidance or data Permenaker5/2018 is in force. Articles7–9 establish assessment/control and include ventilation, drinking water and exposure-time controls; Annex provides ISBB/WBGT limits related to workload and work allocation. No universal ambient-temperature stop-work limit or blanket paid-heat-leave entitlement is inferred. Permenaker 5/2018 current register (opens in a new tab) ↗ · Permenaker 5/2018 regulation text (opens in a new tab) ↗

Costing: next step

Obtain date-specific installed IDR quotations, separate energy/water/staff/maintenance/replacement, then use a dated published FX rate. Neither programme size nor loan principal is a universal protection cost.

Gaps in what we found

  • No harmonised nationwide city heat-health exposure ranking verified.
  • No complete, legally adopted, funded city HAP document retrieved for the seven snapshots; Surabaya drafting is reported separately.
  • Comparable indoor/night exposure, health outcomes, worker income and actual budget execution remain incomplete in collected evidence.
  • Grant/loan eligibility, recurrent maintenance and tenant/disability/migrant access require programme-level verification.

Ways to help (proposed)

  • Contribute local evidence and delivery protocols to the KATALIS-HEAT partners, subject to invitation.
  • Support PMI/city programmes in Medan and Surabaya with warning-to-action tests and inclusion audits.
  • Connect low-income housing technical assistance with grants for households unsuitable for debt.
  • Prepare health-facility/school cooling portfolios with maintenance and power-failure budgets.

Known, inferred, uncertain

Known: Official heat-health activity, labour rules, cooling policies and implementation examples exist. Inferred: Their connection offers a place-based delivery opportunity. Uncertain: National comparability, enforceability of specific heat actions, local budget execution and health effectiveness.
