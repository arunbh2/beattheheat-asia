# Indonesia

## Heat context

**Climate:** Official guidance or data Predominantly hot-humid lowland exposure, with important monsoonal and seasonally drier conditions; elevation and island differences prohibit a single national cooling prescription. [BMKG persistent hot weather explanation ](https://www.bmkg.go.id/berita/utama/suhu-panas-landa-sejumlah-wilayah-indonesia-bmkg-ungkap-penyebab-dan-potensinya-ke-depan) · [CoCHAP Indonesia programme account ](https://preparecenter.org/initiative/coastalheat/cochap-indonesia/) **Night heat:** Measured in a study Urban heat storage makes night exposure important; national and city-specific night-health thresholds and comparable city night trends were not established in this snapshot. [World Bank Unlivable: urban heat in East Asian cities ](https://www.worldbank.org/en/region/eap/publication/unlivable-what-the-urban-heat-island-effect-means-for-east-asia-s-cities) **Temperature records:**

## Plans

- Official guidance or data Health-sector RAN MAPIK2025–2030 adopted by Minister of Health Decree HK.01.07/Menkes/777/2025 on 25August 2025; official register lists in force. It covers climate-health broadly and does not itself verify a heat-specific municipal activation protocol. [Health-sector climate action plan decree ](https://jdih.kemkes.go.id/storage/documents/pdfs/2025kepmenkes777.pdf) · [Health decree current legal register ](https://jdih.kemkes.go.id/documents/keputusan-menteri-kesehatan-nomor-hk0107menkes7772025)
- Official guidance or data I-NCAP2024 provides a cooling roadmap combining passive measures, efficiency and refrigerant transition; binding adoption instrument and funded municipal obligations not verified. [Indonesia National Cooling Action Plan ](https://repository.unescap.org/items/92f8d947-0076-4c72-be50-fd9e3a472ec1/full)
- Reported, not evaluated NAP2026–2030 submission is documented by UNDP; full attachment retrieval failed, so its detailed heat commitments and legal force were not appraised. [UNDP NAP submission and planning systems account ](https://www.undp.org/indonesia/blog/national-adaptation-plan-nap-2025-2030-pathway-toward-2035-integrated-and-inclusive-approach-accelerate-resilience-against-climate)
- Reported, not evaluated KATALIS-HEAT launched July 2026 and plans evidence synthesis and municipal heat-health planning; launch is not completed adoption. [KATALIS-HEAT launch ](https://www.who.int/japan/news/feature-stories/detail/indonesia-launches-katalis-heat-to-understand-and-address-the-health-impacts-of-extreme-heat)

## Warnings

- Official guidance or data BMKG publishes forecasts, hot-weather explanations and official information through web, social channels and InfoBMKG. Nationwide validated heat-health impact thresholds, automatic cash triggers and offline reach were not verified. [BMKG persistent hot weather explanation ](https://www.bmkg.go.id/berita/utama/suhu-panas-landa-sejumlah-wilayah-indonesia-bmkg-ungkap-penyebab-dan-potensinya-ke-depan)
- Reported, not evaluated Pantau Panas Jakarta combines local sensor information and vulnerability mapping; programme page retains BMKG meteorological warning authority. Accessible rendering supplied no live data, so uptime and clinical validity are not established. [Pantau Panas Jakarta ](https://pantaupanasjakarta.id/id)
- Reported, not evaluated CoCHAP describes developing city thresholds and a tiered system in Medan/Surabaya; historical design cannot be labelled a currently operating warning system. [CoCHAP Indonesia programme account ](https://preparecenter.org/initiative/coastalheat/cochap-indonesia/)

## Rules for heat at work

Official guidance or data Permenaker5/2018 is in force. Articles7–9 establish assessment/control and include ventilation, drinking water and exposure-time controls; Annex provides ISBB/WBGT limits related to workload and work allocation. No universal ambient-temperature stop-work limit or blanket paid-heat-leave entitlement is inferred. [Permenaker 5/2018 current register ](https://jdih.kemnaker.go.id/peraturan/detail/1546/peraturan-menteri-No.-5-tahun-2018) · [Permenaker 5/2018 regulation text ](https://jdih.kemnaker.go.id/asset/data_puu/Permen_5_2018.pdf)

## Cooling and buildings

- Official guidance or data Permen PUPR21/2021 covers green-building performance assessment; verify building-class scope and enforcement before applying it to a specific property. [National green-building assessment regulation ](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/217002/permen-pupr-no-21-tahun-2021)
- Official guidance or data ESDM lists energy management, government energy conservation, appliance labels and envelope/HVAC standards; AC procurement would ideally account for amendment134.K/EK.07/DJE/2023, not rely solely on103/2021. [ESDM conservation regulation and standards catalogue ](https://simebtke.esdm.go.id/sinergi/program_konservasi_energi/detail/1/regulasi-dan-standar-konservasi-energi) · [AC standards amendment 134.K/EK.07/DJE/2023 ](https://simebtke.esdm.go.id/sinergi/assets/content/20250310092139_134K_SKEM_dan_LTHE_AC_Perubahan.pdf)
- Official guidance or data Perpres129/2022 approves Kigali amendment domestically; actual refrigerant, safety training and recovery arrangements require project verification. [Kigali approval Presidential Regulation 129/2022 ](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/230496/perpres-no-129-tahun-2022)
- Official guidance or data Jakarta60/2022 was repealed by44/2023, which refers green-building implementation to national rules. [Jakarta repeal of 2022 green-building regulation ](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/274214/pergub-prov-dki-jakarta-no-44-tahun-2023)

## Cooling design ideas (proposed)

- Design concept **Option:** External shade and solar-reflective roof measures **Suits:** Both hot-dry and hot-humid, conditional on structure and detailing **Basis:** Researcher transfer assessment; local deployment reported, no universal effect promised. [Cool Roofs Indonesia university implementation account ](https://dia.upi.edu/upi-lecturer-wins-2-million-dollar-international-competition/) **Checks:**
- Roof access
- Durability
- Fire/structure
- Tenure
- Installed cost and upkeep

- Design concept **Option:** Ventilation and efficient active cooling **Suits:** Both, with local air conditions, humidity, safety and health requirements assessed **Basis:** Context-specific design proposal, not a validated safety threshold. [Indonesia National Cooling Action Plan ](https://repository.unescap.org/items/92f8d947-0076-4c72-be50-fd9e3a472ec1/full) · [Permenaker 5/2018 regulation text ](https://jdih.kemnaker.go.id/asset/data_puu/Permen_5_2018.pdf) · [ESDM conservation regulation and standards catalogue ](https://simebtke.esdm.go.id/sinergi/program_konservasi_energi/detail/1/regulasi-dan-standar-konservasi-energi) **Checks:**
- Outside/inside conditions
- Power and affordability
- Clinical requirements
- Refrigerant/service safety
- Outage fallback

- Design concept **Option:** Evaporative/misting or water-dependent greening **Suits:** Not prescribed; verify seasonal humidity, water and exposure conditions first **Basis:** Assessment proposal; no measured Indonesian benefit claimed. [Jakarta official multi-agency heat response ](https://www.jakarta.go.id/siaran-pers/6028-SP-HMS-10-2025) **Checks:**
- Humidity
- Water/drainage
- Actual people-level benefit
- Maintenance
- Accessibility and displacement

[Build Change/KOMIDA housing adaptation ](https://buildchange.org/extreme-heat-climate-resilient-housing-for-low-income-women-in-indonesia) **Access:** KOMIDA member/borrower route; current underwriting, tenant access and interest terms require verification.
- **PEEB Cool** Official guidance or data Indonesia is in programme scope; AFD/Proparco implement investment facilities and GIZ enabling work with GCF co-finance. [PEEB Cool implementation structure ](https://india.afd.fr/en/resources/programme-energy-efficiency-building-peeb-cool) **Access:** Project preparation through implementing institutions; no direct open small-NGO grant verified.

[Indonesia ESCO and on-bill-finance readiness study ](https://www.international-climate-initiative.com/fileadmin/iki/Dokumente/Publikationen/Projekte/23_I_494/20260311_ESCO-Market-Assessment-and-On-Bill-Financing-Readiness-Study-Indonesia_Country_Report-1.pdf) **Access:** Contract- and project-specific; nationwide operational on-bill route not verified.

## Costing: next step

Obtain date-specific installed IDR quotations, separate energy/water/staff/maintenance/replacement, then use a dated published FX rate. Neither programme size nor loan principal is a universal protection cost.

## Gaps in what we found

- No harmonised nationwide city heat-health exposure ranking verified.
- No complete, legally adopted, funded city HAP document retrieved for the seven snapshots; Surabaya drafting is reported separately.
- Comparable indoor/night exposure, health outcomes, worker income and actual budget execution remain incomplete in collected evidence.
- Grant/loan eligibility, recurrent maintenance and tenant/disability/migrant access require programme-level verification.

## Ways to help (proposed)

- Contribute local evidence and delivery protocols to the KATALIS-HEAT partners, subject to invitation.
- Support PMI/city programmes in Medan and Surabaya with warning-to-action tests and inclusion audits.
- Connect low-income housing technical assistance with grants for households unsuitable for debt.
- Prepare health-facility/school cooling portfolios with maintenance and power-failure budgets.

## Known, inferred, uncertain

**Known:** Official heat-health activity, labour rules, cooling policies and implementation examples exist. **Inferred:** Their connection offers a place-based delivery opportunity. **Uncertain:** National comparability, enforceability of specific heat actions, local budget execution and health effectiveness.
