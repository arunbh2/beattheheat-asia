    Country snapshot

    Nepal

    Heat, the plans and rules that shape protection, the organisations at work, and where a shared effort could help. Researched in October 2026.

    How to read this. Facts link to their sources; anything marked proposed or inferred is the researchers’ suggestion, not a commitment by anyone named. Gaps are gaps in what we found, not faults.

    Heat context

    Climate: Official guidance or data Terai lowland heat includes seasonally drier pre-monsoon exposure and humid monsoon conditions. Climate suitability would be worth assessing at the actual place, season and task. DHM post-monsoon precipitation and temperature summary, 2024 (opens in a new tab) ↗ · Dhangadhi Heat Action Plan (opens in a new tab) ↗ · RAIN heat early-action implementation account (opens in a new tab) ↗ Trends: Reported, not evaluated National synthesis describes intensifying heat concern in the Terai and increasing exposure elsewhere. DHM2024 post-monsoon report documents above-normal southern minimum temperatures; this seasonal observation is not a long-term trend estimate. Living with Heat in Nepal — original publisher overview (opens in a new tab) ↗ · DHM post-monsoon precipitation and temperature summary, 2024 (opens in a new tab) ↗ Night heat: Official guidance or data Dhangadhi plan describes reduced night-time recovery in monsoon months. Comparable occupied-bedroom night records across all eight cities not verified. Dhangadhi Heat Action Plan (opens in a new tab) ↗ Measurement caution: Keep dry-bulb air temperature, Heat Index, WBGT, land-surface and occupied-room temperature separate. No national exposure rank assigned.

    Official heat plans

    • Official guidance or data NDRRMA2024 plan sets responsible/supporting agencies for warnings, water, health, schools, electricity and workplaces. NDRRMA national heatwave action plan 2024 (opens in a new tab) ↗
    • Official guidance or data An additional preparedness/early-action/rapid-response heat plan is catalogued as2025 and references BS2082; selected Nepali text reviewed, with extraction limits. NDRRMA preparedness, early action and rapid response heat plan (opens in a new tab) ↗
    • Official guidance or data Government Bara office lists a heat-and-drought preparedness/early-action/rapid-response plan BS2083. CEEW/GHHIN describes the2026 national plan and anticipatory-action framework. Current full national text, signed adoption decision, annexes and finance rules not fully retrieved. Bara District Administration Office listing of heat-and-drought plan BS2083 (opens in a new tab) ↗ · Living with Heat in Nepal — report (opens in a new tab) ↗

Biratnagar/Siddharthanagar planning is reported by NRCS, but final municipal texts and appropriation decisions not retrieved. Nepalgunj Heat Action Plan (opens in a new tab) ↗ · Dhangadhi Heat Action Plan (opens in a new tab) ↗ · NRCS Leading Change Through Heat Action leaflet (opens in a new tab) ↗

    Legal basis

    Official guidance or data DRRM Act2017 expressly includes heat wave under natural disaster and establishes multilevel disaster arrangements and funds. A published HAP does not by itself establish a new offence, entitlement or budget release. Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act2017 (opens in a new tab) ↗

    Warnings

    Documented: Official guidance or data DHM provides heat monitoring/bulletins; municipalities and humanitarian partners connect forecasts with local action. RIMES/DHM held a national heat impact-based forecasting demonstration workshop inJuly2026. DHM heat and cold wave monitoring archive (opens in a new tab) ↗ · DHM/RIMES national heat impact-based forecasting demonstration workshop (opens in a new tab) ↗ Channels: Reported, not evaluated RAIN reports FCHV visits, radio and public announcements in Bajika/Maithili; municipal/Red Cross arrangements include non-smartphone communication. RAIN heat early-action implementation account (opens in a new tab) ↗ · NRCS/municipal anticipatory action in Nepalgunj (opens in a new tab) ↗ Thresholds: Official guidance or data Dhangadhi HAP proposes daily maximum Heat Index40.1°C for two consecutive days or41.6°C for one or more days, derived from1979–2023 ERA5 weather analysis. Mortality gaps/low variance prevented local epidemiological modelling. Confirm version/metric/forecast product and authorisation before operation. Dhangadhi Heat Action Plan (opens in a new tab) ↗ Cash trigger: No universal nationwide heat-triggered social-protection or insurance payout entitlement verified. Limits: National percentile bulletin, local Heat Index proposal and a funding protocol are distinct triggers. No live alert or universally safe temperature supplied.

    Rules for heat at work

    Law: Official guidance or data Labour Act2017 sections68–69 require workplace safety/health policy and employer protections; sections75–77 address reporting and immediate danger procedures. Primary English translation reviewed, not a current legal opinion. Labour Act2017 English translation (opens in a new tab) ↗ Heat directions: Official guidance or data NDRRMA2024 action plan directs factories/workshops to provide protective conditions including ventilation, water and rest. Health-insurance wording is a planning direction, not evidence of parametric payout coverage. NDRRMA national heatwave action plan 2024 (opens in a new tab) ↗ Heat specific standard: Official guidance or data Department of Labour and Occupational Safety officially lists the workplace maximum heat-level standard BS2082. Existence verified; full technical schedule, measurement conditions and current legal implementation details not independently appraised. Workplace maximum heat-level standard BS2082 — official publication record (opens in a new tab) ↗ Numeric WBGT limit verification: Numerical schedule not verified from primary text; standard existence is verified. Do not infer absence of heat rules or import foreign-country thresholds. Paid rest and coverage: Heat-specific paid-rest entitlement, self-employed coverage, enforcement and current technical schedules require verification. Worker evidence: Measured in a study Original cross-sectional study documents outdoor-worker heat awareness, practices and perceived risks. It is not a measured productivity-loss or clinical intervention study. Outdoor workers heat knowledge, attitudes and practices study (opens in a new tab) ↗

    Cooling and buildings

    • Official guidance or data EU BEEN project and consortium promote climate-responsive design, retrofits, skills and municipal policy. Listed project period endedJanuary2026; current extension and municipality-specific enforceable by-laws not verified. EU Building Energy Efficiency in Nepal project (opens in a new tab) ↗ · BEEN implementer and consortium description (opens in a new tab) ↗
    • Official guidance or data Nepal is listed in the Global Cooling Pledge. Publication of a current national cooling plan or equivalent and binding thermal-performance requirements not verified in this review. Global Cooling Pledge participant register and commitments (opens in a new tab) ↗
    • Official guidance or data UNEP treaty register lists Nepal Kigali ratification6August2025. Verify appliance efficiency, refrigerant safety, technician competency and lifecycle management separately. UNEP Nepal treaty and ozone profile (opens in a new tab) ↗

    Money for protection

    • Public disaster-management funds and municipal sector budgets Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act2017 (opens in a new tab) ↗ Access: Via authorised public counterpart and applicable fund/budget rules; no open NGO entitlement. Limits: Act sections22–23 establish funds. Central fund excludes regular administration; do not assume it pays all permanent core/maintenance costs. Actual local heat allocations/releases not verified.
    • IFRC-DREF heat anticipatory action NRCS/municipal anticipatory action in Nepalgunj (opens in a new tab) ↗ · Living with Heat in Nepal — report (opens in a new tab) ↗ Access: Through NRCS/IFRC approved processes; no direct Common Shade application route verified. Limits: Programme account identifies DREF. sEAP2025NP02 full file inaccessible; exact approval, budget, activation and payment records excluded from verified fields.
    • RAIN RAIN heat early-action implementation account (opens in a new tab) ↗ · FCDO RAIN programme registry (opens in a new tab) ↗ Access: Contribute through existing programme/municipal relationships subject to agreement; not an open call. Limits: Implementation status verified. Programme aims on shock-responsive protection do not prove heat cash transfers were paid.
    • ZCRA-backed heat and education work Practical Action school cooling and heat learning pilot (opens in a new tab) ↗ · Madhesh public-school heat perception study (opens in a new tab) ↗ Access: Existing member/implementer relationships; no unsolicited grant route established. Limits: Do not label alliance a directly accessible pooled fund or equate project activity with evaluated health impact.
    • BEEN EU Building Energy Efficiency in Nepal project (opens in a new tab) ↗ · BEEN implementer and consortium description (opens in a new tab) ↗ Access: Historical project/consortium learning; extension and current finance products not verified. Limits: Finance-product targets are not a verified available loan. Do not prescribe debt for non-revenue public protection.

    Gaps in what we found

    • National all-city comparative exposure ranking not verified.
    • Current national plan and framework full text/adoption/finance annexes incomplete.
    • Comparable indoor/night exposure, health surveillance and worker income outcomes limited in collected evidence.
    • Specific municipal heat budgets, paid rest, current building standards and current finance eligibility require confirmation.

    Known, inferred, uncertain

    Known: Plans, warnings, pilots and research are documented. Inferred: Common Shade could connect seasonal action to lasting public/worker protection. Uncertain: Authority in final current documents, sustained financing, delivered reach and independent effects.
