## Heat context

**Known:** Humid heat and increasing warm nights are documented. Building-code warm-dry zones are not a guarantee of low humidity. **Inferred:** Use location/season/workload-specific assessment and include recovery at home/night. **Uncertain:** Comparable current ward-level exposure, health burden and national city ranking. ## Cooling and buildings

**Costs:** **Reason:** No verified local comparable intervention lifecycle cost with published FX found; no global price substituted. [Urban thermal comfort trends in Sri Lanka ](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00484-022-02328-9) · [Energy Efficiency Building Code of Sri Lanka ](https://www.energy.gov.lk/images/resources/downloads/energy-efficiency-building-code.pdf) · [SLSEA introducing standards ](https://www.energy.gov.lk/en/energy-management/introducing-standards) · [National Cooling Policy - ministry draft ](https://env.gov.lk/web/images/news/NCP_final_draft.pdf) · [UNEP Ozone Secretariat - Sri Lanka country profile ](https://ozone.unep.org/countries/profile/lka) · [UNDP Digital Cooling Project ](https://www.undp.org/srilanka/projects/digital-monitoring-management-enhance-energy-efficiency) **Approach:** Proposed passive-first design assessment combined with appropriate active cooling and reliable operations. **Climate suitability:** Shade/envelope/ventilation design to match actual humidity, night air, security and air quality; code warm-dry designation is not a psychrometric equipment rule. No universal evaporative or fan-use prescription supplied. **Cooling trap:** Loop cooling: consider peak electricity, waste heat, refrigerants, affordable operation and power-cut fallback alongside access. **Affordability:** Income share, tariff-specific bills, landlord permission and maintenance burden not verified; propose measurement before procurement. **Policy cautions:** Standard existence, label programme, code scope, gazette enforceability, product availability and compliance are separate facts.

## Fairness and maladaptation risks

**Assessment questions:**
- Can a worker rest without lost income or retaliation?
- Are women, pregnant people, older people and people with disabilities able to reach and use services?
- Who owns roofs/land and can cooling improvements raise rents or displace residents?
- Will shade or tree cover block needed airflow, and who pays for water/upkeep?
- Are fans/AC usable during outages, with safe wiring and affordable bills?
- Do sensors collect only necessary data, with consent and a real protective action?

**Interpretation:** Researcher design questions, not claims these harms occurred in every selected city.
