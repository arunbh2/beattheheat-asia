    Country snapshot

    Sri Lanka

    Heat, the plans and rules that shape protection, the organisations at work, and where a shared effort could help. Researched in October 2026.

    How to read this. Facts link to their sources; anything marked proposed or inferred is the researchers’ suggestion, not a commitment by anyone named. Gaps are gaps in what we found, not faults.

    Heat context

    Known: Humid heat and increasing warm nights are documented. Building-code warm-dry zones are not a guarantee of low humidity. Inferred: Use location/season/workload-specific assessment and include recovery at home/night. Uncertain: Comparable current ward-level exposure, health burden and national city ranking.     Cooling and buildings

    Costs: Reason: No verified local comparable intervention lifecycle cost with published FX found; no global price substituted. No universal evaporative or fan-use prescription supplied. Cooling trap: Loop cooling: consider peak electricity, waste heat, refrigerants, affordable operation and power-cut fallback alongside access. Affordability: Income share, tariff-specific bills, landlord permission and maintenance burden not verified; propose measurement before procurement. Policy cautions: Standard existence, label programme, code scope, gazette enforceability, product availability and compliance are separate facts.

    Fairness and maladaptation risks

    Assessment questions:
    • Can a worker rest without lost income or retaliation?
    • Are women, pregnant people, older people and people with disabilities able to reach and use services?
    • Who owns roofs/land and can cooling improvements raise rents or displace residents?
    • Will shade or tree cover block needed airflow, and who pays for water/upkeep?
    • Are fans/AC usable during outages, with safe wiring and affordable bills?
    • Do sensors collect only necessary data, with consent and a real protective action?
     Interpretation: Researcher design questions, not claims these harms occurred in every selected city.     Colombo

    Heat context: Humid lowland/coastal urban heat; night recovery matters. National station trends and modelled evening/night exposures are distinct.

    Official heat plan: Status note: No signed/current city-specific HAP retrieved in this review; absence from collected evidence is not proof none exists.

    Money for protection: Note: National/project routes do not establish municipal eligibility, expenditure or ward coverage.

    A place to start (proposed): Co-design market/street-worker protection with reliable water/toilets, shade, rest without income loss, consent-based monitoring and night/home follow-up.
