# Bangladesh

## Heat context

**Known:** Western/southwestern daytime hotspots coexist with humid urban and night exposure. **Inferred:** Combine work, housing and overnight recovery with warning-to-action design. **Uncertain:** Comparable ward-level exposure, health burden and nationally representative indoor monitoring. [Are hotspots and frequencies of heat waves changing over time? ](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0300070) · [Diurnal asymmetry in heat stress intensification across Bangladesh, 1985–2024 ](https://journals.plos.org/climate/article?id=10.1371/journal.pclm.0000848) · [Bangladesh faces health and economic risks from rising temperature - report release ](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/09/16/bangladesh-faces-health-and-economic-risks-from-rising-temperature-world-bank)
