Country snapshot

India

Heat, the plans and rules that shape protection, the organisations at work, and where a shared effort could help. Researched in October 2026.

How to read this. Facts link to their sources; anything marked proposed or inferred is the researchers’ suggestion, not a commitment by anyone named. Gaps are gaps in what we found, not faults.

Heat context

Summary: India spans hot-dry, composite and hot-humid contexts. District composite risk, daytime weather alerts, humid heat, night heat and indoor exposure are different measures. Sources: CEEW: How Extreme Heat Is Impacting India (opens in a new tab) ↗ · IMD heat-wave FAQ (opens in a new tab) ↗

Official plans and authority

Description: National guidance, state/district/local HAPs and health-sector plans operate through different institutional/legal routes. Publication alone is not a separately enforceable heat entitlement. Sources: NCDC advisory for State Health Departments, summer 2026 (opens in a new tab) ↗ · Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions (Central) Rules 2026 (opens in a new tab) ↗ · MHA SDMF/NDMF operational guidelines, 2026–31 (opens in a new tab) ↗ · Delhi Heat Action Plan 2025 (opens in a new tab) ↗ · Tamil Nadu State Heat Action Plan 2026 (opens in a new tab) ↗ · Chennai City Heat Action Plan 2025 (opens in a new tab) ↗ Current rules check: Central OSH Rules 2026 are notified, not a draft. Check appropriate-government, sector and state implementation.

Warning system

Sources: IMD heat-wave FAQ (opens in a new tab) ↗ · NCDC advisory for State Health Departments, summer 2026 (opens in a new tab) ↗ Description: IMD meteorological guidance informs health and local response. Separate forecast, action and financial payout triggers; household receipt not verified.

Known, inferred, uncertain

Known: Primary plans, guidance and selected studies exist as documented. Inferred: Place-based coordination and project preparation could connect currently separate protection functions. Uncertain: A comparable national city exposure ranking, current ward delivery, lifecycle costs, complete heat mortality, enforcement and disbursement.
