How to use this site

Find your
way in.

Common Shade maps how extreme heat harms health, income and homes across Asia, and what can be done about it. It is large on purpose. You do not need all of it: answer two questions and follow a short path.

Your path

The site in four parts

UnderstandThe web of consequences, the vicious cycles, a day in a heatwave, today’s heat, twelve cities, past heatwaves and who heat hits hardest.
ActPractical actions by setting, cooling options, paying for protection, who holds the levers, why action stalls and who is already at work.
ToolsA week in the heat, Plan a response and Make it work together. Each opens in its own tab, so the map stays where you left it.
ReferenceHow the map works, a plain-language glossary, every link as text, and printable one-pagers for officials, partners and funders.

Good to know

  • What you type stays in your browser. Nothing is sent to us, and nothing needs an account.
  • The tools share one plan: actions added anywhere on the map appear in Plan a response.
  • Every person in the exercises is a composite, and every place in the simulation is hypothetical.
  • Facts link to their sources. Live readings and warning bands are estimates, never official warnings.
  • Every section also has a plain-text page, and many have a printable one-pager.
