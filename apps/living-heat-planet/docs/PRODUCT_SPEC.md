# Product specification: Release 1 as built

**North star:** make heat visible, make risk intelligible, make action possible, without implying that unvalidated scores or institutional facts are real.

## Journey
Globe → search or tap a capital → the globe turns it to face you → dossier: **Now** (modelled current conditions) and **Next 72 h** (hourly forecast) → **Patterns, People, Preparedness, Solutions, Investment** show their evidence state → compare up to four capitals → Methods & data, and "Why temperature is not risk".

## Release 1 capabilities
1. Particle Earth from Natural Earth land: 69,261 points (desktop), 24,829 (phones and low-memory devices).
2. 207 capital lights coloured by live modelled air temperature, or apparent temperature by toggle.
3. Idle spin, drag with inertia, pinch and wheel zoom, quaternion focus, reset, pause, reduced motion.
4. Search by city or country, accent- and case-insensitive, keyboard operable.
5. Dossier with source, model time, retrieval time, grid cell and precision notes; "Official warning status not integrated".
6. 72-hour hourly chart in the city's time zone with night shading, an accessible table and daily max/min.
7. Compare up to four capitals: same-time table and a shared-axis 72-hour chart, labelled "not a ranking of heat risk".
8. °C/°F (display only), Methods & data, explainer, 2D map and table fallback without WebGL.
9. Mobile drawer, desktop panel and an embed mode.

## Explicitly not in Release 1
Historical baselines and anomalies, population or built-up layers, official warnings, heat action plans, interventions, financing records, any composite risk score. Their routes and contracts exist and return `not_integrated`.
