    Who holds it up

    For protection to reach people, someone has to permit it, pay for it, deliver it and check it. Choose an action to see who could hold each part, and where we have not yet found anyone.

    The organisations shown are those whose published work relates to each action. Inclusion does not imply partnership, affiliation or endorsement, and nothing here judges how well anyone does their part.

    How to read this

    • Kinds of body: our readingWhich kinds of body usually permit, pay, deliver and check is our reading of the solutions library and worked plans. Real arrangements differ from place to place.
    • Named organisations: published workEach organisation is placed by the kind of work it publishes, and links to it. A gap means we have not found anyone yet, not that nobody is doing it.
    • No scores, no comparisonsCounts describe our research, not anyone’s performance. Organisations are never ranked against each other.
    • Help fill the gapsIf you hold one of these parts, or know who does, please tell us and we will add it with credit.
