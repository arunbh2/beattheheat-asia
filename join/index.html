    Help build Common Shade

    Come as you are.
    Bring what you know.

    Common Shade is being built by people who work on extreme heat in many different ways: in clinics and city offices, unions and community groups, laboratories, businesses and foundations, across South and Southeast Asia. There is room here for whatever you bring, and for however much time you have.

    Find your way in ↓ Or just keep me updated

    No membership, no fees, no commitment. Individuals are as welcome as organisations.

    Why a collaborative

    Extreme heat affects health, work, income, homes, food, water and essential services. These impacts connect, and our responses can too.

    • A shaded workplace needs permission, maintenance and water.
    • A heat warning needs someone able to act on it.
    • Taking a break needs to be possible without losing the income a household depends on.

    No single organisation holds all of these. Common Shade is a place to see the connections, find practical actions, and work on protection that lasts, together. It is early, and it will be shaped by the people who join it.

    No surprises

    What happens after you write

    1. 1You tell us a littleWho you are, what you work on, and what you might like to explore. Share only what you are comfortable sharing.
    2. 2A person reads itSomeone on the coordinating team reads every response. No automated replies, no sales.
    3. 3We write back when there is something to explorePerhaps people working on the same thing, a page that could help, or a conversation. We will not add you to any list you did not ask for.
    4. 4You decide what comes nextTaking part never commits you to a project, a fee or a formal partnership, and you can step back at any time.

    What we might do together

    We would like to explore

    • Improving the platform’s evidence, tools and practical usefulness.
    • Bringing community knowledge and worker experience into planning.
    • Testing approaches in real places, and sharing what works, what does not and why.
    • Connecting organisations with complementary capabilities.
    • Developing joint initiatives and funding proposals.
    • Helping effective protection become part of everyday services, budgets and institutional practice.

    The question connecting this work is simple:

    What would make it easier for people and institutions to protect one another from extreme heat?

    What we hold ourselves to

    Our promises to you

    • No strings attachedExpressing interest does not commit you to a project or any financial contribution, and it does not create formal membership or partnership.
    • Every kind of knowledge countsWhat people living and working with heat know is evidence too. Your contribution is credited on the site only if you would like it to be.
    • Shaped togetherThe collaborative’s priorities, ways of working and governance are still being developed, with the people and organisations involved.
    • Open to correctionWe will sometimes get things wrong. When we do, we say sorry and correct it in the open. See what we have corrected.
    • Your answers stay with usThey are used only to reply to you about the collaborative, and never sold or passed on. How your answers are used.

    When you are ready

    Tell us a little about yourself

    It takes a few minutes. Share as much or as little as you like; a rough idea is a good start.

    Only want to stay informed? Choose “Staying informed” as your contribution. Want to share or build something? Describe it in the last question.

    The registration form needs JavaScript, and opens in Microsoft Forms.

    How your answers are used

    The form runs on Microsoft Forms, so your answers are stored by Microsoft on behalf of the team coordinating the Common Shade collaborative, who read and manage them. We use them only to reply to you about the collaborative. We send general updates only if you tick the box that asks for them. We do not sell your answers or pass them to anyone else.
