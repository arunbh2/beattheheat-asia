Help build Common Shade

Different entry points.
A shared commitment
to heat resilience.

Shade is something we hold up together.

Common Shade is an emerging collaborative for protection from extreme heat in South and Southeast Asia. Both the platform and the collaboration will evolve.

Express your interest ↓ Or just keep me updated

Why a collaborative

Extreme heat affects health, work, income, homes, food, water and essential services. These impacts connect, and so must our responses.

  • A shaded workplace needs permission, maintenance and water.
  • A heat warning needs someone able to act on it.
  • Taking a break needs to be possible without losing the income a household depends on.

Common Shade is being developed to help people understand these connections, identify practical actions and work together on protection that lasts. We are inviting people and organisations to help shape what comes next, with a particular focus on South and Southeast Asia.

There is more than one way to contribute

Your entry point

You might work with communities, represent workers, deliver public services, conduct research, develop technology, influence policy or provide funding.

  • Health and careProtecting people and preparing health and care systems.
  • Workers and livelihoodsSafer work, worker voice and income security.
  • Homes and coolingAffordable, accessible cooling that can be maintained.
  • Places and essential servicesNeighbourhoods, schools, farms, water, energy and public spaces.
  • Warnings and financial protectionConnecting information with timely action and appropriate support.
  • Institutions and financeResponsibilities, operating budgets, public policy and funding.
  • Shared knowledge and toolsEvidence, local experience, translation, data and learning.

You do not need to work only on heat. Experience with one part of the problem can help others see what is missing.

What could we do together?

We want to explore

  • Improving the platform’s evidence, tools and practical usefulness.
  • Bringing community knowledge and worker experience into planning.
  • Testing approaches in real places, and sharing what works, what fails and why.
  • Connecting organisations with complementary capabilities.
  • Developing joint initiatives and funding proposals.
  • Helping effective protection become part of everyday services, budgets and institutional practice.

The question connecting this work is simple:

What would make it easier for people and institutions to protect one another from extreme heat?

Help shape the collaboration

Tell us what you work on, what you could bring and what you would like to explore.

This is an emerging collaborative. Its priorities, ways of working and governance are still being developed. We want them to be shaped by the people and organisations involved, including those living and working with heat every day.

No strings attached. Expressing interest does not commit you to a project or to any financial contribution, and it does not create formal membership or partnership. Individuals are as welcome as organisations.

Only want to stay informed? Use the same form and choose “Staying informed” as your contribution. Already have a specific idea? Describe it in the last question.

The registration form needs JavaScript, and opens in Microsoft Forms.

How your answers are used

The form runs on Microsoft Forms, so your answers are stored by Microsoft on behalf of the team coordinating the Common Shade collaborative, who read and manage them. We use them only to reply to you about the collaborative. We send general updates only if you tick the box that asks for them. We do not sell your answers or pass them to anyone else.
