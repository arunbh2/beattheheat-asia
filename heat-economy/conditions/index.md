# Questions to ask
before you buy, fund or start.

For city officers, employers, worker and community organisations, funders, investors and founders: twenty-four questions, the rules and ways of paying that have helped elsewhere, the common obstacles, and what each kind of organisation could do.

## Does it really protect people here?

- **Who exactly will this protect, and from what kind of heat?**Ask for the people, the place and the evidence. Selling a product is not the same as protecting someone.
- **Will it work here, in our dry heat, humid heat and hot nights?**Ask for tests in conditions like yours, with people inside the building.
- **Will it make someone else hotter?**Air conditioners and some roofs push heat into streets and neighbouring homes.
- **Are the claims measured, estimated or marketing?**Ask how a result was measured, and what it was compared with.
- **Will it still work as heat, floods and water shortages grow?**Ask how long it lasts and what it needs to keep working.

## Is it fair?

- **Can families on the lowest incomes use it without giving up food, school or medicine?**Count the full cost: buying, running, repairs and travel. If they can’t, what is the free or subsidised option?
- **Who is left out because they rent, or lack a meter, ID, bank account or phone?**Make sure there is another way in for them.
- **Can workers stop to rest without losing pay or their job?**Equipment helps only if rest is paid and safe to take, including for contract workers.
- **Could it push up rents or move residents and vendors out?**Involve tenants and vendors early, and agree protections in writing.
- **Does it add unpaid work, often for women?**Pay for upkeep roles, and check opening hours, safety and toilets.
- **Can older and disabled people reach it, use it and have a say?**Check access, transport, communication and help on site, with them.
- **Who sees people’s health, location or repayment data?**Collect only what is needed, and keep a way to use the service without sharing data.

## Who keeps it working?

- **Who looks after it after the pilot, and with what budget?**Name the owner, the repair plan and who pays each year.
- **What happens when the power or phone network fails?**Plan a backup for the hottest days, and keep essential services running first.
- **What happens to electricity demand at the hottest hour?**Start with shade and design, which need no power, before adding machines.
- **Does it compete with drinking water?**Check where the water comes from in a drought.
- **Which cooling gas does it use, and who handles leaks and disposal?**Use trained technicians and safe gases.
- **Where do old fans, batteries and gases go?**Agree safe collection and recycling from the start.
- **Can the product, the installer and the claims be checked independently?**Look for official tests or registration, a warranty, and a way to get faults fixed.
- **Who can people complain to, and who decides to fix, change or stop it?**Agree this before starting, and review it after each hot season.

## Who pays, and who carries the risk?

- **What happens after a missed payment or a bad season?**Check the total cost, what happens to the equipment, and the options in hard times.
- **Could people lose income and still get no payout, or get it too late?**Ask how payouts are triggered, and what help exists if they don’t come.
- **Who receives the subsidy, and what extra protection does it buy?**Make sure the benefit reaches the people using it.
- **If payment depends on results, could it reward the wrong thing?**Pay for working services people can reach, not for units shipped or longer hours.

## Rules that have helped create demand

Examples from Asia. A rule on paper helps most when it is checked and followed.

- India: fans and many air conditioners must carry an energy label, which helps buyers choose efficient models. [BEE standards and labelling current official listing ](https://www.beeindia.gov.in/show_content.php?lang=1&level=1&lid=322&ls_id=198)
- Singapore: air conditioners must meet a higher minimum efficiency from April 2025. [Singapore NEA MELS/MEPS history and current requirements ](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/climate-change-energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency/household-sector/about-mandatory-energy-labelling-and-minimum-energy-performance-standards)
- Telangana, India: a cool-roof policy that applies to certain new buildings. [Telangana Cool Roof Policy 2023–2028 ](https://telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Telangana-Cool-Roof-Policy-2023-2028.pdf) · [Telangana building-permission orders ](https://tgbpass.telangana.gov.in/others/rules)
- India: a building code for homes on keeping heat out and letting air through, where states adopt it. [BEE residential building code official page ](https://beeindia.gov.in/show_content.php?lang=1&level=2&lid=328&ls_id=202)
- Singapore: rules for outdoor work in the heat, with four more practices becoming mandatory on 1 December 2026. [MOM current outdoor heat measures ](https://www.mom.gov.sg/heat-stress-measures-for-outdoor-work) · [Singapore MOM announced heat-framework enhancements ](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/press-releases/2026/0828-enhancements-to-heat-stress-framework)
- India: a public company bought efficient fans and air conditioners in bulk and worked with private energy-service firms. [EESL building energy ESCO partnerships 2021 ](https://eeslindia.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Press%20Release%20-%20EESL%20is%20looking%20to%20partner%20with%20private%20sector%20ESCOs%20to%20scale%20up%20its%20BEEP%5B42%5D.pdf)
- India: a national cooling plan covering buildings, cold storage, servicing and cooling gases. [India Ozone Cell cooling action plan and implementation documents ](https://ozonecell.nic.in/home-page/india-cooling-action-plan/)
- Cambodia: a national cooling plan. [Cambodia National Cooling Action Plan ](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/cam219294.pdf)
- India: national disaster-mitigation funds can now pay for heatwave projects; upkeep needs its own budget. [MHA mitigation fund operational guidelines 2026–2031 ](https://www.mha.gov.in/files/policies_guidelines_document/OperationalGuidelinesforAdministrationoftheStateDisasterMitigationFundandNationalDisasterMitigationFundbasedontherecommendationsofSixteenthFinanceCommission2026-27to2030-31.pdf)
- Bangkok: heat planning and public warnings. [Thailand NESDC Bangkok heat action planning ](https://sdgs.nesdc.go.th/en/urban-heat-transforming-bangkok-into-a-cooler-city-for-sustainable-heat-management/) · [World Bank Bangkok heat study ](https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/thailand/publication/shaping-a-cooler-bangkok-tackling-urban-heat-for-a-more-livable-city)
- ASEAN: guidance that helps lenders and investors recognise projects that protect people from climate risks, including heat. [ASEAN mARs Guide Version 1 official release ](https://theacmf.org/initiatives/sustainable-finance/mitigation-co-benefit-and-adaptation-for-resilience-mars-guide-version-1) · [ASEAN mARs Guide Version 1 ](https://acmfcms.assistasia.org/uploads/FOR_UPLOADING_Phase_2_m_A_Rs_Guide_c2cb0ec348.pdf)

## Ways customers can pay

Each can lower the cost of getting started. Check the total cost, and what happens in a bad month, before choosing one.

- **Pay as you go.** Pay in small instalments, and own the equipment at the end. [Devidayal Solar PAYG finance ](https://ddsolar.in/paygo-finance/)
- **Repay through the electricity bill.** The cost of an efficient fan is added, in small amounts, to monthly bills. [Allied Bank fan replacement programme terms ](https://www.abl.com/personal/loans/prime-minister-fan-replacement-program/) · [State Bank Pakistan fan programme circular ](https://www.sbp.org.pk/circulars/shsfd-circular-no-01-of-2026)
- **Pay for cooling, not the machine.** A provider owns and maintains the equipment and charges for the cooling or storage used. [Kaer cooling services ](https://www.kaer.com/services) · [ColdHubs service model ](https://coldhubs.com/our-solution/)
- **Small loans for roofs.** Microfinance loans for roof improvements that keep homes cooler. [MHT gender and poverty barriers to climate finance 2023 ](https://www.mahilahousingtrust.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Gender-and-Poverty-related-Barriers-of-the-Urban-Poor-to-access-Climate-and-Disaster-Risk-Finance-and-Insurance-Evidence-from-the-Mahila-Housing-Trust-in-India.pdf) · [CODI Baan Mankong ](https://en.codi.or.th/baan-mankong-city-wide-upgrading/)
- **Insurance with a safety net.** Insurance combined with savings and emergency support, so people aren’t left with nothing if the insurance doesn’t pay. [CGAP SEWA climate-health lessons ](https://www.cgap.org/research/publication/confronting-climate-and-health-nexus-lessons-self-employed-womens-association)
- **Loan guarantees.** A guarantee makes banks more willing to lend to cooling suppliers or their customers. [DFC Ecozen public information summary ](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/9000116166.pdf) · [State Bank Pakistan fan programme circular ](https://www.sbp.org.pk/circulars/shsfd-circular-no-01-of-2026)
- **Investors who take the first loss.** Patient investors agree to absorb the first losses, which makes it easier for others to invest. [SEACEF II first close and first-loss investors ](https://www.climecap.com/post/clime-capital-hits-first-close-of-second-southeast-asian-clean-energy-fund-at-127m) · [State Bank Pakistan fan programme circular ](https://www.sbp.org.pk/circulars/shsfd-circular-no-01-of-2026)
- **Paying for results.** Suppliers are paid for each efficient appliance that reaches a user and works. [CLASP Global LEAP RBF monitoring and verification ](https://www.clasp.ngo/rfps/global-leaprbf-appliance-monitoring-verification/) · [CLASP Bangladesh retailer field study 2019 ](https://www.clasp.ngo/research/all/the-appliance-market-in-bangladesh/)

## Common obstacles

- Many families can’t afford the upfront cost, or the running costs. [IFC/UNEP Cooler Finance original report ](https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2024/cooler-finance-report.pdf) · [MHT gender and poverty barriers to climate finance 2023 ](https://www.mahilahousingtrust.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Gender-and-Poverty-related-Barriers-of-the-Urban-Poor-to-access-Climate-and-Disaster-Risk-Finance-and-Insurance-Evidence-from-the-Mahila-Housing-Trust-in-India.pdf)
- The person who pays is often not the one who benefits: landlords and tenants, employers and workers. [IFC/UNEP Cooler Finance original report ](https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2024/cooler-finance-report.pdf) · [MHT gender and poverty barriers to climate finance 2023 ](https://www.mahilahousingtrust.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Gender-and-Poverty-related-Barriers-of-the-Urban-Poor-to-access-Climate-and-Disaster-Risk-Finance-and-Insurance-Evidence-from-the-Mahila-Housing-Trust-in-India.pdf)
- Renters, and people without a meter or documents, often can’t qualify. [MHT gender and poverty barriers to climate finance 2023 ](https://www.mahilahousingtrust.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Gender-and-Poverty-related-Barriers-of-the-Urban-Poor-to-access-Climate-and-Disaster-Risk-Finance-and-Insurance-Evidence-from-the-Mahila-Housing-Trust-in-India.pdf) · [Allied Bank fan replacement programme terms ](https://www.abl.com/personal/loans/prime-minister-fan-replacement-program/)
- Rules only work with enough inspectors and follow-up. [IFC/UNEP Cooler Finance original report ](https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2024/cooler-finance-report.pdf) · [MOM current outdoor heat measures ](https://www.mom.gov.sg/heat-stress-measures-for-outdoor-work)
- Poor-quality or counterfeit products and cooling gases are hard to spot. [UNEP South Asia servicing and enforcement network ](https://www.unep.org/ozonaction/south-asia) · [UNEP regional competency certification for RAC servicing ](https://www.unep.org/ozonaction/news/news/unep-builds-regional-expertise-competency-based-assessment-good-servicing-practices-rac)
- Too few trained installers, spare parts and repair services. [IFC/UNEP Cooler Finance original report ](https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2024/cooler-finance-report.pdf) · [UNEP regional competency certification for RAC servicing ](https://www.unep.org/ozonaction/news/news/unep-builds-regional-expertise-competency-based-assessment-good-servicing-practices-rac)
- Little shared information on who is exposed, what heat costs and what works. [IFC/UNEP Cooler Finance original report ](https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2024/cooler-finance-report.pdf) · [CGAP SEWA climate-health lessons ](https://www.cgap.org/research/publication/confronting-climate-and-health-nexus-lessons-self-employed-womens-association)
- Loans in foreign currency are risky when customers pay in local currency. [DFC Ecozen public information summary ](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/media/documents/9000116166.pdf)
- Contracts are small, and many pilots stop when the first grant ends. [IFC/UNEP Cooler Finance original report ](https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2024/cooler-finance-report.pdf) · [TechEmerge results and lessons ](https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2026/tech-emerge-results-and-lessons.pdf)
- Unreliable power or water, or a product that doesn’t suit the climate. [IFC/UNEP Cooler Finance original report ](https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2024/cooler-finance-report.pdf) · [PNNL evaporative cooling suitability ](https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/evaporative-cooling-systems?existing_homes=602)
- Money to install is easier to find than money to run and repair. [MHA mitigation fund operational guidelines 2026–2031 ](https://www.mha.gov.in/files/policies_guidelines_document/OperationalGuidelinesforAdministrationoftheStateDisasterMitigationFundandNationalDisasterMitigationFundbasedontherecommendationsofSixteenthFinanceCommission2026-27to2030-31.pdf)
- Insurance payouts, loans and support don’t always arrive together, when they are needed. [CGAP SEWA climate-health lessons ](https://www.cgap.org/research/publication/confronting-climate-and-health-nexus-lessons-self-employed-womens-association) · [Devidayal Solar PAYG finance ](https://ddsolar.in/paygo-finance/)

## What each kind of organisation could do

Suggestions to discuss, not commitments.

### National governments

- Set clear rules for products, buildings and heat at work, and check they are followed.
- Fund public protection, and make clear which budgets can pay for heat work, including upkeep.

### City and local governments

- Bundle needs across homes, schools, markets and clinics into larger, costed purchases that suppliers can bid for.
- Approve the upkeep budget, and protections for tenants and vendors, before installing anything.

### Health authorities

- Set out what a heat-ready clinic needs: cooling, staff, supplies and referral.
- Track heat illness while protecting patient data, and check what actually helps.

### Weather and climate services

- Give local warnings people can act on, through radio, SMS and other offline channels too.
- Help others tell apart a weather warning, a trigger to act at work and an insurance payout trigger.

### Employers and businesses

- Buy protection as a package: shade, water, cooling, safe schedules and paid rest.
- Stay responsible for all workers, including contract workers, and pay for upkeep.

### Worker organisations and unions

- Shape protection with workers: rest, pay, benefit rules and how to complain.
- Buy together or run shared funds, without taking on duties that belong to employers or government.

### Utilities and energy regulators

- Help households use efficient cooling, and keep power to clinics, water pumps and shelters first.
- Make sure repayment through bills doesn’t leave anyone cut off or shut out.

### Water authorities

- Plan enough clean water for drinking, cooling, trees and public facilities.
- Coordinate backup pumping and drought rules with health services and communities.

### Farmers’ organisations and agriculture agencies

- Design crop, livestock, farm-worker and cold-storage services with farmers.
- Keep fees and finance fair, and protect water rights and tenant farmers.

### Schools and education authorities

- Buy together for safe classrooms, drinking water, shade and equipment that can be maintained.
- Keep children learning and safe when schools change hours or close.

### Banks, insurers and financial regulators

- Keep loans, public help and insurance clearly separate, and protect borrowers.
- Be open about payout triggers, timing, data use and what subsidies really cover.

### Development banks, donors and climate funds

- Fund preparation, local skills, evidence and public access, as well as projects that can repay.
- Link support to fair delivery and a real upkeep plan, and be open about terms.

### Community and civil society groups

- Make sure tenants, women, migrants, older and disabled people help design services.
- Run trusted outreach and feedback, and paid maintenance where the community chooses it.

### International bodies

- Share guidance, technical help and lessons across countries, adapted to each place.
- Support open evidence and trained servicing, without assuming one standard fits everywhere.
