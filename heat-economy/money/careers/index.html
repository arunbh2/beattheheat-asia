How to read these

  • Remote does not mean available worldwide: where a listing limits remote work to certain countries, it says so.
  • Pay, visa sponsorship and flexibility are shown only where the employer publishes them; nothing is inferred.
  • A university job, a funded PhD, a fellowship, an internship, an individual consultancy and a fee-charging course are different things and are labelled as such.
