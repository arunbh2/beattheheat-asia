How to read this. A targeted review of published sources, checked in October 2026, not a census of the market. Listing a business, investor or programme is not endorsement, partnership or investment advice, and nothing is ranked. Company descriptions are what the company says unless labelled otherwise. Amounts of different kinds (announced, closed, disbursed; equity, loans, grants, guarantees) are never added together.

What this suggests. Most recorded company financing went to air-conditioning technology, building controls, risk analytics and cold chains, and much of it to companies based outside South and Southeast Asia. Very little reached services such as paid rest, warnings that reach everyone, or care for people who cannot pay. That reflects what can be verified as much as what exists, but it matches what the business ideas below show: much of the protection people need has no paying buyer.
