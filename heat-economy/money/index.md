## Funding we traced

**How to read funding news**
- **Announced** is not the same as **paid**. Money often arrives in stages, or later.
- A company’s funding may pay for more than its heat work.
- Shares, loans, grants and guarantees are different things, so we never add them together.

Services such as paid rest, shared cool rooms and warnings for everyone are usually paid for from public budgets, which this list does not cover.
