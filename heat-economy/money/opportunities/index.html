                          How to read these

                          • A procurement contract is not a grant: a tender buys a service, and the buyer sets the terms.
                          • An individual consultancy is not an organisational tender.
                          • Equity and loans are not grants.
                          • A programme budget is not the amount one applicant can receive.
                          • A deadline without a published time is treated as uncertain: no countdown is shown.

                          Application status and verification are separate. "Verified open" needs both: open, rolling or deadline day, and checked against the official page within the last fortnight. A record we could not re-check shows "Review due" and is not counted as verified open. Rules: status and freshness rules.

                          Being listed does not mean that an organisation endorses Common Shade, or that Common Shade recommends or ranks it. Summaries are short and link to the original listing; read the official documents before applying. Something wrong or missing? Tell us through the corrections page. Corrections.
