## How to read these

- A procurement contract is not a grant: a tender buys a service, and the buyer sets the terms.
- An individual consultancy is not an organisational tender.
- Equity and loans are not grants.
- A programme budget is not the amount one applicant can receive.
- A deadline without a published time is treated as uncertain: no countdown is shown.

Application status and verification are separate. "Verified open" needs both: open, rolling or deadline day, and checked against the official page within the last fortnight. A record we could not re-check shows "Review due" and is not counted as verified open. Rules: [status and freshness rules](https://github.com/arunbh2/beattheheat-asia/blob/main/data/funding/README.md).
