The heat economy · Ideas

Ideas for businesses and services,
cycle by cycle.

32 ideas for new businesses and services, one or more for each of the 21 vicious cycles on the map. Each says who could pay, who it helps and what needs to be in place. They are ideas to explore and test, not proven models.

About this section. We gathered this from public websites and reports in October 2026. Being listed does not mean we recommend, partner with or rank anyone, and none of it is financial advice. Companies describe their own products; we have not tested them, so please check details with them directly. If something is missing or out of date, tell us.

What they have in common. The strongest ideas sell protection that is looked after over time, not just a product. 6 could be paid for by customers. 25 need public or shared funding to reach the people who need them most, and many of those could still be run by local businesses, co-operatives or community groups on contract.

Loops that feed the heat itself

The global cooling loop

Cooling powered by fossil fuels adds to the warming that makes us need more cooling.

Co-operatives of trained cooling techniciansCustomers could payLocal technicians team up to install and service air conditioners properly, so they use less power and leak less gas.

Who could pay

Building owners and businesses pay for servicing; training grants help smaller technicians join.

Who it helps

Occupants and cooling users

Who would need to be on board

  • Hospital and school estate managers
  • Building-owner associations
  • Public purchasing agencies
