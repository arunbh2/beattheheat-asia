# Ideas for businesses and services,
cycle by cycle.

32 ideas for new businesses and services, one or more for each of the 21 vicious cycles on the map. Each says who could pay, who it helps and what needs to be in place. They are ideas to explore and test, not proven models.

**Watch: Business ideas for heat** · 0:30 · narrated, captions in the picture

**About this section.** We gathered this from public websites and reports in October 2026. Being listed does not mean we recommend, partner with or rank anyone, and none of it is financial advice. Companies describe their own products; we have not tested them, so please check details with them directly. If something is missing or out of date, [tell us](https://beattheheat.asia/join/).

**What they have in common.** The strongest ideas sell protection that is **looked after over time**, not just a product. 6 could be paid for by customers. 25 need public or shared funding to reach the people who need them most, and many of those could still be run by local businesses, co-operatives or community groups on contract.

## Loops that feed the heat itself

### [The global cooling loop](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-global)

Cooling powered by fossil fuels adds to the warming that makes us need more cooling.

Co-operatives of trained cooling techniciansCustomers could payLocal technicians team up to install and service air conditioners properly, so they use less power and leak less gas.

#### Who could pay

Building owners and businesses pay for servicing; training grants help smaller technicians join.

#### Who it helps

Occupants and cooling users

#### Who would need to be on board

- Hospital and school estate managers
- Building-owner associations
- Public purchasing agencies

#### Similar work so far

[EESL building energy ESCO partnerships 2021 ](https://eeslindia.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Press%20Release%20-%20EESL%20is%20looking%20to%20partner%20with%20private%20sector%20ESCOs%20to%20scale%20up%20its%20BEEP%5B42%5D.pdf) · [Impact Fund Denmark Smart Joules climate finance ](https://impactfund.dk/news/smart-joules-receives-the-first-investment-made-under-a-new-danish-climate-finance-initiative-for-india/)

#### Funders and programmes that could fit

[Impact Fund Denmark (formerly IFU)](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/money/#inv-ifu) · [TechEmerge Sustainable Cooling Innovation Programme](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/money/#prog-techemerge-cooling)

More detail from the research: what needs to be in place, and risks

In the researchers’ own words, which are more technical.

#### What needs to be in place

- Qualified technicians and compatible parts exist.
- Equipment-specific safety, efficiency and refrigerant practices are checked.
- Contracts pay for preventive work, not only breakdowns.

#### Risks to watch

Poor servicing, unsafe refrigerant substitution and rebound can increase energy or injury risk; avoid locking customers into proprietary parts or unaffordable renewals.

Before starting, see the [questions to ask](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/conditions/).

An idea to explore and test locally, not a proven model. [See the cycle it helps break](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-global).

In the researchers’ own words, which are more technical.

#### Risks to watch

Venting, false recovery records, unsafe transport, contamination and unlicensed destruction; reclaimed high-GWP stock would ideally avoid prolonging avoidable inefficient equipment use.

### [The dry-soil loop](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-soil)

Heat dries the soil. Dry soil can’t cool itself, so the land heats up faster.

Soil-cover services for farmers’ groupsNeeds public or shared fundingGroups help farmers keep soil covered and moist, so land stays cooler and crops cope better.

#### Who could pay

Farmers and crop buyers contribute; public farm budgets help include smaller farmers.

#### Who it helps

Smallholders, agricultural workers and neighbouring land users

#### Who would need to be on board

- Farmer organisations and extension agencies
- Agricultural buyers
- Local water/landscape authorities

#### Similar work so far

[FAO soil organic cover guidance ](https://www.fao.org/conservation-agriculture/in-practice/soil-organic-cover/en/) · [IWMI SoLAR ](https://www.iwmi.org/projects/solar/)

#### Funders and programmes that could fit

[Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/money/#inv-sdc) · [Powering Livelihoods](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/money/#prog-powering-livelihoods)

More detail from the research: what needs to be in place, and risks

In the researchers’ own words, which are more technical.

#### What needs to be in place

- Residues are available without removing essential fodder or fuel.
- Cover crops do not worsen dry-season water competition.
- Local soil moisture and crop response can be monitored.

#### Risks to watch

Biomass diversion, pests, fire load, plastic waste, added women’s unpaid work and water competition; no uniform crop-yield or air-cooling promise.

Before starting, see the [questions to ask](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/conditions/).

An idea to explore and test locally, not a proven model. [See the cycle it helps break](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-soil).

### [The melting-ice loop](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-ice)

Heat melts ice. Darker ground and water absorb more sunlight, and the melting speeds up.

Glaciers need global action, not a local businessPublic action, not a businessSlowing the melting-ice cycle depends on cutting emissions worldwide and on public research. Monitoring glacier risks for nearby communities is a separate public task.

#### Who could pay

Public budgets and international climate funds.

#### Who it helps

Mountain and downstream communities, including those unable to pay.

#### Who would need to be on board

- Public climate and mountain research agencies
- Regional intergovernmental bodies
- Downstream public water authorities

#### Similar work so far

[ICIMOD HI-WISE assessment ](https://hkh.icimod.org/hi-wise/)

More detail from the research: what needs to be in place, and risks

In the researchers’ own words, which are more technical.

#### What needs to be in place

- Collective emissions mitigation remains necessary.
- Adaptation monitoring is not misrepresented as restoring ice or albedo.
- Any technical intervention would require credible environmental and governance evidence before investment.

#### Risks to watch

Speculative geoengineering, misleading glacier-rescue credits, tourism displacement and diversion of adaptation money from community protection.

Before starting, see the [questions to ask](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/conditions/).

An idea to explore and test locally, not a proven model. [See the cycle it helps break](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-ice).

### [The wildfire loop](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-fire)

Heat dries forests into fuel. Fires release carbon and strip away the trees that cool the land.

Paid community fire-prevention teamsNeeds public or shared fundingLocal teams are paid to clear fuel, watch for fires and prepare before the dry season, with community rights respected.

#### Who could pay

Governments, forest agencies and landholders, for a yearly fee.

#### Who it helps

Village residents, outdoor workers and downwind communities.

#### Who would need to be on board

- Village/community institutions
- Fire/disaster agencies
- Land managers and concessionaires

#### Similar work so far

[Asian Agri Fire Free Village 2024–2025 ](https://www.asianagri.com/en/media-publications/press-release/asian-agri-holds-fire-readiness-drill-in-anticipation-of-dry-season-and-signs-mou-for-fire-free-village-programme-2024-2025/) · [NParks annual and sustainability report 2025–2026 ](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/portals/annualreport/chapters/transforming-singapore-into-a-city-in-nature/)

More detail from the research: what needs to be in place, and risks

In the researchers’ own words, which are more technical.

#### What needs to be in place

- Land and customary rights are recognised.
- Prevention activities and safe escalation are agreed with fire authorities.
- Payment does not punish villages for fires beyond their control.

#### Risks to watch

Pressure over land use, unsafe unpaid firefighting, under-reporting of incidents and tension between neighbours; fire has causes beyond heat.

Before starting, see the [questions to ask](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/conditions/).

An idea to explore and test locally, not a proven model. [See the cycle it helps break](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-fire).

## Loops that break the systems we rely on

### [The cooling trap](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-cooling)

Air conditioning heats the street, and a hotter city needs even more air conditioning.

Group buying to cool many small buildingsNeeds public or shared fundingA city or agency bundles many schools, clinics or homes into one contract, starting with shade and design, so suppliers can offer better prices.

#### Who could pay

Public building budgets and building owners.

#### Who it helps

Occupants

#### Who would need to be on board

- City housing departments
- School and clinic estate buyers
- Resident/tenant organisations

#### Similar work so far

[Mahila Housing Trust programme profile ](https://earthshotprize.org/winners-finalists/mahila-housing-trust/) · [EESL building energy ESCO partnerships 2021 ](https://eeslindia.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Press%20Release%20-%20EESL%20is%20looking%20to%20partner%20with%20private%20sector%20ESCOs%20to%20scale%20up%20its%20BEEP%5B42%5D.pdf)

More detail from the research: what needs to be in place, and risks

In the researchers’ own words, which are more technical.

#### What needs to be in place

- Tenure and roof permissions are resolved.
- Passive load reduction precedes correctly sized active cooling.
- Comparable occupied-condition measurements and defect remedies are specified.

#### Risks to watch

Unsafe roofs, glare, moisture/fire problems, overclaimed indoor cooling, landlord rent increases and repayments based on savings that do not exist for households without AC.

Before starting, see the [questions to ask](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/conditions/).

An idea to explore and test locally, not a proven model. [See the cycle it helps break](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-cooling).

### [The energy–water loop](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-energywater)

Water systems need power and power plants need water. In a heatwave, each failure deepens the other.

#### Who could pay

Health and water departments.

#### Who it helps

Patients and communities, including unconnected or low-income residents.

#### Who would need to be on board

- Health-estate buyers
- Water utilities
- Municipal disaster and energy teams

#### Similar work so far

[MIT/BRAC Bangladesh climate shelter ](https://climate.mit.edu/posts/mit-led-project-opens-first-climate-shelter-bangladesh) · [NCDC current heat-health programme ](https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in/includes/About/CentresAndDivision/CEOH.php)

More detail from the research: what needs to be in place, and risks

In the researchers’ own words, which are more technical.

#### What needs to be in place

- Critical and flexible loads are mapped jointly.

#### Risks to watch

Battery failure or fire, flood exposure, fuel dependence, over-pumping, monopolistic service contracts and exclusion of settlements outside formal networks.

Before starting, see the [questions to ask](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/conditions/).

An idea to explore and test locally, not a proven model. [See the cycle it helps break](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-energywater).

### [The groundwater loop](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-pumping)

#### Who could pay

Farmer co-operatives and utilities, with public water management.

#### Who it helps

Smallholders and shared aquifer users, including non-pump owners.

#### Who would need to be on board

- Farmer cooperatives
- Electricity distribution companies
- Groundwater authorities

#### Similar work so far

[CGIAR/IWMI solar irrigation and groundwater ](https://www.cgiar.org/news-events/news/beyond-irrigation-scaling-grid-connected-solar-power-sustainable-groundwater-use) · [IWMI SoLAR ](https://www.iwmi.org/projects/solar/)

More detail from the research: what needs to be in place, and risks

In the researchers’ own words, which are more technical.

#### What needs to be in place

- Water rights/caps and third-party aquifer monitoring exist.
- Grid interconnection, tariffs and timely settlement are checked.

#### Risks to watch

Free marginal pumping energy can worsen depletion; elite capture, unaffordable connections, delayed utility payments and drought-driven over-abstraction.

Before starting, see the [questions to ask](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/conditions/).

An idea to explore and test locally, not a proven model. [See the cycle it helps break](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-pumping).

## Loops that trap people

### [The poverty–heat trap](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-poverty)

People who can’t afford to stop working fall ill. Illness costs income, and lost income makes stopping even less affordable.

Paid rest, shade and water at workCustomers could payA service sets up shade, water and paid rest breaks for employers, and helps them follow heat rules.

#### Who could pay

Employers, who are responsible for their workers.

#### Who it helps

Formal and contracted workers

#### Who would need to be on board

- Employers and supply-chain buyers
- Worker organisations as co-designers
- Labour inspection bodies

#### Similar work so far

[ILO heat and occupational safety policy brief 2024 ](https://www.ilo.org/publications/heat-and-osh-policy-brief-best-practices-gcc-region-and-beyond) · [Nicaraguan rest–shade–hydration–hygiene cohort study, published abstract ](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40803826/)

#### Funders and programmes that could fit

[Shell Foundation](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/money/#inv-shell) · [Keep Cool Innovation Challenge](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/money/#prog-keep-cool)

More detail from the research: what needs to be in place, and risks

In the researchers’ own words, which are more technical.

#### What needs to be in place

- Workers can stop safely without wage or job penalties.
- Worker voice and complaints are protected.
- Exposure, workload and rest practice are checked independently of production targets.

#### Risks to watch

Compliance theatre, worker surveillance, exclusion of subcontractors or women, unpaid rest and using wearables to justify higher quotas.

Before starting, see the [questions to ask](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/conditions/).

An idea to explore and test locally, not a proven model. [See the cycle it helps break](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-poverty).
