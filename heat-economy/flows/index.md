## The role money plays

Our reading of each piece of funding, from its type and stage. This is inferred, not stated by the funders.

Most of the funding we could trace is commercial growth for products with a paying customer. Fewer pieces take on risk, build evidence or build the field, and services such as paid rest or shared cool rooms rarely appear, because they are usually paid for from public budgets this trace does not cover.

## Cycles we have not yet traced funding to 5

This may mean the money exists and we have not found it yet. If you know of funding for any of these, [please tell us](https://beattheheat.asia/corrections/).

- [The dry-soil loop](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-soil)Heat dries the soil. Dry soil can’t cool itself, so the land heats up faster.[Ideas for this cycle](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/opportunities/#loop-soil)
- [The melting-ice loop](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-ice)Heat melts ice. Darker ground and water absorb more sunlight, and the melting speeds up.[Ideas for this cycle](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/opportunities/#loop-ice)
- [The wildfire loop](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-fire)Heat dries forests into fuel. Fires release carbon and strip away the trees that cool the land.[Ideas for this cycle](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/opportunities/#loop-fire)
- [The groundwater loop](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-pumping)Dry fields need more water, so farmers pump harder, aquifers fall and the shortage deepens.[Ideas for this cycle](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/opportunities/#loop-pumping)
- [The medical-debt trap](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-debt)Treatment costs push families into debt. Debt removes any option to rest, and working through the heat brings more illness.[Ideas for this cycle](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-economy/opportunities/#loop-debt)

## The health thread

US$3.3 bn declared · US$1.1 bn committed

Forty-eight countries have put a price on what they need each year to protect health from climate change; international funding committed in 2024 covered about a third (Lancet Countdown 2026). Clinics, heat-illness surveillance and health warnings sit inside that gap.
