protection from heat.

Start-ups, small businesses, social enterprises and established companies, from cool roofs and efficient fans to cold storage, heat sensors and insurance. 57 of them work in South or Southeast Asia.

About this section. We gathered this from public websites and reports in October 2026. Being listed does not mean we recommend, partner with or rank anyone, and none of it is financial advice. Companies describe their own products; we have not tested them, so please check details with them directly. If something is missing or out of date, tell us.

Cool roofs 7

Paints, coatings and sheets that reflect sunlight, so less heat gets into the building.

Ask before buying:
  • Does the roof belong to the people who live there, or will the owner agree?
  • Who recoats or repairs it when it wears, and who pays?
  • Has the product been tested for how much sunlight it reflects?

  • Will it work in existing homes, not only new buildings?
  • Who checks that it was built as designed?

Evaporative coolers 6

Coolers that use water to cool the air. They work well in dry heat, but much less well in humid heat.

Ask before buying:
  • Is the air dry enough where it will be used?
  • Is there enough clean water, and who cleans the pads?
  • Could it make a humid room feel worse?

Heat insurance 4

Insurance that pays out automatically when heat passes a set level, often to replace lost income. It is sometimes called parametric insurance.

Ask before buying:
  • Could someone lose income on a hot day and still get no payout?
  • Who pays the premium next year?
  • How fast is the money paid, and how?
