protection from heat.

Start-ups, small and specialist firms, social enterprises and established companies with a product line for heat, gathered from published sources. 57 have an offer or activity in South or Southeast Asia. Cooling food and medicines is included alongside cooling people, and the two are kept distinct.

How to read this. A targeted review of published sources, checked in October 2026, not a census of the market. Listing a business, investor or programme is not endorsement, partnership or investment advice, and nothing is ranked. Company descriptions are what the company says unless labelled otherwise. Amounts of different kinds (announced, closed, disbursed; equity, loans, grants, guarantees) are never added together.

What this list cannot yet show. For every business here, published evidence that it reaches the poorest fifth of households was not found. That is a gap in the evidence, not a judgement on any company; it is also the question buyers and funders most need answered. All company descriptions are labelled as reported by the company.
