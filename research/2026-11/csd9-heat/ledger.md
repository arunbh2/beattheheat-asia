# CSD9 heat session and the social security policy note: claims ledger

Inputs (not published as files): the session transcript and notes from the side event "Extreme heat in Asia and the
Pacific: an urgent call for action" (Committee on Social Development, ninth session, UN Conference Centre, Bangkok,
13 November 2026; organised by UNDP, ESCAP, UNDRR and IFRC), and the site editor's policy note "Harnessing social
security to combat the impacts of extreme heat". The note listed its 52 sources as domain names only; each claim below
was traced to a public page, staged by `stage.js` and checked with `verify-sources.yml` (`2026-11-csd9-heat`).

Region: **asia** (South Korea, Japan, China, the Gulf and the rest of Asia count as Asia), **elsewhere** (labelled so on
the site), **global**.
