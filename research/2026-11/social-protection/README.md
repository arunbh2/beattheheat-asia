# Social protection and heat: research for /social-protection/ (November 2026)

One ChatGPT reply per country (sections A to K: summary, schemes, access ladder, heat and social protection evidence,
delivery routes and organisations, map indicators, a minimum viable map, where no system responds, open questions,
sources, what it could not verify). Saved here as received, tracking tags removed (none were found).
Countries so far: Bangladesh, India, Indonesia, Nepal, Singapore, Thailand.

Nothing here is a fact until its source is checked:
1. `stage.js` stages every source with the terms and numbers the page would quote (data/inbox/2026-11-social-protection).
2. verify-sources.yml checks them on GitHub.
3. `convert.js` writes data/social-protection/<country>.json, keeping only what the check confirmed, and lists the rest
   in `dropped-after-check.json`.

To add a country, save its reply here as `<country>.md` and run the three steps again.
