Prompted by a Climate Hazards Center blog post of 9 November 2026, "CHIRTS-ERA5, a High Resolution Dataset for Monitoring Regional Temperatures in Near Real Time", which the owner pasted without its address. Batch: `data/inbox/2026-11-chirts-era5/`.

## Used
A Heat Tool Finder record, `chirts_era5`, added to the researched seed (`research/2026-11/heat-tools/common_shade_heat_tools.json`, as `data/heat-tools/README.md` allows) and sourced only to the provider's own page, https://chc.ucsb.edu/node/499, where the source check found: "CHIRTS-ERA5", "quasi-global", "maximum and minimum", "Heat Index", "WBGT", "ERA5", "latency", "GeoTIFF", "NetCDF". The CHC data sets page also lists it. Listed as a documented listing, not tested, not locally validated; licence and registration unknown.

## Held back, and why
- **ERA5 reads cooler than CHIRTS-Daily in warm extremes** ("cooler biases across many locations in Africa, South America, western North America, Europe, and Central and Southern Asia. In some locations, these differences exceed 4°C"). The most useful finding for this site, but it rests only on the blog, whose address is not in hand; a guessed address would be an invented URL. If the owner supplies the URL, it can be staged and, once checked, added as a limit on the `era5` record (worded as a comparison between two estimates, about ERA5 temperatures, not ERA5-Land, which the city history pages use).
- **Start year (1980):** not found on the checked page; the record gives no start year.
- **The CHC Heat Monitor, AgriHeat maps and CHIRTS-GEFS forecasts:** not found on the checked pages (the CHIRTS-daily page and the 2020 publication news page hold none of these terms).
- **Update delay:** CHC pages give 5 or 6 days; the record says only "after a short delay".
- **East Africa case studies and the El Nino outlook:** outside the region, and the outlook is another body's forecast.
