# Living Knowledge: knowledge rights and community authority

- **Summaries, not copies.** Every record is written in Common Shade's own words with citations. No text, photographs, drawings or designs from sources are reproduced. Open access is not blanket permission to reuse images or culturally sensitive knowledge.
- **Licences travel with records.** Each source keeps its own licence (`sources.json`); Common Shade's summary is CC BY 4.0, and the public JSON and printed briefs carry both.
- **No claimed consent or partnership.** No community, organisation or expert was contacted. Records say "Not reviewed by the communities described" until such review happens and is documented.
- **Classification with care.** A practice is called Indigenous only where a source identifies the knowledge holders as Indigenous peoples. Geography or rural location is never a basis. Where a source uses the word loosely (the Rajasthan study's title), the record explains why it is classified otherwise.
- **Location precision.** No household or precise location is published, even where a source gives an address; records name a city, district or region.
- **Restricted and draft records** never reach public JSON, search, feeds or llms text (`tools/living-knowledge.js` filters by status and source check).
- **Contributions.** This edition takes none. The route for corrections and proposed records is the site's corrections page and join form, reviewed by a person. A future contribution route would let contributors choose attribution, permissions and reuse limits, and ask for withdrawal; it would not collect sensitive knowledge or precise locations, and paid participation would need funding before it is offered. Copies already published elsewhere cannot be erased.
