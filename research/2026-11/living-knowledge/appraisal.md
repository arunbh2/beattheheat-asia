## The speaker attribution (unresolved)
The brief quoted a passage attributing a call for a dynamic repository of traditional practices to two named speakers, mentioning Churu and Maharashtra's centre of excellence (CEHSC). Searches (web, the Global Heat and Cooling Forum 2026 proceedings) found one of the two listed as a speaker, as a former Chief Secretary of Maharashtra rather than at a university, and did not find the other or the passage. **The attribution is omitted from public copy.** The forum's proceedings record, unattributed, that integrating traditional knowledge and community insights with evidence-based data is optimal; that summary is cited instead. Nothing implies that CEHSC commissioned, partnered with or endorsed Common Shade.
