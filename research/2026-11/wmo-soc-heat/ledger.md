Source: https://wmo.int/news/media-centre/state-of-climate-2026-gives-new-meaning-phrase-charts, already on Signals (records `sig-wmo-state-of-climate-2026` and `sig-el-nino-2026-2027`) for its headline figures, checked in `data/inbox/2026-11-signals-cop31-2/`. ## Used
- `c-india`: an estimated 146.5 million people in India exposed to extreme heat in an April 2026 heatwave (WMO's own estimate, as stated in the release).
- `c-mhews`: countries reporting multi-hazard early warning systems, 56 in 2015 to 136 in 2025 (a count of systems reported, not a measure of coverage or quality).
- `c-who`: WHO has, for the first time, classified the anticipated health impacts of El Nino as a health emergency (as WMO reports it).

## Held back, and why
- **The Himalayan glacier collapse and flash flood in Nepal (more than 1,300 deaths):** an ice and rock collapse, not a heat event the site tracks; the map's glacier entries already cover the long-term link, and WMO does not call it a glacial lake outburst.
- **"Hundreds of millions of people ... exposed to unbearable extreme heat":** a quotation without a figure or place.
- **Europe's heatwave deaths, greenhouse gas, ocean heat, sea level, sea ice and freshwater figures:** outside the region or beyond the heat focus; the headline temperature is already on Signals.
- **The Secretary-General's and WMO Secretary-General's quotations:** the site names no individuals and keeps to findings.
