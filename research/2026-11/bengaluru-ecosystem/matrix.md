# Bengaluru heat ecosystem: research matrix

Researched 24 to 28 November 2026 from public sources only; no organisation was contacted. Every claim the page
uses is in `claims.json`; `confirmed.json` lists those whose words were all found on their source by
`verify-sources.yml` (batch `2026-11-bengaluru-ecosystem`); `dropped-after-check.json` lists the rest. Columns A to J
follow the brief: A coalition relationship, B Bengaluru heat activities, C work elsewhere, D tools and resources,
E community groups or institutions involved, F coverage, G funding, H stage, I outputs and outcomes, J public route.

"Not found" means not found in the sources listed, not that it does not exist.
