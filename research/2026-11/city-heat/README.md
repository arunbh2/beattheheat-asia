# City heat briefing, more cities and the Atlas: the build record

Moved here from CLAUDE.md in November 2026, word for word, so the build history stays findable while CLAUDE.md keeps only the lasting rules. All five steps of `BUILD_BRIEF.md` are complete; step 6 (heat over time) went beyond it.

Step 1 is live: each `/cities/<id>/` page has "Heat today and the next three days", an estimated heat stress (WBGT) now and an estimated daily peak for four days, filled in the browser by `city-heat/city-heat.js` from one Open-Meteo request (and NEA's measured WBGT for Singapore, labelled as a measurement). The formula is the Australian Bureau of Meteorology's approximation, in `city-heat/heat-core.js`; `script.js` keeps a one-line copy for the homepage city view, and `tools/check-city-heat.js` checks the two agree and that the constants match the checked source in `data/inbox/2026-11-city-heat/`. The words are in `LIVE_WBGT`. No heat-stress level is shown beside the estimate, which reads high in cloudy or windy weather and at night.
Step 2 is live: batch A (Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Bhubaneswar) and batch B (Ho Chi Minh City, Chattogram, Surabaya, Quezon City, Chiang Mai). The cities film is captioned "The first twelve cities" (`watch` in `tools/explainers/site.js`); the film itself is unchanged. City profiles are build-only (`CITY_PROFILES`, `"browser": false`); the homepage city view loads `/cities/profiles.json` only when a reader nears it. Cities added in step 2 are listed in `research/2026-11/city-heat/batches.json`, and `tools/check-city-heat.js` fails if one cites a source that has not passed the check in `data/inbox/2026-11-city-heat/`. Map labels on the homepage and the Atlas place themselves and hide where they would collide; the city buttons and lists still name every city. Do not push to the branch while `verify-sources.yml` is running: it commits its results back and its push fails if the branch has moved.
Step 3 is live (districts for Bangkok, Delhi, Jakarta, Phnom Penh and Ho Chi Minh City; Hanoi held back, see `research/2026-11/city-heat/batches.json`). Step 4 added the Atlas place panel and two-city comparison (`#compare-a-b`). Step 5 found too little evidence for a what-if view and lists `/cooling-options/#measured` instead. Details of step 3: every city page has "What heat costs here, and what could be done" (`cityBrief()` in `tools/build-static.js`: a checked cost figure only where the profile names a loop with one in `cost`, otherwise "No checked estimate found yet"; and the actions that break the city's cycles, by when they could act). A city with `data/city-districts/<id>.json` also gets "Inside the city: districts side by side" (`cityDistricts()`, `city-heat/districts.js`, words in `tools/city-districts/words.js`): a table that loads only when opened, each figure marked above or below the middle district, never ranked. The data comes from `.github/workflows/city-districts.yml` (`tools/city-districts/run.js`, discover or build; the only package, `geotiff`, is pinned for the job): geoBoundaries outlines, WorldPop residents and ages, ESA WorldCover built-up share, OpenStreetMap hospitals and park area. Clinics are in the data file but not shown (far more exist than are mapped). To add a city, add it to `tools/city-districts/cities.json`, run discover, pin its WorldPop entry, run build, and read the figures against known totals before publishing.
Step 6 (heat over time) is live on every city page (a station trend where one passes; Ahmedabad, Chattogram, Colombo, Dhaka, Kathmandu, Phnom Penh, Quezon City and Surabaya have none yet): "Heat over time, 1991 to 2025" (`cityHistory()` in `tools/build-static.js`, words in `tools/city-history/words.js`, statistics in `tools/city-history/stats.js`, checked by `tools/check-city-history.js`). The data comes from `.github/workflows/city-history.yml` (`tools/city-history/run.js`, discover or build; no packages): a NOAA station record first (GHCN-Daily or the Global Summary of the Day, which arrives in Fahrenheit), with ERA5-Land from the Open-Meteo archive shown only as regional background, because it has no buildings or paved ground. A year counts with at most 15 missing days and 3 in any month; a station needs 80% of years and 5 valid years in each end decade, or no trend is shown. A station sits within 35 km of the centre, and a city never borrows a neighbour's. `stationBreak()` compares the station's yearly means with the model: a daytime step of 0.5 °C or more (Pettitt under 1%) withdraws hot days and the hottest day; a night-time step withdraws warm nights only where one step fits clearly better than a steady drift. A withdrawn measure keeps its chart, with the break year marked and the reason beside it. Bangkok's daytime trends were withdrawn this way (see /corrections/). To add a city, put station candidates in `tools/city-history/stations.json`, run discover, then build, and read the trends against what is known before publishing.
The approved plan for the daily heat-stress estimate on city pages, ten more cities, a district risk picture, Atlas place panels and layers, and a possible what-if view is in `research/2026-11/city-heat/BUILD_BRIEF.md`. It lists the decisions already made, what to ask the owner before doing, and the order of work. Follow it step by step.
