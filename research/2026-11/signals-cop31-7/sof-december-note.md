> COP31's clearest heat outcomes so far sit beside the negotiations rather than in them. At the Cooling Ministerial on 16 November, governments backed a country-led, voluntary roadmap on heat resilience and sustainable cooling, and more than US$20 million in new support was announced for the Global Cooling Pledge. The roadmap is backed, not yet adopted, and the money is announced, not yet committed or paid. The presidency launched the Antalya Initiative for Health Resilience as a voluntary platform. The conference closed on 22 November without its cover decision; the negotiated decisions set up a task force to refine the adaptation indicators and let the Adaptation Fund receive a share of carbon-market proceeds, but set no figure for tripling adaptation finance, and none was specific to heat.
