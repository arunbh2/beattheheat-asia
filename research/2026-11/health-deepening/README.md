# Deepening the map: maternal, newborn and child health, mental health and gender-based violence (November 2026)

Candidate sources were staged in data/inbox/2026-11 and checked by .github/workflows/verify-sources.yml, which opened
each page, archived it and recorded whether the expected terms appear (link-check.json, excerpts.json).
Journal publishers block automated reading, so their papers were also checked through Europe PMC abstract lookups
(the `epmc-*` records); those lookups confirm the text and are never cited on the site.

Used on the map (cited by their PubMed records or the publisher's page):
- Chersich et al., BMJ, 2020 (PMID 33148618): preterm birth, stillbirth
- Rekha et al., BJOG, 2023 (PMID 37814395): workplace heat and miscarriage, Tamil Nadu
- Carlton et al., International Journal of Epidemiology, 2016 (PMID 26567313): temperature and diarrhoea
- Carleton, PNAS, 2017 (PMID 28760983): heat, crops and suicide in India (already in the evidence, with its caveat)
- Venugopal et al., Global Health Action, 2016 (PMID 27633034): heat and toilets for women workers
- Zhu et al., JAMA Psychiatry, 2023: temperature and intimate partner violence, India, Nepal and Pakistan
- WHO, UNICEF and UNFPA, 2023: maternal, newborn and child health call for action
- WHO, 2022: mental health and climate change policy brief
- WHO, 2026: heat and health fact sheet

Not used: Burke et al., 2018 (the lookup returned no record), and a second lookup for Thompson et al., 2023 (the
site keeps its existing, verified summary).
