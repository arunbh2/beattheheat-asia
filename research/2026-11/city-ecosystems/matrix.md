# City heat ecosystems (nine cities): research matrix

Researched 28 November 2026 from public sources only, found by web search in English; no organisation was contacted.
Every claim the pages use is in `claims.json`; `confirmed.json` lists those whose words were all found on their
source by `verify-sources.yml` (batch `2026-11-city-ecosystems`); `dropped-after-check.json` lists the rest.

"Not found" means not found in the sources listed, not that it does not exist. Local-language sources (Hindi,
Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu, Odia, Bangla, Nepali) were not searched systematically.

## Conflicts kept, not resolved
- Ahmedabad heat insurance: two thresholds of 43.7°C and 44.1°C (Down To Earth, 2025) or one of 44.2 ºC (WHO Foundation, 2024).
- Hyderabad cool roof mandate: plots of 600 sq. m (NRDC) or 600 square yards (ThePrint).
- Dhaka early action: 123,700 people (Anticipation Hub) or 124,000 (Climate Centre).

## Not reached
The Delhi Heat Action Plan PDF and Chennai's climate plan and cooling guidelines (staged or listed, not read in
research); the Rockefeller Foundation's Dhaka case study and Greenpeace's 2025 Dhaka brief (PDFs the research reader
could not parse); The Statesman and PreventionWeb pages on Bhubaneswar, the Japan Times page on Kolkata and the
OpenCity listing of West Bengal plans (refused automated readers); a Dialogue Earth interview from Dhaka (refused).
A vendor's press release about a Delhi heat risk report was found and left out.
