## Update mechanism: prepared, not automated

**What already works:**
- When NOAA's next discussion date passes, the watch and the explainer say the bulletin is due and not yet checked. They keep the last verified bulletin until a newer one is confirmed.
- The Signals calendar (`signals/calendar.ics`) carries the next bulletin date.

**What is not built:** no workflow fetches or applies a new bulletin automatically.

**To update:**
1. Stage the new bulletin in a batch.
2. Run `verify-sources.yml` and the gate.
3. Move the superseded entry to `history`.
4. Edit `data/site/enso-outlook.json`.
5. Rebuild, then run `node tools/check-enso.js`.
