# NIUA learning notes (Climate Centre for Cities, 2026): what was used and what was held back

Two learning notes from the Climate Centre for Cities (C-Cube), National Institute of Urban Affairs, with GIZ India and the
International Climate Initiative (IKI), 2026, under the Urban Act project. The owner supplied the link to Module 1; Module 2 is
the next note in the same series. Both are training material for officials in Indian city governments, so what they say enters
the site as guidance and as NIUA's reading, never as evidence that a measure works. All five sources passed the check on GitHub
(`data/inbox/2026-11-niua-cities-climate/`), and all five claims were confirmed.

## Used
- **How a city can show results** (Module 2, pp. 51-54): a practical guide on three levers (Cool by design, Green, cool cities,
  Cooling centres and check-ins), so it also reaches the reading list of a plan on /plan/.
- **The four-note series** (Module 1): one entry in Free books and guides.
- **Where heat lands within a city** (Module 2, pp. 34 and 51): one passage and source on the urban heat island consequence.
- **The Climate Centre for Cities** (its own page): one organisation in the heat network, placed in India, typed as a research body
  (facets-edits.json, with its reason).

## Held back
- **Module 1's heat figures**, each cited in the note to a newspaper, not to the original: a 71% rise in exposure to dangerous heat
  in India's ten largest cities (1983-1990 to 2010-2016); heat deaths in Indian cities rising from about 144,000 to over 328,000
  by 2050; heat costing Chennai about US$1.9 billion a year (2.3% of its GDP). To use them, trace each to the World Bank original
  and check it there.
- **Module 2's flood and coastal loss estimates**: outside the site's focus on heat.
- **Module 2's passages on the benefits of heat action plans and on Ahmedabad's cool roofs** (pp. 38-40): they restate studies
  the map already cites at the original.
- **Modules 3 and 4** (urban climate finance; institutionalising climate action): not read in this batch.
- **People**: the notes name their authors and team; none is named on the site.
