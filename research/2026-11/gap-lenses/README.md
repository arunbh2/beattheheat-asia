# Gap lenses: deepening the map (November 2026)

Six research replies, each from a separate ChatGPT chat with no sight of the others, looked for what the map misses:
1 people the map does not separate, 2 compound and cascading events, 3 slow, hidden and under-counted harms,
4 places and countries that differ, 5 return links and cycles, 6 what helps and what backfires.

- `lens-1.md` ... `lens-6.md`: the replies as received (tracking tags removed from links; none were found).
- `stage.js`: stages every source for checking, in data/inbox/2026-11-gap-lenses/ (verify-sources.yml).
- `rejected.md`: each lens's own "could not verify" list, and what the reconciliation set aside.

How they were used (the site owner's decisions, following the plan):
- Nothing is a fact until its source is checked. Every number on the site was found in its source by the checker
  (link-check.json, _excerpts.json), on the page, its archived copy, or its Europe PMC record (by PMID, PMCID or DOI).
- No new dots, cycles or at-risk groups; existing names and lever edges stay as they are.
- Grades (KNOWN, INFERRED, UNCERTAIN) become wording: "studies find", "models suggest", "one study found ..., not yet
  confirmed elsewhere". Each study's own limit travels with the claim.
- Documented side effects of levers show as "watch-outs" (LEVERS `watch`).
- Evidence for cycles goes through the weekly review flow (data/evidence/inbox/), so the merge is the sign-off.
- One pull request per lens, in the order 1, 3, 2, 5, 4, 6.
