# Evidence note: the wet-bulb wall (27 November 2026)

Written before the overlays were drawn. Sources are staged in `data/inbox/2026-11-heat-interactives/` and passed
`verify-sources.yml` (every staged term found) except PsychroLib's paper, whose page did not show "ASHRAE"; the page

| Wang et al. 2026, Environ Int (PMID 42025020) | abstract only | 36 unacclimatised healthy young adults | seated light office work, unrestricted water, up to 8 h at Tw 32, 33, 34, 35 °C | compensable vs uncompensable; projected tolerance times | observed, with projected times | 32 to 33 °C compensable, 34 to 35 °C not, for these people; similar responses at equal Tw in shade | survival times on this page; other populations |

Why protocols matter: stepwise rising heat finds the point where balance is lost quickly; long steady exposure asks
whether balance holds for hours. They answer different questions, so the 2026 study does not overturn 2022 or 2023.

Not interchangeable, and kept apart on the page: air temperature, relative humidity, psychrometric wet-bulb, WBGT (needs
radiation and wind, never computed here), heat index, loss of heat balance, heat illness and survival.
