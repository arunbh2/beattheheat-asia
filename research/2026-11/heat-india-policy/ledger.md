# India heat policy and city climate governance: what was used and what was held back

Four documents supplied by the owner:
- NRDC, 2026: *Extreme Heat in India: A Review of the Evolving Policy and Practice Landscape* (24 pages)
- National Institute of Urban Affairs (NIUA), Climate Centre for Cities:
  - *Climate Considerations in City Plans: A Study of 20 Global and Indian Cities* (2024)
  - *Understanding Urban Climate Finance*, Learning Note 3 (2026, developed by Climate Policy Initiative)
  - *Institutionalisation of Climate Actions*, Learning Note 4 (2026)

The NRDC report is a review that cites originals, so state and city examples taken from it name it as their source. NIUA's
notes enter as guidance. Nothing ranks a state, city or plan; no individual is named. Two NDMA documents that the NRDC report
cites were staged so the guides link to them directly.

## Used
- **Examples ("In practice")**, each citing NRDC:
  - Cool by design: Amravati's cool roof bylaws; Tamil Nadu's cool roofs on government schools
  - Green, cool cities: Tamil Nadu's 15% green cover policy
  - Heat action plans: Uttar Pradesh's district thresholds; Kerala's village-level plan
  - Cooling centres: Jodhpur's net-zero cooling station
  - Dedicated heat budgets: mitigation funds for heatwave projects since September 2024; Kerala's allocation to district collectors
- **Practical guides**: NDMA's roof cooling guide for house owners (Cool by design; checked on the owner's copy, roofguide-check.md); NRDC's review (Heat action plans); NDMA's cooling centre
  guidelines (Cooling centres); the study of 20 city plans (Green, cool cities); Learning Note 3 (Dedicated heat budgets);
  Learning Note 4 (Heat action plans: roles by department; Cool by design: a heat-risk check before project approval)
- **Heat protection underfunded**: urban adaptation finance of about USD 10 billion out of USD 831 billion a year (2021-2022),
  as Learning Note 3 reports from the 2024 State of Cities Climate Finance report
- **Free books and guides**: the NRDC review
- **Heat network**: NRDC's India programme

## Held back
- **NRDC's "up to 5 °C" for roof measures**: not found in NDMA's handbook itself, so not used. (The handbook was added after the
  owner supplied a copy, since its NDMA link does not open for the checker; see roofguide-check.md.)
- **National figures the review cites from other originals** (each could be checked at its own source and added there):
  - deaths: 489,000 heat deaths a year worldwide; more than 24,000 heat deaths in India in 1992-2015
  - exposure: 536 heatwave days and over 44,000 heatstroke cases in 2024; 57% of districts at high or very high heat risk
  - projections: the ILO's 5.8% of working hours lost by 2030; the lost "safe hours" estimates; RCP projections
  - Ahmedabad's 1,190 fewer deaths a year (already on the site at the original)
- **The 2026 IMD seasonal outlook**: a forecast for a season now past.
- **"11 states have notified heatwave as a state-specific disaster"**: a count as of April 2026, overtaken by the national listing
  in August 2026 that Signals already tracks.
- **Hyderabad cool roof energy savings (20-22 kWh/m², 14-26% less air conditioning)**: the cited study is a 2008 general modelling
  paper; check before use.
- **State institutions announced but not yet in operation** (Tamil Nadu's Heat Resilience Centre with the UK, Maharashtra's
  Centre of Excellence for Heat Resilience and Sustainable Cooling): candidates for Signals once an original source is checked.
- **The study of 20 city plans, city by city**: left out, because a per-city count of measures would read as a ranking.
- **Learning Note 4's state examples** (Maharashtra's 36 district and 43 city climate cells; Gujarat's state-city-ward heat
  planning): general governance, kept for a later pass.
