# Second coding for the pilot review: proposed, not scheduled

This is a proposal for how a human second coder could check the pilot's codes on /how-heat-is-reported/. It is not a commitment, and no date is set. Until a second coding is done and reconciled, every pilot code stays provisional.

## Why
The pilot's 32 articles were read in full and coded once, with AI assistance, by one coder. A second, independent reading is the usual way to see whether the codes hold.

## Sample
- At least one article per language, wherever a reader of that language is available. English articles can fill the rest.
- Eight to ten articles is enough to see where codes disagree. At that size the result is reported as agreement on a sample, with no reliability statistic.

## How
1. The second coder reads the article in full, without seeing the first coder's codes.
2. They code the fields shown on the page: organisation of protection (levels 0 to 3), follow-up, affected people speak, informal work, women and care, cycles on the map, and format, using research/2026-10/media/pilot/working-definitions-as-applied.md.
3. For each code they note the passage it rests on, as the first coding does.

## When the two codings disagree
- Both codes are shown on the record, and the record stays provisional until the two coders reconcile it.
- A reconciled code records that it was reconciled, and when.
- Disagreements are listed in this folder, so readers can see what changed and why.

## Privacy and credit
Coders are credited in aggregate ("second-coded by one reader of Nepali, month year"). No names are published, in the files or in the repository's history.
