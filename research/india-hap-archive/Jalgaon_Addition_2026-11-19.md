**Record:** `IN-MH-CITY-JALGAON-CAP`, *Jalgaon Climate Action Plan* (168 pages), Jalgaon City Municipal Corporation with
Mahila Housing Trust. PDF: https://www.mahilahousingtrust.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Jalgaon_CAP_14082026pdf.pdf
(listed by Mahila Housing Trust both as "Jalgaon Heat Action Plan" and "Jalgaon Climate Action Plan").

**Type: `EMBEDDED_HEAT_HEALTH_PLAN`.** The document is a city climate action plan with an *Urban Greening & Heat
Resilience* chapter (printed p. 130), a heat-stress assessment (from p. 98) and heat recommendations (Table 10, p. 132).
It names "Jalgaon Heat Action Plan initiatives led by JCMC and MHT, including cool-roof pilots, ward-level
heat-vulnerability mapping", and among its actions recommends to "formally pass an urban Heat Action Plan (HAP) through
the municipal general board". It is therefore recorded as a heat plan inside a wider plan, not as a standalone HAP.

**Edition:** none printed on the document (the hosting page and file name suggest August 2026; not used, per the archive's
rule on editions).
