## Colours and fonts

Read from the site’s own style sheet, so these values are the ones in use. The logo uses three of them; the interface uses each colour for one meaning.

### In the logo

- **Common Shade orange** #F26430The canopy in every version of the logo.

- **Ink** #16202BThe posts and the name on light backgrounds, and the app icon’s tile.

- **Cream** #F6E7D7The posts and the name on dark backgrounds.

### In the interface

- **Canvas** #FAF7F2Page background.

- **Ink** #16202BBody text and headings.

- **Grey 700** #39424DSecondary text.

- **Grey 500** #5E6570Quiet text, captions and dates.

- **Line** #E6DFD4Hairline borders and dividers.

- **Orange** #F26430Brand and action: buttons, link underlines and highlights.

- **Ember** #B8401AHeat and harm: what drives a vicious cycle, and limits.

- **Break green** #17703AA fix: what breaks a cycle, and examples.

- **Amber** #F5A623Caution: “watch for” notes and provisional items.

### Fonts

- COMMON SHADE**Anton**Display: page titles, the homepage’s story headings and the name in the logo, in capitals.Stack: "Anton", "Impact", "Arial Narrow", sans-serif [Anton on Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Anton)
- Shade held up together**Hanken Grotesk**Body and interface: text, headings, buttons and labels.Stack: "Hanken Grotesk", "Segoe UI", Roboto, Arial, sans-serif [Hanken Grotesk on Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Hanken+Grotesk)
- Shade held up together**Newsreader**Editorial: ledes and quotes, not headings.Stack: "Newsreader", Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif [Newsreader on Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Newsreader)

All three are free under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/). Get them from Google Fonts; they are not included in the pack.
