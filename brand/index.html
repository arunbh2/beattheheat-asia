    Read from the site’s own style sheet, so these values are the ones in use. The logo uses three of them; the interface uses each colour for one meaning.

    • Canvas #FAF7F2Page background.
    • Ink #16202BBody text and headings.

    Practical notes, not formal guidelines

    These are practical notes, not formal brand guidelines. No minimum size, clear space or other formal rule has been set.

    • Use the files on this page, as they are, rather than copying the logo from a screenshot.
    • Keep the logo’s proportions: scale it evenly, without stretching or squashing.
    • Do not crop, recolour, outline, rotate or add effects to the logo.
