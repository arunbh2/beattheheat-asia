# Find your way in

Common Shade is large on purpose. You do not need all of it. Each path is five or so stops, each answering one question your work asks. Your choice stays in this browser, and you can leave a path at any time.

- [Funders and philanthropyWhere support could matter most, what works, and who could carry it to people.6 stops](https://beattheheat.asia/paths/funder/)
- [City and local governmentYour city’s heat, the actions that fit, and a plan your team can run.7 stops](https://beattheheat.asia/paths/city/)
- [Health workers and servicesWho is most at risk, what the evidence says, and how clinics prepare.5 stops](https://beattheheat.asia/paths/health/)
- [Employers and businessesWhat heat does to work and pay, and what protects people and the business.4 stops](https://beattheheat.asia/paths/employer/)
- [Worker and community organisationsWho is exposed, what protection needs, and how to make it work together.4 stops](https://beattheheat.asia/paths/community/)
- [National government and regulatorsCountry snapshots, worked plans, the evidence and how protection is paid for.5 stops](https://beattheheat.asia/paths/national/)
- [Journalists and researchersHow heat is reported, the evidence behind every claim, and the open questions.5 stops](https://beattheheat.asia/paths/research/)
