# Roadmap: Made to measure

Status: DRAFT · Profile: Balanced · Format: 45 s, 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps

## 1. Concept
- **Logline:** A tailor cuts each heat plan to the last heatwave's measurements; the plans work, until the heat outgrows the coat, and the tailor learns what every tailor who sews for a child already knows.
- **The turn:** Before: a plan made to measure fits (Odisha's deaths fall). / After: the heat keeps growing, the coat will not close, and the next pattern is cut with room to grow.
- **Ending:** the tailor's chalk draws a new line well outside the old pattern; the title is written in that chalk. It re-reads the opening line ("cut to measure") as the mistake and the fix at once.
- **How code shows it:** hand-made paper on a lit tabletop (canvas 2D with paper texture, real cast shadows from a slatted window, depth layers). The subject is on screen at every beat: pattern paper, chalk, a measuring tape, a paper coat on a dress form, labels in the tailor's chalk with each year. People only as faceless hands and forearms, cut from paper.
- **World test:** every image belongs to a tailor's shop in a hot city at noon: shutter slats, a treadle machine, tailor's chalk, pins in a tin, a ceiling fan, a measuring tape, paper tags. No thermometers, no maps, no charts.

**Tempo:** standard, about 5 s a shot: the story moves in measured steps like the treadle, then holds at the turn · shots 9 · BPM 72 (the treadle's beat)

## 3. Look
- **Base:** custom, direction A "Noon through the shutters" (board: Monsoon Wedding marigolds, Lawrence of Arabia sun).
- **Changes:** bone and cream pattern paper, white tailor's chalk, indigo thread, saffron and marigold tags, terracotta walls; hard noon sun through slats laying bars across everything; shadows warm brown, never black.

## 4. Sound identity
- **Chosen concept:** machine time: a treadle sewing machine sets the tempo (72); cuts, pins and chalk land on it; it stops once.
- **Palette:** recorded foley (shears, paper, chalk, tape, pins), synthesised treadle and fan, the user's chosen piano recording, one calm voice.
- **Signature sound:** the treadle; it starts on frame 1, stops at S07, returns slower at S08.
- **Silence:** 1 s at S07.
- **Voice · music:** calm narrator (Piper LibriTTS, its own treatment: a small room, close) · the piano recording the user picks by ear, low under the voice.
- **Ambience · shared space:** one shop room at noon: fan, faint street through shutters; one small-room reverb for everything.

## 5. Hook (first 2 s)
The shears close along a chalk line in a bar of sunlight on frame 1; the bite of the blades and the first treadle stroke start with it.

## 6. Risks
| Risk | Why | Plan |

| Facts outrun the record | the paper's future figures are not yet checked | only figures on the site's /heatwaves/ page; the future is the title line, with no number |

## 7. Director card
- **Structure:** a craft parable in three movements: measure (S01-S04), outgrow (S05-S07), room to grow (S08-S09).
- **Look:** A, noon through the shutters, because the heat itself is in the room while the plan is made · **Camera:** a patient tailor's eye, mostly top-down and close; signature move: a slow push along the chalk line · **Motion:** "deliberate" (moves start with inertia and settle softly).
- **Tempo:** standard (above) · **Transitions:** cuts on the treadle's beat; one match cut from the old pattern to the new line (S07 to S08).
- **Sound:** machine time; narrator in a small close room.
- **Light:** one idea: noon sun through slats; the bars slide across the table as days pass (S05) and lie still at the end · **Signature:** the chalk line, which opens the film as the old pattern and closes it as the new one.
- **How each thing is seen:** paper pieces with torn and cut edges catching the light; chalk as dusty strokes; the tape as a paper ribbon with ticks; hands as flat paper shapes; the coat as layered paper panels with stitched edges.
- **Atmosphere:** one shop at noon: hard sun from frame left through slats, dust in the light bars, the near table sharp, the rail soft behind, the shutters far.
- **Motion design:** chalk tags carry the years and places; no charts.
- **Type:** at step 4b · **Texture:** paper fibre and a fine grain · **Finishing:** at the style frame, your choice.
