# Fonts used by the reel

Copies of the site's typefaces are kept here, so every render of `tools/reel/reel.html` looks the same on any machine.

- **Anton** (Regular) by Vernon Adams. SIL Open Font License 1.1. Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Anton
- **Inter** (Medium, SemiBold, Bold) by Rasmus Andersson. SIL Open Font License 1.1. Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter

The licence text is at https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/
