## How to read this

- **Approximate places**Heatwaves are drawn as areas around where they were worst; the map has no borders and is not a political map.

- **A milestone is a step, not a score**Each marks something done, with its source. It says nothing about how well it worked, or about places not shown.

- **Heat right now is an estimate**Live model data, refreshed every 30 minutes. It is not an official warning: for alerts, follow the national weather service linked on each city.

- **Missing a step?**Plans and rules are added each month. If one is missing, [please tell us](https://beattheheat.asia/corrections/).
