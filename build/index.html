    Act

    Build protection

    Choose a place, add actions, and see which cycles they could help interrupt, what each depends on, and what they leave untouched. Nothing here is a recommendation: it is a way to think a package through, together.

    How to read this

    • Could help interrupt, never will fixA cycle turns green when an action works on one of its links. Whether it breaks in a real place depends on everything listed under what it depends on.
    • Evidence shown for every pieceEach action carries its evidence level, from design concept to independently evaluated, with its source.
    • No recommendationsThe portfolio view shows balance and blind spots. It never recommends an organisation or a grantee.
    • Saved in the addressYour package lives in the page address, so you can bookmark it or share it. Nothing is sent to us.
    • Carried into a plan"Carry these actions into a plan" adds them to Plan a response, which keeps its plan in your browser. Anything already in that plan stays as it is, and each line says which actions it came from.
