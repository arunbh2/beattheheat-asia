## What decision it helps with

Prepare for El Niño and seasonal risk. Regional rainfall and temperature terciles for Southeast Asia over the coming season.

Suits: City and district teams, NGOs and community groups, Health workers and agencies, Funders.

## What you need to provide

## What you get

- Likely below-, near- or above-normal rainfall and temperature by sub-region.

## Measure and method

**Heat measure**: Defined by the provider

**Method**: Multi-model terciles; national services hold the national-scale outlook.
