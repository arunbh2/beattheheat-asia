## What decision it helps with

Prepare for El Niño and seasonal risk. The official US monthly ENSO status, with phase and strength probabilities for the coming three-month seasons.

Suits: City and district teams, NGOs and community groups, Health workers and agencies, Funders.

## What you need to provide

- Read the current discussion and its issue date.
- Read the probability table for the season you plan for.

## What you get

- Alert status (for example El Niño Advisory), observed Pacific conditions, phase odds and strength odds by season.

## Coverage, scale and time horizon

**Coverage**: Global

**How it applies in Asia**: Relevant by editorial judgement. Not validated locally.

**Local validation**: Local validation not assessed

**Scale**: Tropical Pacific (Niño-3.4 region)

**Time horizon**: Seasonal and extended-range outlooks

## Measure and method

**Heat measure**: Defined by the provider

**Method**: Official bulletin; outlooks verified with the Relative Oceanic Niño Index (RONI), 1991-2020 base.

Air temperature, surface temperature, heat index, WBGT and UTCI measure different things and are never interchangeable.

## What it cannot tell you

- Describes the Pacific, not any town's heat, rain or water supply.
- Strength odds describe the Pacific index, not the strength of local impacts.
