# Copernicus ERA5-HEAT

Copernicus / ECMWF. Analyse historical outdoor thermal conditions.

Public documentation reviewed

**Important limit:** Reanalysis is not a local measurement; outdoor grid estimates do not describe indoor rooms or specific workloads.

[View source / access information](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/derived-utci-historical?tab=overview)
[Add to a comparison](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/?compare=era5#compare)

## What decision it helps with

Analyse heat exposure. Analyse historical outdoor thermal conditions.

Suits: Researchers, City and district teams.

## What you need to provide

- Area or point, dates and variable; technical data workflow.

## What you get

- UTCI and mean radiant temperature data.

## Coverage, scale and time horizon

**Coverage**: Global (except Antarctica)

**How it applies in Asia**: Relevant by editorial judgement. Not validated locally.

**Local validation**: Local validation not assessed

**Scale**: 0.25-degree grid

**Time horizon**: Past conditions

## Measure and method

**Heat measure**: UTCI (universal thermal climate index)

**Method**: ERA5-derived reanalysis product.

Air temperature, surface temperature, heat index, WBGT and UTCI measure different things and are never interchangeable.

## What it cannot tell you

- Reanalysis is not a local measurement; outdoor grid estimates do not describe indoor rooms or specific workloads.
