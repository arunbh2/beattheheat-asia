# CBE Thermal Comfort Tool

UC Berkeley Center for the Built Environment. Compare indoor thermal-comfort design conditions.

Public documentation reviewed

**Important limit:** Comfort predictions are not medical safety limits. Check each model's population and applicability assumptions.

[View source / access information](https://comfort.cbe.berkeley.edu/)
[Add to a comparison](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/?compare=cbe#compare)

## What decision it helps with

Design cooler places. Compare indoor thermal-comfort design conditions.

Suits: Designers and engineers, Researchers, Employers and workplaces.

## What you need to provide

- Temperature, humidity, air speed, radiation, clothing and activity, depending on method.

## What you get

- Comfort-model calculations and visual comparison.

## Measure and method

**Heat measure**: Thermal-comfort indices

**Method**: Thermal comfort models and documented standards.

Air temperature, surface temperature, heat index, WBGT and UTCI measure different things and are never interchangeable.

## What it cannot tell you

- Comfort predictions are not medical safety limits. Check each model's population and applicability assumptions.
