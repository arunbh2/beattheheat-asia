## What decision it helps with

Prepare for El Niño and seasonal risk. Multi-model global probabilities for temperature and rainfall over the next three months, with the state of the main ocean drivers.

Suits: City and district teams, NGOs and community groups, Health workers and agencies, Funders.

## What you need to provide

- Read the edition for the three months you plan for.

## What you get

- Global maps of likely above- or below-normal temperature and rainfall; ENSO and Indian Ocean summaries.

## Coverage, scale and time horizon

**Coverage**: Global

**How it applies in Asia**: Relevant by editorial judgement. Not validated locally.

**Local validation**: Local validation not assessed

**Scale**: Global, coarse regional scale

**Time horizon**: Seasonal and extended-range outlooks

## Measure and method

**Heat measure**: Defined by the provider

**Method**: Multi-model ensemble from WMO's global producing centres; the Niño 3.4 figures are a plain anomaly, not RONI.

Air temperature, surface temperature, heat index, WBGT and UTCI measure different things and are never interchangeable.

## What it cannot tell you

- Global maps are too coarse for a district; read with the national service's outlook.
- Each edition covers its own three months; compare like with like.
