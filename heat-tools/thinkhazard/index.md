# ThinkHazard!: extreme heat

GFDRR. Screen extreme-heat hazard when scoping a project.

Public documentation reviewed

**Important limit:** Hazard is not full risk or a site-specific engineering assessment; its categories are not work-stop thresholds.

## What decision it helps with

Decide where to focus. Screen extreme-heat hazard when scoping a project.

Suits: City and district teams, Funders, Researchers.

## What you need to provide

- Select a place and the extreme-heat hazard.

## What you get

- Hazard category and planning guidance.

## Coverage, scale and time horizon

**Coverage**: Global

**How it applies in Asia**: Relevant by editorial judgement. Not validated locally.

**Local validation**: Local validation not assessed

**Scale**: Administrative area

**Time horizon**: Past conditions

## Measure and method

**Heat measure**: WBGT (wet-bulb globe temperature)

**Method**: Probabilistic hazard screening using daily maximum WBGT.

Air temperature, surface temperature, heat index, WBGT and UTCI measure different things and are never interchangeable.

## What it cannot tell you

- Hazard is not full risk or a site-specific engineering assessment; its categories are not work-stop thresholds.
