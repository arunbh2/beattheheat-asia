# CHIRTS-ERA5 daily temperature and heat data

UC Santa Barbara Climate Hazards Center. Analyse recent and past daily heat across a quasi-global high-resolution grid.

Public documentation reviewed

**Important limit:** A gridded estimate built partly from reanalysis, not a local measurement; outdoor grid values do not describe indoor rooms or specific workloads.

## What decision it helps with

Analyse heat exposure. Analyse recent and past daily heat across a quasi-global high-resolution grid.

Suits: Researchers, City and district teams.

## What you need to provide

- Area, dates and variable; technical data workflow (GeoTIFF or NetCDF files).

## What you get

- Daily maximum and minimum temperature, heat index and WBGT grids.

## Coverage, scale and time horizon

**Coverage**: Global

**How it applies in Asia**: Relevant by editorial judgement. Not validated locally.

**Local validation**: Local validation not assessed

**Scale**: High-resolution grid

**Time horizon**: Past conditions

## Measure and method

**Heat measure**: Air temperature; Heat index; WBGT (wet-bulb globe temperature)

**Method**: Satellite- and station-based temperature climatology (CHIRTS) combined with ERA5 reanalysis, updated after a short delay.

Air temperature, surface temperature, heat index, WBGT and UTCI measure different things and are never interchangeable.

## What it cannot tell you

- A gridded estimate built partly from reanalysis, not a local measurement; outdoor grid values do not describe indoor rooms or specific workloads.
- Heat index and WBGT here are estimates from grid temperature and humidity, not readings from a site.
