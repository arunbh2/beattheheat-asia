## I need to prepare for El Niño-related seasonal risk

Tools sorted by the question each answers, from the Pacific to a local warning. A bulletin, a monitoring dashboard, a forecast service, a warning service and a workbook do different jobs; read each with its own issue date. The current outlook, explained: [El Niño and heat in Asia](https://beattheheat.asia/el-nino/#outlook); preparing a city: [El Niño and India's smaller cities](https://beattheheat.asia/el-nino-and-cities/).

None of these turns El Niño odds into a local heatwave forecast, and Common Shade does not build one. Pacific monitoring and seasonal outlooks give context and lead time; the official local warning sets agreed arrangements in motion.

## Four illustrative workflows

For a practitioner, the useful answer is often a combination: regional context, an official warning or local measurement, then institutional action. These are illustrative workflows, not validated protocols, automated recommendations or endorsed procedures, and no partnership with any provider is implied.

Illustrative workflow, not an official recommendation

### An Indian city preparing its heat plan

- [CEEW District Heat Risk Assessment](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/ceew/)

**Why:** Establish district context; commission finer local assessment where needed.

**What it leaves open:** District ranking does not measure street-level or individual risk. Climate and social inputs have different reference years.

- [IMD Heat Wave Guidance](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/imd/)

**Why:** Consult current official warnings and local activation rules.

**What it leaves open:** Check issue and validity times at source. Experimental heat index is not a WBGT measurement or workplace safety clearance.

- [WHO Heat–Health Action Plans Guidance (2026)](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/who_hhap/) + [Common Shade: Intersections](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/cs_intersections/)

**Why:** Review planning responsibilities and access barriers.

**What it leaves open:** Developed for WHO Europe and adaptable elsewhere; local law, climate and institutional roles need checking. Not a validated vulnerability score or a prediction of outcomes in a real street.

- [Common Shade: Plan a response](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/cs_plan/)

**Why:** Record agreed actions, payers, triggers and follow-up.

**What it leaves open:** Named roles are proposals until agreed; completion does not establish funding or readiness.

### I need to prepare for El Niño-related seasonal risk

- [NOAA ENSO Diagnostic Discussion and probabilities](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/noaa_enso/) + [Bureau of Meteorology climate driver update](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/bom_enso/) + [ASMC El Niño / La Niña status](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/asmc_enso/) + [IMD ENSO and IOD update bulletin](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/imd_enso/)

**Why:** A. ENSO monitoring: what is happening in the Pacific, as each agency reports it, with its own date and index.

**What it leaves open:** Describes the Pacific, not any town's heat, rain or water supply. Weekly values move from week to week; they are not the three-month index used to declare an event. Typical effects are historical tendencies, not this season's forecast for any province. May differ from other agencies' readings of the Indian Ocean Dipole, which use their own indices and thresholds.

- [WMO Global Seasonal Climate Update](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/wmo_gscu/) + [ASMC seasonal outlook](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/asmc_so/)

**Why:** B. Seasonal outlooks: how temperature and rainfall odds may shift by region over the next three months.

**What it leaves open:** Global maps are too coarse for a district; read with the national service's outlook. A regional tercile does not give a province's rainfall or temperature.

- [IMD extended range forecast](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/imd_erf/)

**Why:** C. Extended range and local forecasts: what local services prepare for in the coming weeks.

**What it leaves open:** Weeks ahead are less certain than days ahead; local action follows the district warning.

- [IMD Heat Wave Guidance](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/imd/) + [BMD Heat Wave Warnings](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/bmd/) + [Nepal DHM Weather and Heat-Wave Bulletins](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/dhm/) + [Singapore Heat Stress Advisory](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/sg/)

**Why:** D. Official warning services: the local warning that sets agreed arrangements in motion.

**What it leaves open:** Check issue and validity times at source. Experimental heat index is not a WBGT measurement or workplace safety clearance. Directory existence does not establish that an active warning exists today. Check the bulletin date, scope and validity. Retrieved direct page required JavaScript; heat-wave services confirmed in indexed official text. Verify the current bulletin before relying on it. General-population outdoor advisory; local workplace conditions and occupational requirements need separate assessment.

- [El Niño preparedness worksheet (one A4 page)](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/cs_enso_worksheet/) + [Common Shade: Plan a response](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/cs_plan/) + [WHO Heat–Health Action Plans Guidance (2026)](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/who_hhap/)

**Why:** E. Readiness and planning: who acts, with what resources and permissions, before the season.

**What it leaves open:** It does not forecast anything; local warnings set the timing. Named roles are proposals until agreed; completion does not establish funding or readiness. Developed for WHO Europe and adaptable elsewhere; local law, climate and institutional roles need checking.

## How this catalogue works

It lists 24 outside resources and 5 Common Shade pages, researched up to 2026-11-10. That number describes this starter catalogue only, not the size of the field or how well heat protection works.

**Public description reviewed**: The provider's public description was read.

**Selected methodology documentation reviewed**: Parts of the method documentation were read.

**Official indexed description reviewed; direct verification incomplete**: The official description was found in indexed text, but direct access was incomplete. That does not mean the service is broken.

Reviewed never means validated or certified, and none of these tools was run by Common Shade. "Not verified" means nobody has checked it yet, which is different from "no". Free to read is not an open-data licence, and a listed API is not a connection Common Shade has tried. Global coverage is not validation in an Asian city, and different heat measures (air temperature, heat index, WBGT, UTCI) are never interchangeable. Live warnings stay with the official agencies that issue them.

Records are reviewed monthly, with a broader search each quarter; a record older than 90 days is flagged for recheck and kept, not deleted. Know of a tool we have missed, or a detail that has changed? [Tell us, with its source](https://beattheheat.asia/join/?about=Heat%20Tool%20Finder#way-share): suggestions are checked before they appear. The catalogue is open as [one JSON file](https://beattheheat.asia/data/heat-tools/catalogue.json).

Inspired by UNEP FI's approach to making risk tools easier to compare. Independently developed by Common Shade.
