# ClimateCHIP WorkHeat

ClimateCHIP. Explore modelled heat challenges for work today and in future climates.

Public documentation reviewed

**Important limit:** Modelled output losses are not measured wages. Grid estimates cannot clear an individual worker for work.

[View source / access information](https://www.climatechip.org/heat-effects-assessment-tool)
[Add to a comparison](https://beattheheat.asia/heat-tools/?compare=workheat#compare)

## What decision it helps with

Plan worker protection. Explore modelled heat challenges for work today and in future climates.

Suits: Employers and workplaces, NGOs and community groups, Funders, Researchers.

## What you need to provide

- Location, work type and selected period/scenario.

## What you get

- Estimated days needing protective action and potential labour-output effects.

## Coverage, scale and time horizon

**Coverage**: Global

**How it applies in Asia**: Relevant by editorial judgement. Not validated locally.

**Local validation**: Local validation not assessed

**Scale**: 0.5-degree grid

**Time horizon**: Past and future climates

## Measure and method

**Heat measure**: WBGT (wet-bulb globe temperature)

**Method**: Climate/station inputs and occupational heat modelling.

Air temperature, surface temperature, heat index, WBGT and UTCI measure different things and are never interchangeable.

## What it cannot tell you

- Modelled output losses are not measured wages. Grid estimates cannot clear an individual worker for work.
