Proposal · example

Let's spend next quarter paying down debt

From Platform teamTo Engineering leadsStatus Open for comments
TL;DR

Every feature now takes longer because of shortcuts we took to ship the last ones. One quarter of steady cleanup will make the rest of the year faster.

Why everything feels slower

Each shortcut was the right call when we took it. But every one sits on top of the last, and now a small change in one place breaks something three floors up.

  1. Ship fastQuick fixes land a little off-centre. Fine on their own.
  2. It wobblesStacked up, they lean. Every new change needs someone holding the tower.
  3. Pay it downA planned refactor squares it up, and the next crate goes on safely.

Why small fixes are enough

We don't need a rewrite. One small cleanup a day doesn't look like much in a week, but it adds up the same way interest does.

  1. Start smallPick one flaky test, one slow query, one confusing module.
  2. Keep goingEvery day. Each fix makes the next one easier to find and do.
  3. It compoundsBy the end of the year, the gain is far bigger than the sum of the days.
1.01365 ≈ 37.81% better every day for a year
0.99365 ≈ 0.031% worse every day for a year

What we're asking for

  1. Each team keeps one engineer on cleanup for the quarter, rotating every two weeks.
  2. A shared list of the ten worst spots, ranked by how often they slow us down.
  3. A check-in at the end of the quarter to see if feature work got faster.
