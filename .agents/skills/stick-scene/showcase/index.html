/stick-scene · showcase

Not just for landing pages. Anywhere a small drawing explains it faster than a paragraph.

Each scene is one canvas with no dependencies. It idles quietly and acts out one idea when you scroll to it or click it. Here are three places that aren't a hero section, each with scenes made for it.
