Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Hanoi

The energy–water loop

In May and June 2023 heat drove demand up as hydropower reservoirs ran low; blackouts in the north cost about US$1.4 billion.

Water systems need power and power plants need water. In a heatwave, each failure deepens the other. Follow it on the map.

The global cooling loop

Southeast Asia’s air conditioners could rise from 40 million to 300 million by 2040.

Cooling powered by fossil fuels adds to the warming that makes us need more cooling. Follow it on the map.
