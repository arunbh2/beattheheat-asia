## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Hanoi

### [The energy–water loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#energywater)

In May and June 2023 heat drove demand up as hydropower reservoirs ran low; blackouts in the north cost about US$1.4 billion. Source: [World Bank estimate, reported by AFP via The Manila Times, 2023](https://manilatimes.net/2023/08/12/business/foreign-business/power-crisis-cost-vietnam-14b-wb/1904920/amp)

Water systems need power and power plants need water. In a heatwave, each failure deepens the other. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-energywater).

### [The global cooling loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#global)

Southeast Asia’s air conditioners could rise from 40 million to 300 million by 2040. Source: [International Energy Agency, 2019](https://www.iea.org/reports/the-future-of-cooling-in-southeast-asia)

Cooling powered by fossil fuels adds to the warming that makes us need more cooling. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-global).

## Already at work here

After Typhoon Yagi in 2024, it let lenders reschedule repayments for affected borrowers across 26 provinces, with final repayment allowed until the end of 2027.
