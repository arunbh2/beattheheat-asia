# Dhaka: who is doing what about heat?

Who is working on heat in the city, what exactly they are doing, what anyone can use, and what is still only planned. Built from public sources, each line checked against its source.

Dhaka has two strands of heat work. Dhaka North appointed Asia's first city Chief Heat Officer in 2023, with water booths, plans for cooling zones and greening. The Red Crescent has the most tested system: an early action protocol that, in April 2024, released cash, cooling stations and water before the worst heat. A city heat action plan is still proposed.

### Red Crescent early action for heatwaves

A protocol that releases money and action once a heat forecast crosses a threshold. In April 2024 it was activated for the first time: cash grants, cooling stations at three locations, ambulance support, early warnings and drinking water.

**Lead**: [Bangladesh Red Crescent Society](#org-bdrcs)

**With**: [International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (IFRC)](#org-ifrc) (funded the activation)

**Where**: Adabor, Gendaria, Kamrangir Char, Lalbagh, Mohammadpur and Pallabi; warnings city-wide (Neighbourhoods)

**People**: Older people, Outdoor workers, Slum residents

**When**: April 2024

**Outputs**: Activated 21 April 2024; Three cooling stations

**Evaluation**: A post-activation report exists but was not read for this profile.

**Evidence**: The organisation's own account; latest dated evidence April 2024

## How this was made

Organisations and activities identified in public sources, read on 28 November 2026. It is not a complete list of everyone working on heat in Dhaka, and no organisation was contacted.

11 of 11 claims passed the source check, last run 28 November 2026: each was kept only when every word it rests on was found on its source. A source that only opened does not count. The date of the check is not a date any project moved.

Sources were found by web search in English; local-language sources were not searched systematically. Where a source could not be read, nothing rests on it.
