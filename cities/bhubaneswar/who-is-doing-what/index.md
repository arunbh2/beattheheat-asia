# Bhubaneswar: who is doing what about heat?

Who is working on heat in the city, what exactly they are doing, what anyone can use, and what is still only planned. Built from public sources, each line checked against its source.

Bhubaneswar, in a state long known for heatwave preparedness, launched an integrated heat and cooling plan in 2025 that links heat to the city's fast-growing demand for air conditioning, its lost water bodies and its building codes. It follows an earlier city heat action plan prepared with IRADe.

### Integrated Heat and Cooling Action Plan (2025)

A plan linking heat and cooling demand: greening in all wards, strict use of the state's energy building codes, and projections of cooling demand to 2050.

**Lead**: [Bhubaneswar Municipal Corporation](#auth-bmcbbs)

**With**: [International Forum for Environment, Sustainability and Technology (iFOREST)](#org-iforest) (developed the plan); [Singapore-ETH Centre](#org-sec) (collaborated)

**Where**: All wards (One city corporation)

**People**: Residents

**When**: September 2025

**Outputs**: The plan, launched September 2025

**Evaluation**: Not evaluated; temperature reductions are modelled projections.

**Evidence**: Independent reporting; latest dated evidence September 2025
