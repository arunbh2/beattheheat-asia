## Heat action plan: Heat plan in force

In September 2025 the Bhubaneswar Municipal Corporation and the think tank iFOREST launched the city’s first Integrated Heat and Cooling Action Plan, which addresses heat stress and cooling demand together. An earlier climate-adaptive heat action plan, prepared with IRADe, the Indian Institute of Public Health and the Odisha State Disaster Management Authority, mapped heat hotspots ward by ward.

Sources: [OdishaTV, 2025: Bhubaneswar Municipal Corporation unveils heat and cooling action plan](https://odishatv.in/news/odisha/bhubaneswar-to-face-extreme-heat-sevenfold-rise-in-ac-demand-by-2050-bmc-unveils-action-plan-271303) · [Bhubaneswar Municipal Corporation and IRADe: Heat wave action plan, Bhubaneswar city](https://heathealth.info/wp-content/uploads/Bhubaneswar-Heat-Action-Plan.pdf)

How the estimate is made

Wet-bulb globe temperature (WBGT) is the heat-stress index used in international and national standards for heat at work.

The figure here is our estimate for the city as a whole, calculated from forecast air temperature and humidity with the Australian Bureau of Meteorology's simplified formula. That formula assumes fairly strong sun and light wind, and leaves out changes in sun and wind. In cloudy or windy weather, and at night or early in the morning, it is likely to read higher than a measured value.

It is not a measurement and not an official warning. No heat-stress level is shown beside the estimate: published WBGT levels are for measured values.

Researchers comparing methods recommend a fuller calculation for outdoor work, which also uses sun and wind; this page does not use it yet.

Sources: [Australian Bureau of Meteorology: Thermal comfort observations, about the approximation to the WBGT](http://www.bom.gov.au/info/thermal_stress/) · [Lemke and Kjellstrom, 2012: Calculating workplace WBGT from meteorological data, Industrial Health](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/indhealth/50/4/50_MS1352/_pdf) · [Weather data by Open-Meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com/)

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Bhubaneswar

### [The cooling trap](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#cooling)

The city’s heat and cooling plan expects demand for air conditioning to rise sevenfold by 2050. Source: [OdishaTV, 2025: Bhubaneswar Municipal Corporation unveils heat and cooling action plan](https://odishatv.in/news/odisha/bhubaneswar-to-face-extreme-heat-sevenfold-rise-in-ac-demand-by-2050-bmc-unveils-action-plan-271303)

Air conditioning heats the street, and a hotter city needs even more air conditioning. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-cooling).

### [The poverty–heat trap](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#poverty)

The city’s heat plan found poor working women more vulnerable to heat stress, with dehydration among women who lack drinking water at work. Source: [Bhubaneswar Municipal Corporation and IRADe: Heat wave action plan, Bhubaneswar city](https://heathealth.info/wp-content/uploads/Bhubaneswar-Heat-Action-Plan.pdf)

People who can’t afford to stop working fall ill. Illness costs income, and lost income makes stopping even less affordable. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-poverty).
