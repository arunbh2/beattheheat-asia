## Heat action plan: Heat plan in force

Singapore launched a national Heat Stress Advisory in July 2023. It rates heat stress as low, moderate or high from the wet-bulb globe temperature, and tells people what to do at each level. Since March 2025 a heatwave is declared when the daily maximum reaches at least 35 °C and the daily mean at least 29 °C for three days in a row; a taskforce of 37 public agencies then opens air-conditioned cooling spaces in community centres. In March 2026 a Heat Resilience Policy Office was set up to coordinate action across agencies, oversee research and draft a heat resilience action plan.

Sources: [Ministry of Sustainability and the Environment, 2023](https://mse.gov.sg/latest-news/press-release-heat-stress-advisory) · [Malay Mail: Singapore’s heatwave response plan, 2025](https://www.malaymail.com/news/singapore/2025/03/20/singapore-rolls-out-cooling-measures-as-heatwave-response-plan-unveiled/170163) · [Ministry of Sustainability and the Environment: Committee of Supply speech, 2026](https://www.mse.gov.sg/latest-news/speech-by-sms-janil-puthucheary-committee-of-supply-2026/)

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Singapore

### [The sleepless-nights loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#sleep)

Singapore has averaged 76 warm nights a year over four decades; by 2100 they could come on most nights. Source: [National Environment Agency, Singapore, 2024](https://www.nea.gov.sg/media/news/news/index/latest-climate-projections-for-singapore-show-intensifying-urban-heat-and-more-wet-dry-extremes)

Hot nights wreck sleep. Poor sleep frays mental health, and stress makes sleep harder still. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-sleep).

### [The cooling trap](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#cooling)

The commercial centre has measured up to about 7 °C warmer than the outskirts on calm, clear nights. Source: [Cooling Singapore, citing Chow and Roth, 2006](https://www.thegpsc.org/sites/gpsc/files/cooling_singapore_-_digital_urban_climate_twin.pdf)

Air conditioning heats the street, and a hotter city needs even more air conditioning. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-cooling).

### [The dying-green loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#deadgreen)

Among the cities where the most urban tree species already face temperatures beyond their range. Source: [Esperon-Rodriguez et al., Nature Climate Change, 2022](https://www.vibrantcitieslab.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/climate-change-increases-global-risk-to-urban-forests.pdf)

Heat and drought kill city trees. Without their shade the streets get hotter, and hotter streets kill more trees. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-deadgreen).

### [The invisible-deaths loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#invisible)

Workplace monitors found more heat stress than nearby weather stations reported. Source: [NUS Project HeatSafe, 2024](https://medicine.nus.edu.sg/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/PRESS-RELEASE_Project-HeatSafe-18MAR2024-FOR-IMMEDIATE-RELEASE.pdf)

Heat deaths go uncounted, so heat protection goes unfunded, so more people die uncounted. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-invisible).

## Already at work here

- [National Environment Agency, Singapore](https://www.nea.gov.sg/media/news/news/index/new-heat-stress-advisory-launched-to-guide-public-on-minimising-risk-of-heat-related-illnesses): Issues public heat stress advisories through myENV and weather services, so people can adjust outdoor activity as heat rises.
- [Ministry of Manpower, Singapore](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/press-releases/2023/1024-enhanced-measures-to-reduce-heat-stress-for-outdoor-workers): Requires hourly wet-bulb globe temperature (WBGT) checks on outdoor worksites, shaded rest breaks when heat stress is high, and time for new workers to acclimatise, and enforces the rules.
- [Ministry of Sustainability and the Environment, Singapore](https://www.mse.gov.sg/latest-news/speech-by-sms-janil-puthucheary-committee-of-supply-2026/): Coordinates heat resilience and sustainable cooling policy. In March 2026 it set up a Heat Resilience Policy Office to coordinate action across agencies, oversee research and draft a heat resilience action plan, and announced a S$40 million ‘Adapting to Heat Impacts’ funding initiative for research on heat impacts and community heat resilience, within its RIE2030 Heat Resilience R&D Programme.
- [Global Heat Health Information Network, Southeast Asia Hub](https://heathealth.info/southeastasia/): Based in Singapore, shares heat-health evidence and practice across the region; Philanthropy Asia Alliance supports its work in countries including Indonesia and Thailand.
- [Singapore Management University, Urban Institute](https://cityperspectives.smu.edu.sg/wcs-2026/pages/research/cooling-singapore-solutions-for-a-heat-resilient-city.html): Studies urban heat vulnerability and cooling through the Cooling Singapore collaboration, linking neighbourhood conditions to climate-sensitive planning.
- [National Research Foundation, Singapore](https://medicine.nus.edu.sg/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/PRESS-RELEASE_Project-HeatSafe-18MAR2024-FOR-IMMEDIATE-RELEASE.pdf): Funds Project HeatSafe, which studies heat, health and productivity among workers in Singapore and partner countries including Vietnam and Cambodia, and Cooling Singapore.
- [Monetary Authority of Singapore](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2023/mas-launches-worlds-first-multi-sector-transition-taxonomy): Its 2020 environmental risk guidelines expect banks to assess physical climate risk, including through scenario analysis and stress tests. Its Singapore-Asia Taxonomy (2023) covers emission cuts only, so there are no criteria yet for finance that helps people adapt to heat.
- [C40 Cities](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/bengaluru-joins-global-coalition-to-beat-extreme-heat-3786724): Its Cool Cities Accelerator brings together cities including Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai and Singapore on early warnings, building standards and tree cover.
