## Heat action plan: Heat plan in force

Ahmedabad launched South Asia’s first heat action plan in 2013, after a 2010 heatwave caused an estimated 1,344 excess deaths. An estimated 1,190 deaths a year were later avoided.

Sources: [Hess et al., Journal of Environmental and Public Health, 2018](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6236972/)

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Ahmedabad

### [The overwhelmed-clinic loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#clinic)

The plan prepared hospitals and warned the public before heat peaked. Source: [Hess et al., Journal of Environmental and Public Health, 2018](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6236972/)

Heat illness floods clinics. Overwhelmed clinics treat people later, and more cases become severe. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-clinic).

### [The poverty–heat trap](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#poverty)

Up to three-quarters of India’s workforce depends on heat-exposed work. Source: [World Bank, 2022, reported by Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/climate-change/heatwaves-in-india-could-soon-break-human-survivability-limit-says-world-bank-analysis-86431)

People who can’t afford to stop working fall ill. Illness costs income, and lost income makes stopping even less affordable. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-poverty).

### [The invisible-deaths loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#invisible)

As in most countries, heat deaths are hard to count, which makes the case for prevention harder to see.

Heat deaths go uncounted, so heat protection goes unfunded, so more people die uncounted. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-invisible).

## Already at work here

- [Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation, with IIPH Gandhinagar and NRDC](https://www.nrdc.org/press-releases/new-evidence-shows-pioneering-heat-action-plan-helps-millions-avoid-killer-heat): Launched South Asia’s first heat action plan in 2013: early warnings, public outreach and training for medical staff.
- [All India Disaster Mitigation Institute (AIDMI)](https://aidmi.org/key-resources-on-extreme-heat-cooling/): An Ahmedabad-based institute that helps Indian states, districts and cities turn heat warnings into local action. It supports local heat action planning, surveyed 121 street vendors, auto drivers and construction and domestic workers in Lucknow on how heat hits their work, offers Afat Vimo disaster microinsurance to low-income families, and runs a regular southasiadisasters.net series on extreme heat.
- [IBISA](https://www.icmif.org/news_story/ibisa-launches-a-new-parametric-heat-index-product-in-india-to-protect-women-workers-from-heat-induced-wage-losses/): Launched a parametric heat-index product in Ahmedabad in 2024 that pays women workers when heat cuts their working hours, and Ushma Kavach, which protects dairy farmers against heat-stress losses.
- [C40 Cities](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/bengaluru-joins-global-coalition-to-beat-extreme-heat-3786724): Its Cool Cities Accelerator brings together cities including Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai and Singapore on early warnings, building standards and tree cover.
- [Heat Resilience Action (HERA), formerly Climate Resilience for All](https://heranow.org/resources/as-extreme-heat-threats-grow-can-heat-insurance-offer-protection/): Works on extreme heat with a focus on women and low-income communities. With SEWA and Swiss Re, it combined insurance payouts and cash for lost income after India’s 2024 heatwaves.
