# Hyderabad: who is doing what about heat?

Who is working on heat in the city, what exactly they are doing, what anyone can use, and what is still only planned. Built from public sources, each line checked against its source.

Hyderabad tested cool roofs on 25 homes in 2017, and Telangana turned that into India's first state cool roof policy in 2023, with targets and a building permit rule. Each summer the state heatwave plan and the city corporation add water kiosks, health camps and warnings on junction screens, though the plan is advisory.

### Cool roof pilot on 25 homes (2017)

Cool roofs on 25 homes in one low-income neighbourhood, measured against homes without them.

**Lead**: [Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation](#auth-ghmc)

**With**: [Administrative Staff College of India (ASCI)](#org-asci) (partner); [IIIT Hyderabad](#org-iiith) (partner); [Natural Resources Defense Council (NRDC), India programme](#org-nrdc) (partner)

**Where**: One low-income neighbourhood (not named) (Neighbourhoods)

**People**: Low-income households

**When**: 2017

**Outputs**: 25 roofs

**Evaluation**: Indoor air on average 2°C cooler; 76% of residents in the trial group satisfied (NRDC's account).

**Evidence**: The organisation's own account; latest dated evidence May 2018

### Telangana Cool Roofs Policy 2023-2028

State targets for cool roofs, a rule in building permits, mandatory coatings in government low-cost housing, and delivery to low-income homes through city heat plans.

**Lead**: [Municipal Administration and Urban Development Department, Telangana](#auth-maud)

**With**: [Natural Resources Defense Council (NRDC), India programme](#org-nrdc) (working with the state); [Administrative Staff College of India (ASCI)](#org-asci) (working with the state); [IIIT Hyderabad](#org-iiith) (working with the state)

**Where**: Telangana, 200 sq km of it in Hyderabad (State)

**People**: Building owners, Low-income households

**When**: April 2023 to 2029

**Outputs**: Policy in effect from 1 April 2023; First-year target: 5 sq km of Hyderabad

**Evaluation**: Progress against the targets was not found.

**Evidence**: Own account and independent reporting; latest dated evidence April 2023

### Telangana Heatwave Action Plan 2025

The state plan that sets what departments do once temperatures cross 40°C: treatment in health centres, changed work hours, free drinking water stands.

**Lead**: [Revenue (Disaster Management) Department, Telangana](#auth-tsrev)

**With**: [India Meteorological Department](#auth-imd) (inputs); [UNICEF](#org-unicef) (inputs); [Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation](#auth-ghmc) (acts under the plan in the city)

**Where**: Telangana (State)

**People**: Residents, Outdoor workers

**When**: May 2025

**Outputs**: The plan, released 2 May 2025

**Evaluation**: Not evaluated. The measures are directory, without legal backing.

**Evidence**: Independent reporting; latest dated evidence May 2025

## What is not known

Open questions, stated as gaps in what the sources show, not as failures of any organisation.

- How many square kilometres of cool roofs have been installed against the policy targets.
- Whether a Hyderabad city heat action plan exists separately from the state plan.
- Budgets for the summer measures.
- Telugu and Urdu sources were not searched systematically for this first version.

### Where sources disagree

- Residential threshold of the cool roof mandate: NRDC gives 600 sq. m of plot area; ThePrint gives 600 square yards.
