## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Kathmandu

### [The melting-ice loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#ice)

The Hindu Kush Himalaya’s glaciers lost ice 65% faster in the 2010s, and could lose 30–50% of their volume by 2100. Source: [ICIMOD, 2023](https://lib.icimod.org/record/36322)

Heat melts ice. Darker ground and water absorb more sunlight, and the melting speeds up. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-ice).

### [The medical-debt trap](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#debt)

In five cities in Nepal and Bangladesh, heat cut incomes and pushed some households towards high-interest loans. Source: [American Red Cross, Red Cross Red Crescent Climate Centre, Nepal Red Cross Society and Bangladesh Red Crescent Society, 2026](https://www.climatecentre.org/publications/17312/the-lived-experiences-of-vulnerable-populations-in-the-context-of-extreme-heat-a-comprehensive-narrative-report-based-on-survey-data-from-five-cities-in-nepal-and-bangladesh/)

Treatment costs push families into debt. Debt removes any option to rest, and working through the heat brings more illness. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-debt).

## Already at work here

- [National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority, Nepal](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/reassessing-nepals-heat-wave-response-lessons-from-2024-and-the-way-forward.html): Issued Nepal’s first federal heat plan in 2024.
- [Nepal Red Cross Society](https://www.climatecentre.org/publications/16234/dhangadhi-heat-action-plan-2025/): Published the Dhangadhi Heat Action Plan in 2025 with the Climate Centre, setting out when and where to act before and during heatwave days in a city that passes 40 °C every year.
- [Tewa, Nepal Women’s Fund](https://annualreport.bothends.org/nepalese-women-lead-local-advocacy-on-climate-change/): Makes small grants to grassroots women’s groups in Nepal; one won municipal funding for irrigation after training on climate budgets. Heat-specific funding is not confirmed.
- [Nepal Rastra Bank](https://www.nrb.org.np/): Sets environmental and social risk rules for banks (2018), and since October 2024 a green finance taxonomy that sorts lending into green, amber and red.
- [Practical Action](https://practicalaction.org/news-stories/zurich-climate-resilience-alliance-to-expand-focus-on-destructive-extreme-heat/): Connects heat risk intelligence, local action, practical technology, institutions and finance, following a Sense → Decide → Act → Sustain logic. In Nepal, Dhangadhi’s heat action plan links temperature, humidity and health data, hotspot mapping and community views to thresholds and set municipal responses. In Bangladesh, heat is being built into early warning and livelihood work. A partner in the Zurich Climate Resilience Alliance.
