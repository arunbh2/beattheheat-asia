## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Manila

### [The lost-schooling loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#school)

In April 2024, 5,288 schools in the Philippines moved to remote learning, affecting more than 3.6 million students. Source: [AFP via Malay Mail, April 2024](https://www.malaymail.com/news/world/2024/04/05/thousands-of-philippine-schools-suspend-in-person-classes-due-to-heat-says-education-dept/127511)

Children pulled out of school earn less as adults, and poorer families pull more children out of school. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-school).

### [The cooling trap](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#cooling)

One of the dense, high-rise city cores where air-conditioner waste heat adds to the night. Source: [Salamanca et al., Journal of Geophysical Research, 2014 (summary by Arizona State University)](https://news.asu.edu/content/excess-heat-air-conditioners-causes-higher-nighttime-temperatures)

Air conditioning heats the street, and a hotter city needs even more air conditioning. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-cooling).

### [The farm-debt loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#farmdebt)

Rice yields in the Philippines fell about 10% for each 1 °C rise in growing-season night temperature. Source: [Peng et al., PNAS, 2004](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0403720101)

A failed harvest pushes farmers into debt. Debt leaves less for seed, water and fertiliser, so the next harvest fails more easily. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-farmdebt).

## Already at work here

- [PAGASA, Philippines](https://www.gmanetwork.com/regionaltv/news/115185/pagasa-urges-public-to-brace-for-more-intense-heat/story/): Issues heat index warnings with danger levels that local governments use to decide when to suspend classes.
- [Department of Education, Philippines](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1940232/deped-all-set-for-june-school-opening-in-25): Lets school heads suspend in-person classes when the heat index is dangerous, and moved the school year back to a June start to keep classes out of the hottest months.
