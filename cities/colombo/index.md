## Heat action plan: Parts in place, no full plan found

Colombo Municipal Council, with the Department of Meteorology and SLYCAN Trust, has worked on guidelines for a gender-sensitive heat action plan. The Department of Meteorology issues heat index advisories. No adopted city heat plan found yet.

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Colombo

### [The cooling trap](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#cooling)

A University of Colombo study found a pronounced urban heat island, as green space gives way to concrete and asphalt. Source: [University of Colombo: urban heat island and green space in Colombo District](https://archive.cmb.ac.lk/handle/70130/8937)

Air conditioning heats the street, and a hotter city needs even more air conditioning. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-cooling).

### [The poverty–heat trap](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#poverty)

Heat advisories ask people to work in the shade; market and fish workers who sell in the open often cannot. Source: [News 1st, 2024](https://english.newsfirst.lk/2024/03/04/heat-advisory-issued-by-sri-lanka-s-met-dept)

People who can’t afford to stop working fall ill. Illness costs income, and lost income makes stopping even less affordable. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-poverty).

## Already at work here

- [Asia-Pacific Network for Global Change Research (APN)](https://www.apn-gcr.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CBA2021-07SY-Magotra-Final-Report-_APN.docx-1.pdf): Funds regional research and capacity building, including a US$40,000 grant for gender-sensitive heat action policies in South Asia and newer work on urban heat in Southeast Asian cities.
- [World Meteorological Organization](https://wmo.int/node/38332): With WHO, runs the South Asia Climate–Health Desk with India’s meteorological agencies, turning weather and climate information into health protection.
- [UN ESCAP](https://unescap.org/our-work/environment-development/cities-for-a-sustainable-future/addressing-urban-extreme-heat): Helps fast-growing cities in South and Southeast Asia plan for extreme urban heat, working with Iloilo City and Cox’s Bazar.
