# Chennai: who is doing what about heat?

Who is working on heat in the city, what exactly they are doing, what anyone can use, and what is still only planned. Built from public sources, each line checked against its source.

In Chennai the visible heat work is led from the state: Tamil Nadu has declared heat a disaster, and its green climate company is coating school roofs with UNEP. The city corporation has trained school staff, signed an agreement with CEEW to plan ward-level heat projects, and is folding heat mapping by UNEP and CEPT University into its master plan.

### Cool roofs on 55 schools

Reflective roof coatings on schools in areas vulnerable to high temperatures, with training for teachers and headmasters.

**Lead**: [Greater Chennai Corporation](#auth-gcc)

**With**: [TN Green Climate Company](#auth-tngcc) (implementing agency); [UNEP and its Cool Coalition](#org-unep) (partner)

**Where**: 55 schools (49 Corporation, six government) (Named sites)

**People**: Schoolchildren, Teachers

**When**: Start not stated

**Outputs**: Five government schools completed (June 2026); Teachers and headmasters trained

**Evaluation**: Earlier pilots were reported to lower classroom temperatures; no evaluation was read for this profile.

**Evidence**: Independent reporting; latest dated evidence June 2026

### Who has public authority to act

Public bodies with responsibilities that touch heat in the city, under their current names. Older names are kept for older records.
