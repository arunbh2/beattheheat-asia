# Kolkata: who is doing what about heat?

Who is working on heat in the city, what exactly they are doing, what anyone can use, and what is still only planned. Built from public sources, each line checked against its source.

For Kolkata, the sources reviewed show more analysis than action: research on where heat is most severe and on lost water bodies and greenery, a state heat wave plan, and repeated calls for a city plan. No Kolkata Municipal Corporation heat action plan was found.

## What is not known

Open questions, stated as gaps in what the sources show, not as failures of any organisation.

- Whether the Kolkata Municipal Corporation has a heat action plan; none was found.
- Who in the city runs water points, shelters or health measures in a heat wave; no city-specific source was read.
- Worker, community and health organisations working on heat in Kolkata; none was found in the sources reviewed, which says more about the search than the city.
- Bengali-language sources were not searched systematically for this first version.
