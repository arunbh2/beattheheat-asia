## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Mumbai

### [The sleepless-nights loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#sleep)

Mumbai gained 15 very warm nights a summer over the last decade. Source: [Council on Energy, Environment and Water (CEEW), 2025](https://www.ceew.in/sites/default/files/ceew-how-extreme-heat-is-impacting-india-final-web-file.pdf)

Hot nights wreck sleep. Poor sleep frays mental health, and stress makes sleep harder still. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-sleep).

### [The energy-poverty trap](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#energypoverty)

Tin-roofed informal settlements trap heat for people who can’t afford to cool.

People who can’t afford to cool fall ill. Illness brings debt, and debt puts cooling even further out of reach. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-energypoverty).

### [The invisible-deaths loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#invisible)

Death certificates usually record the immediate cause, so heat as the trigger goes unrecorded. Source: [Down To Earth, 2024](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/climate-change/lost-in-the-heat-the-critical-miscalculation-in-indias-heatwave-mortality-data)

Heat deaths go uncounted, so heat protection goes unfunded, so more people die uncounted. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-invisible).

## Already at work here

- [WRI India](https://india.mongabay.com/2025/04/safeguarding-indian-cities-from-heat-in-conversation-with-sudeshna-chatterjee-interview/): Works on heat mitigation planning with Indian cities, including greening and cooling pilots in Kochi and Mumbai and the Bengaluru Heat Collective.
- [C40 Cities](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/bengaluru-joins-global-coalition-to-beat-extreme-heat-3786724): Its Cool Cities Accelerator brings together cities including Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai and Singapore on early warnings, building standards and tree cover.
- [HDFC Life (Swabhimaan CSR)](https://www.hdfclife.com/hdfc-careers/csr.html): Funds city forests and waterbody revival in Sikkim, Maharashtra and Gujarat alongside livelihoods and health work. Heat-specific funding is not confirmed.
- [ICLEI South Asia](https://southasia.iclei.org/news/extreme-heat-new-knowledge-products-highlight-need-to-build-resilience-adaptation/): Supports cities with heat risk assessments, heat action plans, urban greening and preparedness tools in India, Nepal and Bangladesh.
