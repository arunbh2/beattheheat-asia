## Heat action plan: Heat plan in force

For the 2026 hot season Bangkok opened 304 cooling centres in schools, health centres and district offices. The city’s own Urban Heat Management Action Plan for 2026 has 22 indicators and a committee chaired by a deputy governor; its targets include cooling rooms in 80% of schools and public health service centres, at least two cooling points in every district, and home visits to at least 5,000 vulnerable households a year during heat alerts. A 2026 financing strategy with UNDP costs the plan at about THB 146 million to set up and THB 10.8 million a year to run, mostly from existing city budgets, and notes that running costs are not always covered.

Sources: [Government of Thailand, 2026](https://thailand.go.th/issue-focus-detail/bangkok-launches-304-cooling-centers-to-combat-extreme-heat) · [World Bank, Shaping a Cooler Bangkok, 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/thailand/publication/shaping-a-cooler-bangkok-tackling-urban-heat-for-a-more-livable-city) · [UNDP and Bangkok Metropolitan Administration: Bangkok's financing strategy for tackling urban heat, 2026](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-10/en-undp-bma_financial_strategy_on_urban_heat.pdf)

## Vicious cycles the evidence ties to Bangkok

### [The poverty–heat trap](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#poverty)

Nearly 80% of informal workers surveyed cut their hours in the heat; 75% of home-based workers lost a day’s work a month to heat illness. Source: [WIEGO and HomeNet Thailand, 2025](https://www.wiego.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/WIEGO-Policy-Brief-33-Climate-Change-and-Heat-Stress.pdf)

People who can’t afford to stop working fall ill. Illness costs income, and lost income makes stopping even less affordable. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-poverty).

### [The reactive-spending loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#reactive)

Heat deaths, about 593 a year, cost up to 0.7% of the city’s economy: more than its 2020 public health budget. Source: [Park and Rubinyi, World Bank working paper, 2026](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/d8ba6f53-d778-47d2-b4a6-37dff7db2466/full)

Stretched budgets skimp on prevention. Emergencies multiply, and costly emergency response stretches budgets further. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-reactive).

### [The sleepless-nights loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#sleep)

One of the tropical cities whose nights stay hot.

Hot nights wreck sleep. Poor sleep frays mental health, and stress makes sleep harder still. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-sleep).

### [The green-displacement loop](https://beattheheat.asia/loops/#greendisplace)

Fieldwork is tracing how green urban renewal affects the city’s lower-income residents. Source: [Endo, The Developing Economies, 2026](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/deve.70013)

Green space follows wealth. When greening reaches poorer districts without protection, rents rise, residents are pushed into hotter housing, and the gap grows. [Follow it on the map](https://beattheheat.asia/#loop-greendisplace).

## Already at work here

- [Bangkok Metropolitan Administration](https://www.bangkok.go.th): Runs heat warnings and public cooling spaces. Its Urban Heat Management Action Plan for 2026 has 22 indicators and a committee chaired by a deputy governor, and a 2026 financing strategy with UNDP sets out how to pay for it.
- [HomeNet Thailand](https://www.wiego.org/research-library-publications/climate-change-and-heat-stress-impacts-bangkok-thailand/): With WIEGO, surveyed more than 1,000 informal workers in Bangkok on heat: nearly 80% had cut their hours. A trusted link between workers and authorities.
- [Bank of Thailand](https://www.bot.or.th/en/financial-innovation/sustainable-finance/green/FIPCS-internalizing_environmental_and_climate_change.html): A 2023 policy statement asks banks to build environmental and climate risk into governance, strategy, risk management and disclosure. It has worked with the World Bank on assessing physical climate risk in Thai banks’ loan books.
- [United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)](https://www.undp.org/thailand/publications/bangkok-financing-strategy-tackling-urban-heat): Helps governments connect heat resilience with health systems, urban planning and finance. Its work includes a financing strategy for tackling urban heat in Bangkok.
- [Asian Development Bank](https://www.preventionweb.net/publication/beating-heat-investing-pro-poor-solutions-urban-resilience): Supports heat resilience in Asian cities, including technical assistance on heat stress among women and girls in Bangladesh, Cambodia, Pakistan, Sri Lanka and Tajikistan. Its 2026 report on the heat crisis in Thailand and Southeast Asia calls for mobilising public and private finance, and proposes a heat action plan for Thailand.
