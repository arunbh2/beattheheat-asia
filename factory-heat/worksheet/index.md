## 3. Can people use the protection?

- People can drink water without asking or losing pay
- Water points are close to every line and do not run dry
- Toilets are close, clean, safe and used without permission
- Breaks are paid and long enough to reach the cool rest space
- Piece-rate, target or attendance pay does not punish breaks or heat-related absence
- Pregnant and breastfeeding workers have individual arrangements, with pay protected
- New and returning workers build up time in heat
- Symptoms can be reported confidentially, without penalty
- Someone trained in first aid for heat is on every shift
