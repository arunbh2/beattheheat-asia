# Cite and reuse

Everything on Common Shade is free to use, share and adapt under CC BY 4.0, with credit. Here is how to cite it, and where to find the data.

## How to cite

Every page carries a ready citation at its foot. For the site as a whole:

Common Shade. *Extreme heat in South and Southeast Asia: a shared map of consequences, cycles and protection.* beattheheat.asia. Updated November 2026. CC BY 4.0.

When you cite a figure, please cite its original source too: each one is linked where it appears, and listed in [Sources and references](https://beattheheat.asia/sources/).

## The data

The content behind the map is open, as plain JSON files, one for each part. They are updated as the knowledge base grows.

- [The 90 consequences of heatdata/core/CONSEQUENCES.json](https://beattheheat.asia/data/core/CONSEQUENCES.json)
- [Every link between themdata/core/LINKS.json](https://beattheheat.asia/data/core/LINKS.json)
